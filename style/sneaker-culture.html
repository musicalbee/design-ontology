Sneaker culture

fashion · 1985–present · also: Signature basketball shoe, Hip-hop sneaker, Collector sneaker

The sneaker as a badge. It is a leather high-top in team colours with a bold brand mark, an air-cushioned sole and a limited release. Players, rappers and collectors wear it.

Core thesis

In this style the shoe is a signal: colour, release and story matter more than function, and an owner shows taste by what is rare.

Design principles

  • Cut the upper in leather panels of 3 or more colours, in a blocked pattern.
  • Show one large mark on the side, the tongue or the heel.
  • Raise the sole to 3 to 4 cm, with a visible cushion unit in the heel.
  • Use a high-top shaft to the ankle for a basketball shoe, or a low-top for daily wear.
  • Release a colourway in a limited run, and name each colourway.
  • Keep the white midsole clean, and lace the shoe on purpose, loose or tight.
  • Collect the pair in its box, and wear it with a matched jersey or a tracksuit.

Anti-patterns

  • A plain, unmarked shoe
  • A single dull colour
  • A worn and dirty pair
  • A dress leather shoe
  • A thin canvas sole

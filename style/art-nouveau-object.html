Art Nouveau object

industrial · 1893–1914 · also: Jugendstil object, Modern Style object, Liberty-style object

Lamps, glass, furniture and fixtures drawn from the line of a plant stem: cast bronze, art glass, carved fruit wood and porcelain, with one long curve that joins structure and ornament.

Core thesis

An object grows like a plant: its structure and its ornament are one curved line, made by a craftsman from the best material.

Design principles

  • Draw each support as a plant stem that is thin at the root of the curve and thick where the load meets it.
  • Join structure and ornament in one S-shaped line; a leg, a handle and a leaf are one stroke.
  • Use coloured art glass in two to four layers for a shade or a vase, and let light pass through it.
  • Cast the metal parts in bronze or brass and give them a brown or green patina.
  • Carve fruit wood with a curved edge; leave 3 to 5 mm of soft radius on every corner.
  • Take the motif from nature: the iris, the orchid, the water lily, the dragonfly and the thistle.
  • Make each piece a unit with its room: the same curve runs on the lamp, the tap, the chair and the handle.

Anti-patterns

  • Straight rods and sharp right angles
  • Machine-perfect chrome with no hand mark
  • Revival ornament, such as acanthus leaves or Gothic crockets
  • Flat colour with no depth in the glass
  • Plastic and laminate surfaces
  • Strict symmetry with no curve

Clickbait Thumbnail

internet · 2012–now · also: YouTube thumbnail, Reaction face thumbnail, MrBeast-style thumbnail

A small still that must win a click at 168px wide. It has a huge face with an open mouth, a red arrow or circle, bright colours and 1 to 3 words in a thick outline.

Core thesis

The still has one second to ask a question: show one clear shock, one object and 3 words, and make it readable at the size of a thumb.

Design principles

  • Make the image 1280x720 and test it at 168px wide before you ship it.
  • Fill 40% to 60% of the frame with one face at a strong expression, cut out with a 6px white edge.
  • Use at most 3 words in a thick display face of 120px to 200px with a 8px black outline.
  • Draw one arrow or circle in red (#FF0000) or yellow (#FFE500) to point at the key object.
  • Push saturation by 20% to 40% and add a 12% vignette to the edge.
  • Split the frame into 2 halves (before and after, or A and B) with a diagonal line of 8px.
  • Keep all text and faces 48px away from the lower-right corner, where the duration badge sits.

Anti-patterns

  • Calm, subtle colour and soft grading
  • Long titles of 6 words or more
  • Small faces and wide, empty scenes
  • Thin or serif type
  • Neutral expressions
  • Many small objects with no single focus

Palette

  • Shock Blue #0A57FF background
  • Card Black #111111 surface
  • Outline White #FFFFFF text
  • Arrow Red #FF1A1A primary
  • Hook Yellow #FFE500 accent
  • Skin Warm #F2B48C highlight

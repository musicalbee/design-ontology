Green-Screen Short

internet · 2020–now · also: Green screen video, TikTok green screen, Chroma-key explainer

A person stands in front of a screenshot, a map or a photo that fills the whole vertical frame, and talks about it. The creator cuts out the background with the chroma-key tool of a short-video app.

Core thesis

Put the speaker in front of the evidence: the image is the set, the creator is the commentator.

Design principles

  • Fill the 1080 x 1920 px frame with the background image; show the speaker from the waist up at 40% to 55% of the height.
  • Place the speaker at the left or the right third; keep the key area of the image free.
  • Cut out the speaker with a clean mask with a 1 px to 2 px feather.
  • Add a 3 px white stroke around the speaker when the background is busy.
  • Zoom the image in by 110% to 130% over the clip to guide the eye.
  • Mark a key detail with a 6 px red circle or an arrow at 100% opacity.
  • Caption the speech in 40 px words at the bottom third.

Anti-patterns

  • A speaker with a visible green fringe
  • A background with no link to the talk
  • Tiny screenshot text that nobody can read
  • Two speakers who hide the image
  • Stock-photo backdrops

Palette

  • Backdrop Dark #16181D background
  • Marker Red #E5322D primary
  • Key Green #00B140 accent
  • White #FFFFFF text
  • Mist #B6BBC4 muted text
  • Panel #242730 surface

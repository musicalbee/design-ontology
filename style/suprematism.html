Suprematism

painting · 1915–1935 · also: Malevich's Suprematism, Pure feeling

Flat geometric shapes, such as a square, a bar, a circle and a cross, that float on a white field. The picture shows no object: it shows pure feeling and the sense of space.

Core thesis

Art should free itself from the object: pure shapes in a limitless white field give the feeling of form and of space and nothing else.

Design principles

  • Build the picture from 1–12 flat geometric shapes: a square, a rectangle, a circle, a cross, a bar.
  • Float the shapes on a white or pale field, and leave 50–70% of the picture empty.
  • Tilt the shapes at 10–45° from the vertical, so that they rise, fall or drift.
  • Limit the colour to 2–4 flat hues and black, with each shape in one flat colour.
  • Paint the shapes with a hard, clean edge and no brush texture in the colour.
  • Hold the weight in balance by size and position, not by symmetry.
  • Show no object, no horizon, no light source and no shadow.

Anti-patterns

  • A recognisable object, figure or landscape
  • Depth, perspective, shadow and modelling
  • Visible, expressive brush strokes
  • A symmetric, centred, formal layout
  • Many small, busy, decorative shapes
  • A rich, blended, naturalistic colour

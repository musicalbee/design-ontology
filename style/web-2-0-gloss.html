Web 2.0 Gloss

internet · 2004–2010 · also: Web 2.0 style, Glossy buttons, Beta badge style

Glossy rounded buttons, vertical gradients, reflections under logos, big friendly type, star badges and a 'beta' tag on a clean white page. Social and tool start-ups used it after 2004.

Core thesis

A site is a friendly tool: make every control look glossy, round and easy to press.

Design principles

  • Fill buttons with a vertical 2-stop gradient, for example #5B9BD5 to #2F6DB5, with a white 1px inner highlight at the top.
  • Set a gloss on the top half: a white overlay at 35% to 50% opacity that ends at 50% of the button height.
  • Round corners at 6px to 12px on buttons and cards, and use pill shapes for tags.
  • Place a reflection under the logo: a flipped copy at 30% opacity that fades to 0% over 40px.
  • Set big, rounded headlines at 36px to 48px in a friendly sans, and 14px body text on a white ground.
  • Add a star or burst badge of 60px to 90px with 'beta', 'new' or 'free' in bold white on orange (#FF8A00).
  • Use a soft drop shadow of 0 2px 4px at 20% black under cards and a 1px light grey line (#D0D7DE).

Anti-patterns

  • Flat colour with no gradient
  • Sharp 0px corners and hard bevels
  • Dark moody grounds
  • Small, dense text in tables
  • Thin light type with no emphasis
  • Cluttered tiled backgrounds

Palette

  • White #FFFFFF background
  • Sky #E8F1FA section ground
  • Web Blue #2F6DB5 primary
  • Fresh Green #7AB800 success
  • Badge Orange #FF8A00 accent
  • Line Grey #D0D7DE border
  • Navy Ink #1F2D3D text
  • Gloss White #FFFFFF highlight

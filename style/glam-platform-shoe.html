Glam platform shoe

fashion · 1970–1979 · also: Platform boot, Glitter-rock platform, Seventies platform

The raised shoe of glam rock and disco. It has a platform sole of 5 to 10 cm, a thick heel, and a loud upper in patent, glitter, suede or snake print.

Core thesis

A glam shoe adds height and noise: a thick platform and a bright upper make the wearer taller, louder and not quite real.

Design principles

  • Raise the sole 5 to 10 cm at the toe, with a heel 2 to 4 cm higher than the platform.
  • Cut the platform in layers of cork, wood or foam, and cover them in leather or cloth.
  • Use a rounded or a squared toe, and a wide, solid heel.
  • Cover the upper in a loud material: patent, glitter, mirror, snake print or metallic.
  • Add stars, stripes or a stacked sole in two or three colours.
  • Close the shoe with a thick ankle strap, a buckle, or a high lace shaft.
  • Match the platform edge to the upper in colour, or contrast it on purpose.

Anti-patterns

  • A thin, tall stiletto
  • A quiet, plain dress shoe
  • A flat, low sole
  • A fine, small pattern
  • A single muted colour and a thin heel

Get Ready With Me

internet · 2019–now · also: GRWM, Get ready with me video, Morning routine clip

A creator talks to a close phone camera while she or he does make-up or picks an outfit, with soft light, a mirror, jump cuts and a line of captions. The form grew from YouTube make-up tutorials of the 2000s.

Core thesis

Share a real routine in real time: the talk matters more than the result.

Design principles

  • Frame the face and shoulders at 55% to 70% of the frame height, with eyes at 38% from the top.
  • Light with a ring light or a window at 5600 K to 6500 K.
  • Use jump cuts at 1 cut every 2 s to 5 s; remove all pauses.
  • Set a hook line in the first 1.5 s, for example the event or the place.
  • Caption the speech in 40 px words, 2 lines at most, at 65% of the frame height.
  • Show product names as 28 px text tags with a 12 px radius, placed near the product.
  • Use a warm, soft grade: lift shadows by 10% and keep skin tones at 2 to 3 stops of exposure.

Anti-patterns

  • Studio lighting with hard shadows
  • Scripted, stiff delivery
  • Wide shots of an empty room
  • Heavy filters that change the skin
  • Long intros with a logo

Palette

  • Cream #FBF3EA background
  • Blush #F2B8B0 primary
  • Rose Clay #C9776F accent
  • Espresso #3B2A26 text
  • Taupe #8A746C muted text
  • Linen #EFE0D2 surface

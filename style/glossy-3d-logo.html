Gradient 3D and glass

logo · 2001–2012 · also: Web 2.0 logo, Glossy logo, Aqua logo, 3D logo

A mark lit like an object: top highlights, gradients from light to deep colour, a glass bevel, a reflection under it and a soft drop shadow. The screen was now the main medium, and the mark imitated a polished button or a globe.

Core thesis

On a screen a mark can be a lit object: gloss, depth and reflection make it feel new, touchable and alive.

Design principles

  • Light the mark from the top: a gradient from 20–30% lighter at the top to the base colour at 60% of the height.
  • Add a gloss highlight: a white shape at 40–70% opacity over the top 40–50% of the mark.
  • Model the volume with a bevel or a sphere: shading from the base colour to 30–40% darker at the edge.
  • Add a floor reflection: a mirrored copy at 20–30% opacity, faded over 30–40% of the mark height.
  • Use 2–4 hues of high saturation; a gradient counts as its two end colours.
  • Keep the silhouette simple, a globe, a bubble, a rounded square, so the lighting reads at 32 px.

Anti-patterns

  • A flat fill with no light
  • Fine line detail under 3% of the mark height
  • Engraved hatching
  • Muted, low-saturation colour
  • A mark that has no one-colour version in the manual

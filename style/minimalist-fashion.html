Minimalist fashion

fashion · 1990–2000 · also: 1990s minimalism, Clean line fashion, Pared-back dress

The plain, quiet dress of the 1990s. It has clean lines, fine cloth in grey, black and white, no logo and no trim, and a cut that does all the work.

Core thesis

After the show of the 1980s, dress is plain: a perfect cut in fine cloth needs no ornament.

Design principles

  • Cut each piece with as few seams as the body allows.
  • Remove each trim, logo, pocket flap and extra button that has no use.
  • Choose fine, dense cloth in wool, silk crepe, poplin and cashmere.
  • Keep the palette to black, white, grey, navy and camel.
  • Cut a straight, long line: a slim trouser, a long jacket and a column dress.
  • Hide the seam and the facing, and finish the inside as clean as the outside.
  • Keep the shoulder natural, with no pad.

Anti-patterns

  • Wide padded shoulders
  • A logo or a monogram
  • Ruffles, lace and sequins
  • Bold print and strong colour
  • Visible hardware and trim
  • A fussy, layered outfit

Usenet Thread

internet · c. 1980–1999 · also: Newsgroup thread, Netnews, Threaded newsreader

A thread of plain-text posts in a newsgroup, read in a terminal newsreader: a tree of subject lines, quoted lines that start with >, and a signature under each post.

Core thesis

A discussion is a tree of plain-text replies, and each reply quotes the part that it answers.

Design principles

  • Set all text in one monospace face at one size, 80 characters to a line.
  • Show the thread as a tree of subject lines, with 2 spaces of indent for each reply level.
  • Start each quoted line with '> ', and add one more '>' for each older level.
  • Put a header block on each post: From, Newsgroups, Subject and Date, one field to a line.
  • End each post with a signature of at most 4 lines after a line of '-- '.
  • Mark the current line in the thread list with reverse video: light text on a dark bar.

Anti-patterns

  • Proportional type and type sizes for hierarchy
  • Images, avatars and icons in the post body
  • Colour for meaning other than the cursor bar
  • Quotes without the > mark
  • Rounded cards and shadows

Palette

  • Terminal Black #101010 background
  • Panel Grey #1C1C1C panel
  • Phosphor White #D8D8D0 text
  • Quote Grey #8A8A80 muted
  • Amber #FFB000 highlight
  • Cursor Green #33FF66 accent

Vaporwave

internet · 2012–2016 · also: Vapourwave, Aesthetic, A E S T H E T I C

Pink and cyan gradients, Greek busts, palm trees, Japanese text, 1990s desktop windows and a sunset grid. It came from online music scenes around 2011 and looks at 1980s and 1990s consumer dreams with irony.

Core thesis

Nostalgia for a future that did not happen: use the dead gloss of 1990s corporate culture to question it.

Design principles

  • Set a gradient from magenta (#FF71CE) through violet to cyan (#01CDFE) at 135° across the hero.
  • Draw a perspective grid floor of 2px lines in cyan or pink with a horizon at 55% to 65% of the viewport height.
  • Place a classical statue, a palm or a sun disc with horizontal cut-lines (6 to 8 bars) as the focal image.
  • Show content in retro desktop windows: 2px bevelled border, a 24px title bar and 3 square buttons.
  • Set headlines in wide-tracked capitals (+0.3em) and add a line of Japanese katakana at 50% of the size.
  • Use a dark purple ground (#2A1B5C) for text areas and keep body text at 16px with contrast of 7:1 or more.
  • Add a scanline overlay of 2px stripes at 6% opacity and a glow of 8px to 16px on neon text.

Anti-patterns

  • Neutral flat palettes with no neon
  • Clean corporate sans-serif body text
  • Photographs of real, modern people in daily use
  • Subtle, serious tone with no irony
  • Warm earth tones
  • Realistic dark mode with grey surfaces
  • Strict minimal layouts

Palette

  • Night Violet #2A1B5C background
  • Neon Pink #FF71CE primary
  • Cyan #01CDFE accent
  • Mint #B967FF second accent
  • Sun Yellow #FFFB96 highlight
  • Window Grey #C0C0D8 window surface

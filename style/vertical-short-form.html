Vertical short-form

film · 2016–today · also: TikTok style, Reels style, Shorts style, 9:16 video

A full-screen 9:16 clip of 15 to 45 s, shot on a phone and cut every 1.5 to 3 s. A hook in the first second, burned-in captions and a sound that can loop. The feed decides each rule.

Core thesis

A viewer swipes in 1 to 2 s, so every second must earn the next: the hook, the cut and the caption are the whole design.

Design principles

  • Frame in 9:16 at 1080 × 1920 px, 30 fps (24 or 60 allowed), 180° shutter, and fill the screen edge to edge.
  • Put the hook in the first 1 to 2 s: a face, a movement or a text line; the first cut comes by 3 s.
  • Cut or change the picture every 1.5 to 3 s: new angle, new scale, a punch-in of 10% to 20% or a new caption.
  • Keep faces and captions inside the safe zone: leave the bottom 20% and the top 7% of the height and the right 12% of the width clear for the interface.
  • Burn in captions of 1 to 6 words for each beat, at 48 px or larger on a 1080 px wide frame, 2 lines at most.
  • Shoot the main camera at 24mm (35mm format) at 0.3 to 1.5 m from the subject, with a ring light or a window at 5600K.
  • End so the clip loops: match the last frame to the first, and keep the run time at 15 to 45 s.
  • Mix to −14 LUFS integrated with the voice over a trending or looped sound.

Anti-patterns

  • A horizontal 16:9 clip with black bars above and below
  • A slow intro or a logo before the hook
  • Captions under the platform interface or in the safe-zone margin
  • A wide establishing shot held for more than 3 s
  • A cinematic letterbox with a 2.39:1 crop
  • A voice with no captions on a feed that plays muted
  • A clip longer than 60 s with no change of angle

Critical Regionalism

architecture · 1981–present · also: Regionalist modernism, Place-based modernism

Modern form rooted in a place. Utzon, Moneo, Bawa, Piano and Zumthor keep the rigour of modernism but take their light, climate, materials and craft from the site, and they refuse both the universal box and the historical joke.

Core thesis

A building should use modern means to answer a specific place: its climate, its materials and its craft give the form, and universal style and nostalgia are both refused.

Design principles

  • Read the site first: set the orientation, the openings and the roof by the sun, the wind and the rain of that place.
  • Use materials from within 100 to 300 km, and let their texture and joints be touched and seen.
  • Build a tectonic order: express the joint, the span and the load in details of 20 to 60mm.
  • Control views: frame the landscape with 2 to 4 deliberate openings, and keep the rest of the wall solid.
  • Borrow from local types, such as the courtyard, the veranda or the thick wall, and recast them in modern construction.
  • Move through the building on a sequence of light, shade and touch, and make each room slow and sensory.

Anti-patterns

  • Imported glass-box facades with no climate response
  • Historical pastiche and applied local symbols
  • Faux-vernacular theme-park details
  • Identical plans repeated in every city
  • Sealed air-conditioned volumes with no shade
  • Decorative cladding that hides the real structure

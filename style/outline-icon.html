Outline icon

icon · 2013–present · also: Line icon, Stroke icon, Feather-style icon

Icons drawn as one even stroke on a 24 px grid, with round caps and joins and no fill. The set reads like a light typeface: the same line weight, the same corner radius and the same live area for every glyph.

Core thesis

An icon set is a typeface of objects: one stroke, one grid and one corner radius make hundreds of glyphs read as one family.

Design principles

  • Draw on a 24 × 24 px grid with a live area of 20 × 20 px and 2 px of padding on each side.
  • Use one stroke width for the whole set: 2 px at 24 px (1.5 px for a light set).
  • Set round caps and round joins on every path.
  • Give rectangles a corner radius of 2 px; circles and arcs keep the same stroke.
  • Keep a gap of at least 2 px between two parallel strokes.
  • Align strokes to the pixel grid so that a 2 px line covers whole pixels at 1× and 2×.

Anti-patterns

  • Mixed stroke widths in one set
  • Solid fills inside the glyph
  • Square caps next to round caps
  • Detail closer than 2 px at 24 px
  • Colour inside a UI glyph

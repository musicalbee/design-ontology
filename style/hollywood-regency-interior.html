Hollywood Regency

interior · 1935–1965; revival from 2005 · also: Hollywood Regency, Regency moderne, Hollywood glamour

The decorator's room of the film stars. Regency and neoclassical forms meet lacquer colour, bold stripes and mirror; a symmetric plan around a centre piece, white plaster and gilt make each room ready for a camera.

Core thesis

A room is a stage set: bold colour, gloss and symmetry make the people in it look their best.

Design principles

  • Plan the room around one centre piece, such as a round table or an ottoman, with seats in pairs about it.
  • Use one saturated colour boldly, often lacquered, against white or black.
  • Add mirror and gilt for sparkle: mirrored furniture, gilt frames and brass fittings.
  • Use bold graphic pattern on one surface: wide stripes, trellis or a black and white floor.
  • Mix Regency and neoclassical forms, such as klismos chairs and pagodas, with modern comfort.
  • Make the room photogenic: warm light that flatters faces and a clear view of each pair.

Anti-patterns

  • Matt, rustic or raw surfaces
  • Muted earth palettes with no contrast
  • Asymmetric informal groups
  • Bare, cold light
  • Many small patterns at once

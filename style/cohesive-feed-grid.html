Cohesive Feed Grid

internet · 2016–now · also: Curated feed, Instagram grid, Aesthetic feed

A profile planned as one board: a 3-column grid of square photos with one preset, one palette and a fixed rhythm of image types. The viewer reads the whole grid before one photo.

Core thesis

The grid is the product: each photo serves the profile page, so tone, colour and spacing repeat until the feed reads as one brand.

Design principles

  • Lay photos in 3 columns with 2px gaps, each a 1:1 square of 1080px.
  • Apply one preset to every photo, so that the average hue stays within 15° across the feed.
  • Limit the palette to 3 colours and repeat each in at least 1 of every 3 photos.
  • Alternate image types in a fixed rhythm: portrait, detail, text tile, in rows of 3.
  • Build text tiles on a flat colour from the palette with a centred quote at 48px to 64px.
  • Keep the profile header plain: a 77px round avatar, a 14px bio and a row of 5 highlight circles of 64px.
  • Plan 9 posts ahead in a scheduling app and check the grid before each post.

Anti-patterns

  • Photos with clashing colour casts
  • Random posting with no plan
  • Heavy filters that change each week
  • Collage tiles with 4 or more colours
  • Photos with busy text overlays
  • Different crop ratios in one grid

Palette

  • Feed White #FAFAFA background
  • Page White #FFFFFF surface
  • Feed Ink #1A1A1A text
  • Signature Tan #B5835A primary
  • Preset Teal #4F7F82 accent
  • Dusty Blush #E8CFC4 tile
  • Hairline #DBDBDB border

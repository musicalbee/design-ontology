Glitchcore

internet · 2019–now · also: Glitch core, Digicore visuals

Fast, crowded edits with split colour channels, repeated frames, datamosh, bright flash and old web clutter. It grew with the digicore music scene on SoundCloud, Discord and short-video apps.

Core thesis

Overload the signal: show the fault, the repeat and the flash until the error becomes the style.

Design principles

  • Split the colour channels by 4px to 12px in opposite directions on all key images.
  • Cut the edit every 4 to 12 frames at 30 fps and repeat the same frame 2 to 4 times.
  • Use hot colours: acid green (#39FF14), magenta (#FF00AA) and cyan (#00E5FF) on black.
  • Fill the frame with 5 or more layers: text, stars, cursors, stickers and old web icons.
  • Set display text at 48px to 120px in a bold face, with a 3px stroke in a second colour.
  • Add scan lines of 2px at 12% opacity and noise at 8%.
  • Add a flash warning for any flicker above 3 flashes in 1s.

Anti-patterns

  • Calm, empty layouts
  • Soft pastel palettes
  • Slow, long takes
  • Subtle grey tones
  • Clean, uniform type
  • Natural photographic colour
  • Static, centred composition

Palette

  • Signal Black #050508 background
  • Acid Green #39FF14 primary
  • Hot Magenta #FF00AA accent
  • Cyan Split #00E5FF second accent
  • Flash White #FFFFFF text
  • Warning Yellow #FFE600 highlight
  • Window Grey #2B2B36 surface

Unboxing Video

internet · 2006–now · also: Unboxing, Product reveal, First look

A fixed top-down shot of a table. Two hands open a box and lift out a product, and a voice names each part. The look is plain on purpose, so the object is the star.

Core thesis

The viewer owns the first touch by proxy: show the opening of the box in real time and let the object, not the frame, carry the interest.

Design principles

  • Fix the camera above the table at 90° and frame the box at 40% to 60% of the picture height.
  • Use a plain ground in light grey or white (#F2EFE9) with no pattern and no props.
  • Show only two hands, with no face, until the product is out of the box.
  • Keep each cut at 4s to 12s; hold a close-up of the product for at least 3s.
  • Name each part with a lower-third label of 24px to 32px for 2s at the moment the part appears.
  • Show the price or the model name in one chip in the top-right corner for the full clip.
  • Light the table from the front at 5600K so that shadows stay soft and the colours stay true.

Anti-patterns

  • Dark, moody grounds with heavy vignettes
  • Quick cuts under 2s that hide the object
  • Busy desk props around the box
  • Stage lighting with coloured gels
  • Dense on-screen text over the product
  • Handheld camera shake

Palette

  • Table Grey #F2EFE9 background
  • Box White #FFFFFF surface
  • Packing Black #1B1B1D text
  • Label Orange #F26B1D primary
  • Tape Blue #1E88E5 accent
  • Cardboard #C9A26B highlight

Demotivational poster

internet · 2004–2012 · also: Demotivator, Despair poster

A black poster with a thin white frame, a photo, a large serif capital title and a small line of sad or dry text. It parodies the office motivational poster and spread on web forums from 2004.

Core thesis

Turn the cheerful office poster into a dry joke: the same frame and layout, with a bleak caption.

Design principles

  • Fill the canvas with black (#000000) and keep a 6% to 8% margin around the picture.
  • Frame the picture with a 2px white line and a 3px gap of black between the picture and the line.
  • Set the title in white serif capitals at 56px to 72px, centred, 24px under the frame, with +0.12em tracking.
  • Set one line of caption in 18px serif at #C8C8C8, centred, 12px under the title.
  • Choose a photograph with one clear subject; desaturate it by 10% to 30% and crop it to 4:3.
  • Keep the total ratio near 4:5; the picture takes 66% to 72% of the height.
  • Write a title of one word and a caption of one sentence; the caption states a hard fact in a calm voice.

Anti-patterns

  • Bright coloured backgrounds
  • Rounded frames or drop shadows
  • Hopeful, sincere captions
  • Sans-serif titles
  • More than two lines of caption
  • Busy collage instead of one subject

Palette

  • Poster Black #000000 background
  • Frame White #FFFFFF primary
  • Caption Grey #C8C8C8 secondary text
  • Photo Slate #5B6E80 photo tone
  • Warning Red #B3261E accent

AI Avatar Portrait

internet · 2022–2024 · also: Magic avatars, Avatar pack, Stylised AI selfie

A set of 20 stylised portraits of one face in many looks: fantasy, anime, space and painting. Phone apps made the form common at the end of 2022.

Core thesis

One face, many costumes: the pack turns a selfie into a character sheet, and the glow is the reward.

Design principles

  • Show a pack as a 4x5 grid of 20 square tiles, each 160px with a 6px gap.
  • Keep the same face in every tile and change the world around it: costume, ground and colour grade.
  • Set the eyes 10% to 15% larger than natural and smooth the skin to a airbrushed finish.
  • Put a glow ground behind the head: a radial gradient from #FFC857 to #2B1E5A.
  • Name each style under the tile in 12px sans, for example Fantasy, Anime, Cosmic.
  • Add a rounded Save pack button of 48px height in pink (#FF5FA2) below the grid.
  • Show a before and after pair: the plain selfie at 30% width beside the best result at 70%.

Anti-patterns

  • Raw, unedited selfies
  • Documentary grain and honest flaws
  • One uniform look across the pack
  • Muted, flat palettes
  • Dense text blocks
  • Brutalist, harsh frames

Palette

  • Violet Night #2B1E5A background
  • Panel Violet #3A2A78 surface
  • Cosmic Pink #FF5FA2 primary
  • Gold Glow #FFC857 accent
  • Cyan Edge #4ED6E6 second accent
  • Skin Glow #F4C7A9 image reference
  • Mist #F3EEFF text

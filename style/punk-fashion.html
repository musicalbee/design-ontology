Punk fashion

fashion · 1976–1984 · also: Punk dress, Bondage look, Seditionaries look, UK punk

The dress of the London punk scene from 1976. It has ripped and pinned T-shirts, tartan bondage trousers, studded leather jackets, rubber and PVC, and safety pins as ornament.

Core thesis

Dress is a protest that the wearer makes by hand: the garment is torn, pinned and written on, and it shocks on purpose.

Design principles

  • Tear, slash or cut open the cloth, and pin or lace the opening shut with a safety pin or a cord.
  • Print a slogan or an image on a plain cotton T-shirt by screen print, in one or two flat colours.
  • Cut the trouser narrow, to 16 to 18 cm at the hem, and join the legs with a bondage strap.
  • Use black leather, tartan, PVC and rubber as the main cloths.
  • Fix 20 or more metal studs or spikes along the collar and the shoulder of a leather jacket.
  • Hold the look with chains, zips, safety pins and dog collars.
  • Make or modify the piece yourself, and keep each stitch and tear visible.

Anti-patterns

  • Smooth couture finish
  • Soft floral prints and flowing cloth
  • Clean, new and matched sets
  • Pastel and gentle colour
  • Padded corporate tailoring
  • Expensive, polished ornament

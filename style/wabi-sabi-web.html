Wabi-Sabi Web

internet · 2015–now · also: Wabi-sabi, Imperfect minimal web, Japanese earth web

Muted earth tones, rough textures, uneven edges, much quiet space and small, calm type. It finds beauty in what is imperfect, old and incomplete, and it is the slow side of minimal design.

Core thesis

Imperfection is beauty: a worn, quiet surface shows time and honest craft.

Design principles

  • Use a ground of unbleached tones (#EAE4D8, #D9D1C1) and text in a warm charcoal (#3B3833); keep the palette to 4 muted colours at 10% to 35% saturation.
  • Add paper, plaster or clay texture at 4% to 10% opacity and let tone vary by 3% to 6% across a surface.
  • Leave 55% to 75% of each screen empty and place 1 focal item off-centre by 8% to 15%.
  • Set headings in a calm serif or a light sans at weight 300 to 400, size 28px to 56px, with tracking of +0.02em to +0.08em.
  • Round edges unevenly: use radii of 2px, 6px, 14px and 4px on the four corners of an image or shape.
  • Cut borders: use a 1px line at 20% opacity or none; mark the sections with 96px to 160px of space.
  • Animate slowly: fades of 800ms to 1200ms; one motion at a time.

Anti-patterns

  • Bright, saturated colours
  • Glossy gradients and glass
  • Perfectly symmetrical layouts
  • Dense grids of many items
  • Sharp perfect geometry with no texture
  • Bold, heavy type
  • Fast animation and popups

Palette

  • Washi #EAE4D8 background
  • Plaster #D9D1C1 surface
  • Charcoal #3B3833 text
  • Stone #5E5A54 secondary text
  • Moss #7A8564 accent
  • Rust #9B5B3C primary

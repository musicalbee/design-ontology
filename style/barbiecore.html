Barbiecore

internet · 2022–now · also: Barbie pink, Hot pink look

One hot pink on everything: pink dresses, pink plastic, pink houses and glossy toy shine. Photos of a Barbie film set in 2022 started the trend, and a pink season in fashion followed.

Core thesis

Make the whole world one pink, and play with it: toy gloss and total colour turn a doll into a dress code.

Design principles

  • Set the ground in hot pink (#E0218A) or pale pink (#FFE3F1) and keep pink at 60% or more of the frame.
  • Add one set of supports: white (#FFFFFF), sky blue (#7FD0F2) and a dark plum (#4A0F32) for text.
  • Set headings in a heavy, round or wide sans at 56px to 120px, tight at -0.02em, in white on pink or plum on pale pink.
  • Give surfaces toy gloss: a white diagonal highlight at 35% opacity and a 2px inner edge of lighter pink.
  • Dress one subject in head-to-toe pink, shot front-on against a matching pink wall.
  • Use rounded boxes of 20px to 32px, pill buttons and star or heart sparkles of 16px to 32px.
  • Frame products as packaging: a window box with a pink backing card and a plain price tag.

Anti-patterns

  • Muted, earth or grey palettes
  • Dark, moody grading
  • Thin serif text
  • Rough or grungy textures
  • Minimal neutral layouts
  • Several competing accent colours

Palette

  • Pale Pink #FFE3F1 background
  • White #FFFFFF surface
  • Plum #4A0F32 text
  • Barbie Pink #E0218A primary
  • Sky Blue #7FD0F2 accent
  • Candy #FF8CC6 highlight
  • Wine #7A2457 muted text

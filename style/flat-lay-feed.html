Flat Lay Feed

internet · 2014–now · also: Flat lay, Overhead still life, Knolling feed

Objects arranged on a flat surface and shot from straight above: coffee, a notebook, flowers, a phone. Marble or linen fills the ground, light is soft, and props sit in neat clusters with empty space.

Core thesis

A life laid flat on a table: choose objects, set them in order and shoot straight down so that the story reads like a diagram.

Design principles

  • Shoot at 90° from directly above and fill the square frame with one surface.
  • Use a pale ground: white marble (#F6F5F2), light oak or linen, lit by a window at 45° to the side.
  • Group 5 to 9 objects in odd numbers and keep 30% to 40% of the frame empty.
  • Repeat 1 accent colour, such as blush (#D9A7A0) or eucalyptus (#7C9A8A), in 3 props.
  • Align objects to the frame edge or at 90° and 45° angles, and keep 8% to 12% of the frame as a margin.
  • Place the hero object at one third of the frame and add a hand or a cup for scale.
  • Add a short lower-case caption at 14px and a row of 10 to 20 tags under the photo.

Anti-patterns

  • Photos from an oblique angle
  • Dark, cluttered tables
  • Even, centred, symmetric spacing with no focal object
  • Hard flash shadows
  • Busy patterned grounds
  • Many competing accent colours

Palette

  • Marble White #F6F5F2 background
  • Surface White #FFFFFF surface
  • Ink #222222 text
  • Blush #D9A7A0 primary
  • Eucalyptus #7C9A8A accent
  • Oak #B98F63 prop
  • Coffee #3B2A21 prop shadow

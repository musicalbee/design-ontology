Liquid Glass

internet · 2025–now · also: Apple Liquid Glass, Refractive glass UI, iOS 26 design

Clear, curved glass that bends the content behind it, with specular edges, tint that adapts to the ground and floating controls. Apple introduced it in 2025 and it goes beyond frosted glass.

Core thesis

Controls are a transparent lens above the content: the interface floats, refracts the page and stays out of the way.

Design principles

  • Make a glass layer with backdrop blur of 16px to 40px, saturation of 160% to 200% and a tint of white or ground colour at 8% to 20% opacity.
  • Draw a specular edge: a 1px inner border of white at 40% to 60% on the top and left, and white at 10% on the bottom and right.
  • Nest corners in concentric order: the inner radius equals the outer radius minus the padding (for example 28px outer, 12px padding, 16px inner).
  • Float navigation and toolbars as pills of 56px to 64px height, with a 12px to 16px margin from the screen edge and no full-width bar.
  • Let glass shrink and expand: a bar of 64px collapses to 40px on scroll in 250ms with a spring.
  • Place glass only over rich content (photo, video, map); no more than 2 glass layers may overlap.
  • Use a solid fill for content cards and a glass fill for controls only, so that glass marks what you can act on.

Anti-patterns

  • Glass on glass stacked more than 2 layers
  • Full-width opaque top bars
  • Hard borders of 2px or more and flat outlines
  • Glass behind long body text with low contrast
  • Heavy drop shadows that make the control look like a card
  • Square corners on floating controls
  • Tinted glass that hides the content behind it

Palette

  • Ground #F2F2F7 background
  • Ink #111114 text
  • Glass White #FFFFFF glass tint
  • Azure #0A84FF accent
  • Slate #5C5C66 secondary text
  • Dusk #1C1C22 dark ground

Health Goth

internet · 2013–2016 · also: Healthgoth, Health goth

Black, white and silver, 3D-rendered blades and mesh forms, sportswear and gym signs, with a cold, clean look. It grew from a 2013 online group that mixed goth, sport and net art.

Core thesis

Treat the body and the interface as one clean, dark machine: goth mood, gym function, no decoration that has no use.

Design principles

  • Set the ground at #0A0A0C and use white (#F4F4F5) as the main ink.
  • Render one 3D form (a blade, a mesh or a chrome ring) at 40% to 60% of the frame, with no people.
  • Use thin sans type in capitals at +0.2em, in 1 weight (300) for display.
  • Add one acid-silver accent (#C9D1D9) on no more than 8% of the surface.
  • Set strict 8px modules and align text on a 12-column grid with 24px gutters.
  • Add sport labels: a size code, a small cross or a rep count at 11px to 12px.
  • Keep the radius at 0px on panels and at 2px on buttons.

Anti-patterns

  • Warm, cosy palettes
  • Playful rounded type
  • Floral or lace goth decoration
  • Photographs of crowds
  • Rainbow gradients
  • Hand-drawn texture
  • Dense ornament

Palette

  • Void Black #0A0A0C background
  • Bone White #F4F4F5 text
  • Cold Silver #C9D1D9 primary
  • Graphite #1C1D22 surface
  • Steel #6B7280 muted
  • Acid Ice #B8F2E6 accent

16-bit sprite and parallax

game · 1988–1997 · also: 16-bit pixel art, SNES-era graphics, Fourth-generation console look

Pixel art at 256×224 with up to 256 colours on screen from a 32,768-colour range. Several scrolling layers give parallax depth, sprites carry 15 colours and soft ramps, and skies get gradients made of colour bands.

Core thesis

A richer palette and stacked layers let pixel art paint light, air and distance without a 3D engine.

Design principles

  • Use 2 to 4 background layers that scroll at different speeds for depth.
  • Shade each sprite with ramps of 3 to 5 steps of one hue, lit from the top left.
  • Use 15 colours plus transparent in each sprite.
  • Paint skies as horizontal bands of 4 to 8 colours, not a smooth gradient.
  • Use selective outlines: dark on the outside, coloured inside.
  • Make a character 24 to 48 px high.

Anti-patterns

  • Smooth photographic gradients
  • Pillow shading from the centre
  • Filtered or blurred scaling
  • Inconsistent light directions between sprites
  • 3D polygon objects

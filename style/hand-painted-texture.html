Hand-painted texture

game · 2004–now · also: Painted 3D, Stylised hand-painted, Painterly low poly

Mid-poly models with textures painted by hand: light, shadow, wear and colour shifts are in the paint, not in the engine. Chunky, exaggerated buildings and bright, saturated colour read clearly at a distance.

Core thesis

A painter's hand can carry the light of a world, so a small engine budget still gives a warm, rich place.

Design principles

  • Paint light from above into each texture: lighter top edges, darker bottoms.
  • Exaggerate shapes: thick walls, big beams, crooked roofs.
  • Use saturated, warm colours with cool hue-shifted shadows.
  • Paint edge wear as bright strokes on corners.
  • Keep the polygon count moderate and the silhouette strong.
  • Avoid photo sources; every pixel of texture is painted.

Anti-patterns

  • Photo textures
  • Normal-map micro detail
  • Grey, desaturated palettes
  • Thin, realistic proportions
  • Specular gloss on every surface

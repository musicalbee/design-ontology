Cordel xilogravura

print · c.1900–present · also: Cordel woodcut, Xilogravura de cordel, Literatura de cordel cover

A woodcut for the cover of a cordel chapbook, a cheap booklet of verse that hangs from a string. The cutter works in a soft wood, with bold white lines, flat shapes and one black ink.

Core thesis

One woodcut on a cheap cover can tell the whole story of a verse in a single bold picture.

Design principles

  • Cut the block in a soft wood with a knife and a gouge.
  • Make a strong, simple scene: a hero, an animal, a saint or a bandit, drawn full-face or in profile.
  • Use a thick black mass with white outline and a repeat pattern in the background.
  • Cut the title into the block, in simple blocky letters, or set it in type.
  • Print in black on a coloured paper cover; the paper gives the second colour.
  • Keep the picture to a small size, about A6 to A5.
  • Leave the wood grain and the knife marks in view.

Anti-patterns

  • Fine, shaded engraving
  • Halftone or photographic cover
  • Glossy paper
  • Many colours with an even blend
  • A thin, precise vector line

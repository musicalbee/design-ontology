Japandi

interior · 2016–present · also: Japanese-Scandinavian, Japanordic

A blend of the Scandinavian room and the Japanese room. Low, heavy wood furniture in pale and dark tones stands in a warm off-white room with wide empty floor; paper light, linen, stoneware and slatted screens give texture, and the tatami ratio of 1:2 orders the plan.

Core thesis

Calm comes from emptiness and from a few natural things made by hand and placed with care.

Design principles

  • Keep furniture low: seats at 38–40 cm and tables at 35–40 cm, so that the eye level of the room drops.
  • Leave 70% of the floor and 85% of the walls empty.
  • Hold the ratio 1:2 in rugs, panels, bed platforms and screens, after the tatami mat of 91 x 182 cm.
  • Pair pale oak or ash with one dark element, such as charred or black-stained wood, at a reflectance difference of 40–50 points.
  • Use only matt finishes: oiled wood, lime or clay plaster, unglazed or matt-glazed ceramics.
  • Divide zones with slatted screens of 3 cm battens at 3 cm gaps, not with walls.
  • Show 1 or 3 objects per surface, asymmetric, with one tall element such as a branch.

Anti-patterns

  • Gloss lacquer, chrome or mirror surfaces
  • Saturated colour on any surface larger than an object
  • Tall furniture above 90 cm in the middle of the room
  • Collections of small objects on open shelves
  • Printed pattern on textiles or walls
  • Bright cool light above 3500 K

Neumorphism

internet · 2019–2021 · also: Soft UI, New Skeuomorphism

Monochrome surfaces that look extruded from or pressed into the background, made with a light shadow and a dark shadow on opposite corners. It appeared on Dribbble in late 2019 as a soft answer to flat design.

Core thesis

An interface can be tactile without imitating objects, if every control is moulded from the same material as the page behind it.

Design principles

  • Give the background and every surface the same fill, so that shadows alone define the shape.
  • Pair two shadows on each raised element: a dark one at 8px 8px 16px and a light one at -8px -8px 16px, with a light source at the top left.
  • Invert both shadows with the inset keyword to show a pressed, selected or input state.
  • Keep the corner radius between 16px and 32px on cards and 999px on pills, so that the edges catch the shadow evenly.
  • Limit the palette to one mid-light neutral, a darker and a lighter tint of it, and one saturated accent used on less than 5% of the surface.
  • Use only three elevation levels: flat, raised at 8px offset and pressed at inset 4px offset.
  • Put the accent on the icon, the label or a thin indicator, because the shadow already marks the shape.

Anti-patterns

  • Hard black borders or 1px outlines around raised elements
  • Pure white or pure black backgrounds that cancel one of the two shadows
  • Raised and pressed elements that use different background colours
  • Light grey text on a light grey surface below 4.5:1 contrast
  • More than three elevation levels on one screen
  • Shadow offsets that disagree about the light direction
  • Saturated gradients on the surface itself

Palette

  • Clay #E0E5EC background and surface
  • Highlight #FFFFFF light shadow
  • Shade #A3B1C6 dark shadow
  • Slate #2D3748 text
  • Steel #5A6A80 secondary text
  • Signal Blue #3B6FE0 accent

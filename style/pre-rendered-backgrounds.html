Pre-rendered backgrounds

game · 1993–2002 · also: Pre-rendered 3D, Fixed camera with rendered scenes, Myst-like stills

Rooms and streets are rendered offline in 3D software and shown as still images at fixed camera angles. The player moves as a low-poly figure, or as a cursor, over a scene with far more light and detail than the console can draw.

Core thesis

If the camera does not move, each view can be a finished render, composed like a film frame.

Design principles

  • Compose each view as a still: a fixed camera with a strong diagonal or a low angle.
  • Render the background offline with soft shadows and many lights.
  • Match the real-time figure to the background with the same light direction.
  • Use dramatic camera angles: high, low, through a doorway.
  • Cut between views when the player crosses an edge.
  • Paint depth masks so the figure goes behind a table or a pillar.

Anti-patterns

  • A free camera
  • Backgrounds that the figure does not match in light
  • Flat eye-level views with no composition
  • Real-time textures on the background
  • Empty rooms with no props

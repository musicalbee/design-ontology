Editorial Serif Revival

internet · 2021–now · also: Serif web, Magazine web, New editorial

Large expressive serif headlines, warm off-white paper, a small sans for labels and a restrained palette. It came back as a reaction to the geometric sans-serif default of the SaaS web.

Core thesis

Voice beats neutrality: a serif with character makes a brand sound like a person, as in a magazine.

Design principles

  • Set headlines in a high-contrast serif at 56px to 120px with a leading of 0.95 to 1.05 and tracking of -0.02em to -0.04em.
  • Use a warm off-white ground (#F6F1E7) and near-black ink (#1A1714); one muted accent (terracotta #A64B25 or green) covers less than 8% of the surface.
  • Set body in a serif or a quiet sans at 18px to 20px with a measure of 55 to 70 characters and a leading of 1.55.
  • Set labels, dates and bylines in a small sans or mono at 11px to 13px in upper case with +0.08em tracking.
  • Use italic as the emphasis in a headline: one word or phrase in italic in each headline.
  • Divide the page by 1px rules; use drop caps and pull quotes at 32px to 48px.
  • Crop photographs in clean rectangles with a caption of 12px below and no radius.

Anti-patterns

  • Geometric sans-serif headlines in bold
  • Gradient backgrounds and glow
  • Rounded cards, pills and emoji
  • Uniform tile grids with shadows
  • All-bold type with no italic
  • Pure white backgrounds
  • Stock illustration in a corporate-flat style

Palette

  • Paper #F6F1E7 background
  • Ink #1A1714 text and rules
  • Terracotta #A64B25 accent
  • Stone #6F675C secondary text
  • Linen #EAE2D3 alternate surface

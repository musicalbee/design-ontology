Flat geometric isometric

game · 2014–now · also: Impossible isometric, Pastel geometric 3D, Low-poly flat mobile

Clean geometric architecture in an orthographic isometric view, coloured in flat pastel faces with no texture. Stairs, arches and towers are built like a paper model, and the space can fold into impossible shapes.

Core thesis

Architecture reduced to pure faces and an orthographic view becomes a calm puzzle that a hand can turn.

Design principles

  • Use an orthographic isometric camera with no perspective.
  • Colour each face direction in its own flat pastel tone: top light, sides darker.
  • Build with cubes, arches, stairs and columns only; no texture.
  • Keep generous empty space around the structure.
  • Use a soft gradient sky in one hue as the ground.
  • Make the figure tiny and simple against the structure.

Anti-patterns

  • Textures or noise
  • Perspective views
  • Cluttered props
  • Dark, saturated palettes
  • Realistic figures

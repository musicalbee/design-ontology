AI Slop

internet · 2024–now · also: Slop, Engagement-bait AI image, Feed filler art

Mass-made generated images in social feeds: a plastic gloss, a generic subject, warped hands, false text and a bait caption. The word slop for such content spread in 2024.

Core thesis

The image exists to fill a feed: it has a high finish, no author and no point, and its small errors show the lack of care.

Design principles

  • Pick a generic, emotive subject: a child with a cake, a log cabin at sunset, an animal made of fruit.
  • Render the subject at full gloss with a warm glow (#FFD08A) and a saturation of 120%.
  • Leave 1 to 3 faults in each image: an extra finger, a sign with false letters, or a seam in the background.
  • Put a bait caption of 16px to 20px above the image, in plain sans, with 2 to 4 emoji.
  • Add a reaction row with 3 round icons and a count in 14px under the image.
  • Set a square 1:1 or a 4:5 frame at 1080px wide, with a 1px light border on a light grey feed (#F2F3F5).
  • Post the same subject in 6 to 12 near copies, with small changes of colour or crop.

Anti-patterns

  • A clear author credit and a stated purpose
  • Careful, hand-drawn detail
  • Honest, quiet photography
  • Restraint in gloss and colour
  • Strong editorial layout
  • Faults that are corrected

Palette

  • Feed Grey #F2F3F5 background
  • Card White #FFFFFF surface
  • Ink #1C1E21 text
  • Feed Blue #2F6FDB primary
  • Plastic Orange #E8742A accent
  • Warm Glow #FFD08A image reference
  • Dead Grey #5C6168 muted

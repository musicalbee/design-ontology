Luxury Typography Web

internet · 2014–now · also: Luxury type web, High-end editorial web, Fashion house web

Large, high-contrast serif type on a calm ground, wide tracking on small caps, thin rules and few words. The letter is the main image, and space signals price and care.

Core thesis

Luxury is restraint and craft: fine type, wide space and few words.

Design principles

  • Set the hero in a high-contrast serif at 72px to 160px, weight 300 to 400, with tracking of -0.02em.
  • Set labels and menu in upper case at 11px to 12px with tracking of +0.2em to +0.3em.
  • Use a ground of ivory (#F6F1E8) or deep black (#0E0D0C) and text in the opposite tone; add one metal accent (#A8863B) for rules and small marks.
  • Use hairlines of 1px in the accent; use no box, no shadow and no radius above 0px.
  • Show one product or one image per screen at large size with 120px or more of space around it.
  • Write short copy of 12 words or fewer per block and avoid sale words and prices in the hero.
  • Animate slowly: fades and 16px lifts in 700ms to 1100ms.

Anti-patterns

  • Discount badges and urgency timers
  • Bold, heavy sans type
  • Bright saturated colour
  • Rounded, soft buttons
  • Dense product grids with small images
  • Stock icons and emoji
  • Drop shadows and gradients

Palette

  • Ivory #F6F1E8 background
  • Linen #ECE4D4 surface
  • Ink #16140F text
  • Taupe #6A6153 secondary text
  • Black #16140F primary
  • Brass #836320 accent
  • Rule #CFC4AC border

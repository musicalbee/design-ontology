Surreal meme

internet · 2016–now · also: Surreal memes, Absurdist meme, Weird meme

An image with an odd crop, a strange cut-out or a calm, odd caption that has no clear punch line. It grew from Facebook groups and Reddit in 2016 and often breaks the old image-macro rules on purpose.

Core thesis

Drop the joke structure: let a strange picture and a calm caption make a mood with no answer.

Design principles

  • Compose 2 to 4 layers: a flat or photographic ground, a cut-out figure and one odd object.
  • Use a calm caption of 1 to 6 words in a plain sans-serif at 24px to 36px, off the usual top and bottom positions.
  • Place the cut-out with a 1px to 2px visible rough edge, scaled 20% to 400% out of true size.
  • Use a limited, odd colour pair: a flat teal (#2E9C9A) and a dull orange (#E08A3C) on grey.
  • Add a 3% to 8% grain and one soft glow, with no clean grading.
  • Leave 30% to 50% of the canvas empty, so that the odd object has air.
  • Repeat one object or name across a series to build a private joke.

Anti-patterns

  • Clear setup and punch line
  • Impact capitals at top and bottom
  • Polished photo realism
  • Many captions at once
  • Clean sticker-style outlines
  • Explanations of the joke

Palette

  • Dull Teal #2E9C9A ground
  • Dull Orange #E08A3C accent
  • Concrete Grey #8A8F98 ground
  • Night #121417 dark
  • Off White #F2EFE8 text

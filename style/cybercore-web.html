Cybercore Web

internet · 2020–now · also: Cybercore, Cyber Y2K, Techwear web

Black grounds, acid-green or cyan accents, chrome, angular cuts, HUD frames, small caps data labels and glitch. It mixes 1990s cyber fiction with techwear, and it feels like a machine interface.

Core thesis

The page is a machine interface: sharp, labelled, cold and dense with data.

Design principles

  • Set the ground at #05070A and use 1 acid accent (#C6FF00 or #00F0FF) on no more than 10% of the surface.
  • Cut corners at 45° with a clip-path of 8px to 16px on cards and buttons; set radius to 0px elsewhere.
  • Frame panels with a 1px line in the accent at 40% and add 4 corner ticks of 8px to 12px.
  • Set data labels at 10px to 12px in upper case with tracking of +0.12em to +0.2em in a mono or a wide display face.
  • Add a glitch on hover: offset the text layers by 2px to 4px in cyan and magenta for 120ms to 200ms.
  • Overlay scanlines of 2px at 6% to 10% opacity and a grain of 3% to 5%.
  • Add 3 or more HUD data items per screen: a code, a coordinate, a version number or a timer.

Anti-patterns

  • Soft pastel palettes
  • Rounded corners above 4px
  • Friendly illustration
  • Large empty areas without any data label
  • Warm photography with natural colour
  • Serif body text
  • Smooth drop shadows on cards

Palette

  • Void #05070A background
  • Acid #C6FF00 primary
  • Cyan #00F0FF accent
  • Chrome #C9D1D9 text
  • Steel #8E949B secondary text
  • Alarm #FF2D6F alert

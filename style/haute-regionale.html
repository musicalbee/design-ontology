Haute cuisine régionale

cuisine · 1925–1965 · also: Fernand Point cuisine, La Pyramide cuisine, Regional haute cuisine

The restaurant of a master in a provincial town. Fernand Point at La Pyramide in Vienne cooks the classic sauce with the butter, cream and produce of his own region. A small kitchen, a short menu and a fine cellar draw guests to the road.

Core thesis

Cook the classic dish well with the best produce of the region, on a short menu, and make the restaurant a place of travel.

Design principles

  • Buy the best local produce each day: Bresse chicken, crayfish, cream, butter and cheese.
  • Keep the classic sauce, and cook it with fresh butter and cream, with no short cut.
  • Keep the menu short and cook each dish to order, with a small team in the kitchen.
  • Serve each dish on a plate or a platter at the table, with the sauce beside it.
  • Train young cooks in the kitchen; each one learns each station.
  • Pair each dish with a wine of the Rhône valley, and let the cellar lead the meal.
  • Let the guest take time: a good meal takes a whole afternoon.

Anti-patterns

  • A long menu of dishes made in advance
  • A grand pièce montée on the table
  • A heavy sauce that hides the produce
  • Produce from far away when a local one is as good
  • A cook who does not taste
  • A hurried meal

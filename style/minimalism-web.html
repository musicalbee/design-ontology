Minimalism Web

internet · 2012–now · also: Minimal web, Less is more UI, Quiet web

A page with one idea per screen: few elements, much space, one type family and one accent. Every part must earn its place; what stays is clear, calm and fast.

Core thesis

Remove until only the need is left: space and restraint carry the message.

Design principles

  • Show one idea, one action and one image or none per screen.
  • Use 60% to 75% of the surface as empty space; the spacing scale is 8, 16, 32, 64 and 128px.
  • Use one type family in 2 weights and a scale of 14, 16, 24 and 64px.
  • Set the page in white or off-white (#FAFAF8) with text in near-black (#161616) and one accent for the main action only.
  • Use no border, shadow or gradient; separate parts with space or a hairline of 1px at 12% opacity.
  • Keep navigation to 5 items or fewer and show no icon that has no clear meaning.
  • Animate only state changes, in 150ms to 250ms.

Anti-patterns

  • Decorative ornament
  • Gradients and shadows for depth
  • More than 1 accent colour
  • Banners, pop-ups and carousels
  • Dense navigation with many levels
  • Stock photos used as filler
  • Clutter of badges, icons and labels

Palette

  • Paper #FAFAF8 background
  • Panel #F0F0EC surface
  • Ink #161616 text
  • Stone #55554F secondary text
  • Signal #2F5BEA primary
  • Signal Ink #1E3FB0 accent
  • Hairline #D9D9D3 border

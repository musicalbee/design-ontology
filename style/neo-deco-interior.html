Neo-deco

interior · 2020–present · also: Art Deco revival, New Deco, Modern Deco

Art Deco after a century, made soft. Curved bouclé and velvet seats, fluted wood, arches, ribbed glass and brushed brass keep the geometry and the glamour of the 1920s, but in warm, muted colours and rounded forms.

Core thesis

Deco glamour without the hard edges: curves, ribs and warm metal make luxury feel soft and personal.

Design principles

  • Round every form: curved sofas, arched mirrors and doorways, pill-shaped tables.
  • Use ribs and flutes as the main ornament: fluted wood, reeded glass, ribbed plaster.
  • Keep the palette warm and muted: terracotta, sage, ochre, burgundy and cream with brushed brass.
  • Place one curved seat group in the centre of the room, not against the walls.
  • Use one or two glamorous materials, such as velvet and brass, with matt plaster.
  • Light with globe and mushroom lamps and with arched niches lit from inside.

Anti-patterns

  • Sharp-cornered, rectilinear furniture
  • High-gloss black lacquer on large walls
  • Cold blue light
  • Rustic, raw finishes
  • Saturated primary colours

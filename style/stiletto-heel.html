Stiletto heel

fashion · 1953–present · also: Spike heel, Pointed-toe court shoe, Stiletto court shoe

The court shoe on a thin heel of 7 to 12 cm, held by a steel shank. It has a long, narrow vamp, a pointed toe and a heel no wider than a pencil at the tip.

Core thesis

A thin steel-cored heel lifts the heel and lengthens the leg, so the shoe is about line, balance and the arch of the foot.

Design principles

  • Set the heel at 7 to 12 cm, and thin it to 1 cm or less at the tip.
  • Core the heel with a steel rod, so that it carries the weight of the body.
  • Fit a steel shank in the arch, so that the sole does not sag.
  • Cut a long, pointed vamp; show the toe cleft or a small toe cover.
  • Set the heel straight under the centre of the heel bone.
  • Cut the throat low so that the instep shows.
  • Cover the shoe in one material and one colour, with a matched heel.

Anti-patterns

  • A thick, block heel
  • A square or round toe
  • A platform sole
  • A shoe with decoration on every part
  • A flat, soft sole that bends at the arch

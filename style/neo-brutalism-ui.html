Neo-Brutalism UI

internet · 2020–present · also: Neubrutalism, Neobrutalism, Brutalist Web UI

Flat, loud colour fields with thick black borders and hard, unblurred offset shadows. It grew from product sites such as Gumroad and from Figma community kits as a deliberate reply to corporate softness.

Core thesis

An interface earns trust by showing its structure openly and being loud about it, because every border and shadow is a stated fact.

Design principles

  • Draw a solid #000000 border of 2px to 4px around every card, button, input and image.
  • Cast one hard shadow with zero blur, offset 4px 4px, for example 4px 4px 0 0 #000000, on every raised element.
  • Fill surfaces with flat saturated colour at 100% opacity, and use two to four loud hues on one page.
  • On hover, move the element by the shadow offset and cut the shadow to 0, so that it appears to press into the page.
  • Use radii of 0px to 8px only, and use the same value on all elements of one page.
  • Set headings in a heavy grotesque or mono face at weight 700 or higher, in sizes from 48px to 96px.
  • Keep text black on colour fields, and reach at least 7:1 contrast on every fill.

Anti-patterns

  • Blurred drop shadows with a blur radius above 0px
  • Subtle grey gradients or glossy highlights on surfaces
  • Pastel, low-saturation fills with grey text
  • Hairline 1px borders or borders in a colour other than black
  • Mixed corner radii on one page, such as 4px on cards and 24px on buttons
  • Translucent or backdrop-blurred panels
  • Thin font weights below 500 for headings

Palette

  • Ink #000000 borders, shadows and text
  • Cream #FFF8E7 page background
  • Lemon #FFE14D highlight and primary fill
  • Hot Pink #FF5DA2 accent fill
  • Electric Blue #4D7CFF secondary accent
  • Mint #6EE7B7 success and tag fill
  • White #FFFFFF card surface

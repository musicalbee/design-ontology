Glassmorphism

internet · 2020–present · also: Frosted Glass UI, Glass UI

Translucent panels that blur the colourful shapes behind them, edged with a thin light border. It spread after macOS Big Sur in 2020 and revives the glass of Frutiger Aero with real backdrop blur.

Core thesis

Layers are readable when each one is a pane of frosted glass: it shows what lies beneath it and still keeps its own plane.

Design principles

  • Place panels over a vivid background of gradients or blurred shapes, because blur needs colour behind it to be visible.
  • Fill each panel with rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15) to 0.25 and apply backdrop-filter: blur(16px to 24px) saturate(160%).
  • Add a 1px border of rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3) on every panel, to mark the glass edge.
  • Use radii of 16px to 24px on panels and 12px on buttons.
  • Show depth by stacking: the further layer has a blur of 24px and the nearer layer a blur of 8px.
  • Keep one soft shadow of 0 8px 32px rgba(31, 38, 135, 0.2) under each panel, to lift it from the background.
  • Put text on a panel only when the area under it holds at least 4.5:1 contrast, or add a scrim of rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25).

Anti-patterns

  • Blur on a plain white or plain grey background where nothing shows through
  • Body text on a panel with less than 4.5:1 contrast to the blurred layer behind
  • Opaque panels with a faked frost texture and no backdrop-filter
  • Stacks of more than three overlapping glass layers
  • Panel borders thicker than 1px or darker than the fill
  • Hard black drop shadows under a glass panel
  • Blur radii above 40px on large areas, which cost frame rate

Palette

  • Aurora Violet #6C5CE7 background gradient start
  • Aurora Pink #FD79A8 background gradient end
  • Glass White #FFFFFF panel fill at 15% to 25% alpha and border
  • Deep Ink #1B1B3A text on light glass
  • Sky #74B9FF accent
  • Midnight #0F0F2D dark mode background

Normcore

internet · 2013–now · also: Anti-fashion, Plain basics

Plain, unbranded clothes and plain pages: grey fleece, white trainers, dad jeans, default fonts and flat daylight snapshots. A trend report named it in 2013, and a magazine story spread it.

Core thesis

Choose sameness over difference: a look that blends in is a calm answer to a feed that rewards standing out.

Design principles

  • Set the page on a light grey (#F2F2F0) or white ground with one denim blue (#35597F) for links and buttons.
  • Use one neutral sans-serif in two weights, 400 and 600, at sizes 14, 16, 20 and 32px.
  • Show products or people in flat daylight, shot front-on at eye level with no filter and no props.
  • Keep a single column of 640px to 720px with 24px side margins and 32px between blocks.
  • Write labels as plain nouns: "Jeans", "Fleece", "Cart". Use no slogan and no exclamation mark.
  • Keep corners at 4px and borders at 1px in #D4D4D0; use no shadow and no gradient.
  • Cap the palette at 4 neutrals and 1 blue, and set every logo or brand mark at small size or not at all.

Anti-patterns

  • Loud brand logos or monograms
  • Hero gradients and neon accents
  • Styled or retouched photography
  • Display fonts with character
  • Dramatic shadows and glass panels
  • Slogans that claim a lifestyle
  • Dense decoration of any kind

Palette

  • Fleece Grey #F2F2F0 background
  • White #FFFFFF surface
  • Ink #1F2328 text
  • Denim #35597F primary
  • Sage Grey #6B7F73 accent
  • Hairline #D4D4D0 border
  • Slate #5A5F66 muted text

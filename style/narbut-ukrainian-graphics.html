Narbut Ukrainian Baroque graphics

print · 1910–1920 · also: Narbut style, Ukrainian Baroque graphics, Графіка Нарбута, Українська книжкова графіка

A black pen drawing with Baroque capitals and heraldry, printed from zinc line blocks, sometimes with one or two flat tints. The dense ornament and the hand lettering imitate old engraved Ukrainian books.

Core thesis

A dense black pen line and Baroque lettering can make a modern cover that looks like a page from an old Ukrainian book.

Design principles

  • Draw every form with a fine pen line of 0.15–0.3 mm at print size, and fill the darks with solid black.
  • Set the title in hand-drawn Baroque capitals; give each letter a swelling stem and a curled foot.
  • Put the title, the arms or the main figure in a cartouche of scrolls, leaves and ribbons.
  • Use heraldic forms (shield, crown, helm, mantling) as the main motif of a cover or a page.
  • Balance black and white: let heavy black fills sit beside open areas of bare paper.
  • Use one flat red or ochre tint as the only colour, and put it on the title or the shield.
  • Print the pen line from a zinc line block so that the fine hairlines stay sharp.

Anti-patterns

  • A soft, tonal drawing and a gradient
  • A clean modern sans-serif title
  • Pastel colours and loose watercolour
  • A thick, blunt outline with no fine detail
  • A page with no ornament and no lettering of its own

Art Nouveau monogram

logo · 1890–1914 · also: Jugendstil monogram, Nieuwe Kunst vignette, Secession monogram

Interlaced initials and lettering drawn as one ornament, inside a frame of whiplash curves and plant forms. Publishers, societies and workshops used it as a vignette on books, journals and printed matter.

Core thesis

The letters and the ornament are one drawing: a mark grows like a plant from one line, and the hand of the artist shows in each curve.

Design principles

  • Interlace 1–3 initials so that their strokes share at least one stem or one bowl.
  • Draw each curve as a whiplash: an S-curve whose radius changes along the stroke by a factor of 3 or more.
  • Close the mark in a frame of plant stems or tendrils, with the frame line 1.5–2 times the letter stroke.
  • Draw custom capitals with small x-heights, high crossbars and open, rounded counters; no typeface from a foundry.
  • Keep stroke contrast low, 1.5:1 to 3:1, so that the mark prints in one ink as a block.
  • Fit the mark to a vertical rectangle near 3:4 or a square, centred on a vertical axis.

Anti-patterns

  • Straight-sided geometric letters from a typeface
  • Hatching and tonal shading
  • More than 2 printing inks
  • Rigid circles and squares as the only frame
  • Letters that float apart with no shared stroke

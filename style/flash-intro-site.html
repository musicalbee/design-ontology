Flash Intro Site

internet · 1998–2010 · also: Skip intro, Flash website, Macromedia Flash site

A full-window site built as vector animation: a loading bar, a 'skip intro' link, tiny pixel type, custom cursors, sound and transitions between sections. Macromedia Flash made it possible.

Core thesis

A site is a performance: control every pixel and every second, and make the visitor wait for it.

Design principles

  • Fill the full window with one stage and centre a fixed area of 800px by 600px or 1000px by 600px.
  • Open with a loading bar of 1px to 4px and a counter from 0% to 100%, then a 5 to 20 second intro.
  • Put a 'skip intro' link of 11px in the lower corner, visible from the first frame.
  • Set navigation in pixel type of 8px to 10px in upper case, with 0.1em tracking, and place it on a free path.
  • Move each section in by tween: slide 40px to 120px with an ease-out over 400ms to 800ms.
  • Use one accent (#FF6600) on a dark ground (#111111) and keep the rest in grey tones.
  • Follow the pointer: an element trails the cursor at 0.1 to 0.2 easing.

Anti-patterns

  • Instant static pages with no intro
  • Standard text navigation in a bar
  • Long blocks of readable body text
  • Responsive reflow to small screens
  • Native scroll with no scene change
  • Plain system fonts at 16px for all text

Palette

  • Stage Black #111111 background
  • Panel Grey #1E1E1E panel
  • Text Light #EEEEEE text
  • Muted Grey #9A9A9A secondary text
  • Flash Orange #FF6600 primary
  • Cyan #33CCFF accent
  • Line Grey #444444 border

Chiaroscuro woodcut

print · 1508–c.1620 · also: clair-obscur, xilografia a più legni (Italian), Helldunkelholzschnitt (German)

A line block carries the drawing and one to three tone blocks are printed over it in flat washes of one hue. The flat steps from paper white to dark ground imitate a brush-and-wash drawing heightened with white.

Core thesis

Flat tone blocks printed in steps over a line block imitate a brush-and-wash drawing heightened with white, with no hatching.

Design principles

  • Cut a key block for the contour and the deepest shadow, and print it in black or dark brown.
  • Cut one to three tone blocks, and print each as a flat wash of one hue at 20–60 % of the key density.
  • Cut the highlights out of each tone block, so that the bare paper shows as white.
  • Keep each tone edge hard; step from one wash to the next and do not blend.
  • Print all tone blocks in one hue family, so that the picture reads as one drawing.
  • Allow 0.5–1.5 mm of key-block offset against the tone blocks.

Anti-patterns

  • A smooth gradient or a soft wash edge
  • Halftone dots or a photographic tone
  • White highlights added with opaque white ink
  • Hatching that fills the tone areas
  • Tone blocks in unrelated hues

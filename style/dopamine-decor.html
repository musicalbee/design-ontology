Dopamine decor

interior · 2021–present · also: Dopamine interior, Joyful maximalism, Colour drenching

A room built for joy after the years at home of 2020 and 2021. Saturated hues drench walls, trim and ceiling; curved, inflated furniture in velvet and bouclé stands on patterned rugs; objects are personal and sorted by colour.

Core thesis

Colour and play in the home lift the mood, and a room must show the joy of the person who lives in it.

Design principles

  • Drench one wall plane, with its trim and its part of the ceiling, in one saturated hue.
  • Use 4–6 saturated hues and repeat each hue in at least 3 places in the room.
  • Choose curved, pill-shaped and inflated forms with corner radii of 10 cm or more.
  • Lay one patterned rug, checkerboard, wave or free blob, with a repeat of 20–40 cm.
  • Set the strongest hue on the wall in the sightline from the entrance.
  • Sort books and objects by hue on the shelves, in rainbow order.
  • Give the lamps colour too: candy-coloured glass, acrylic and mushroom shades.

Anti-patterns

  • Beige, grey and greige as the main palette
  • Straight-edged boxy furniture in neutral fabric
  • One accent colour on a neutral room
  • Bare walls without art or objects
  • Cool white light above 4000 K
  • Matching furniture sets from one range

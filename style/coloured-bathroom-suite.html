Coloured bathroom suite

ceramics · 1927–1975 · also: Pastel bathroom suite, Pink and green sanitary ware, Matching coloured fixtures

A bath, a basin and a WC glazed in the same strong colour: pink, green, blue, yellow or later avocado. The even gloss glaze and the matching set make the bathroom a room of colour.

Core thesis

Sanitary ware can be a colour of the room, and one even glaze on every piece makes a bathroom that matches.

Design principles

  • Glaze the basin, the pan, the cistern and the bath in one colour from the same batch.
  • Choose one pastel or earth colour: pink, sky blue, mint, buttercup yellow or avocado green.
  • Shape each piece with soft curves and a radius of 30 to 60 mm on every edge.
  • Keep the glaze even, with a gloss of 85 to 95 GU and no pattern.
  • Match the colour of the tiles, the towels and the walls to the suite.
  • Add chrome taps with a small white or clear cross handle.
  • Mould a shell, a ridge or a streamline band as the only relief.

Anti-patterns

  • A white suite with a coloured bath only
  • Two different shades of one colour in a set
  • A printed flower pattern on the glaze
  • A dark matte glaze that stains
  • A thin shape with a sharp edge

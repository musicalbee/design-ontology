Blob World

internet · 2015–present · also: 3D blob illustration, Soft 3D, Clay render

Motion design and illustration made from strange 3D renders: soft blobs, surreal rooms, clay, fur, jelly and inflatable textures, sometimes uncanny. It is an illustration world, not a component style: glassmorphism and 3D UI style the controls; Blob World fills the picture.

Core thesis

Make the screen a toy world: soft, over-lit 3D bodies with odd textures carry the brand, and the interface stays plain around them.

Design principles

  • Build each figure from rounded volumes with no sharp edge: a minimum radius of 24% of the shape width.
  • Render one hero scene per page at 1600px or wider, with a soft key light from the top left and a shadow of 40px to 80px blur.
  • Give each surface one tactile material: clay, fur, jelly, inflatable plastic or felt, with a visible grain at 100% zoom.
  • Use a palette of 3 saturated hues on a pastel ground (#FFE9D6), with one acid accent.
  • Place 3 to 7 floating shapes around the hero at 3 depths, with sizes in a ratio of 1 : 2 : 4.
  • Set type in a rounded, heavy face (weight 600 to 700) at 56px or larger for display, and keep body text at 18px in a plain ground block.
  • Loop motion for 4s to 8s: bodies squash and stretch by 6% to 12%, and drift by 8px to 24px.

Anti-patterns

  • Flat vector figures with no volume
  • Hard-edged geometric shapes
  • Photographic stock images
  • Frosted glass panels as the main image
  • Dark, realistic lighting with deep shadow
  • Dense data tables beside the render
  • Static scenes with no motion

Palette

  • Peach Ground #FFE9D6 background
  • Jelly Coral #FF5A4D primary
  • Clay Violet #7A5CFF accent
  • Fur Teal #2EC4B6 second accent
  • Acid Lime #D4FF3A highlight
  • Cream Surface #FFF7EE surface

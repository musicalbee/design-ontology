GAN Portrait

internet · 2018–now · also: StyleGAN face, This person does not exist, Generated headshot

A square headshot of a person who does not exist: the eyes sit at a fixed height, the background is a soft swirl, and small faults show in teeth, ears and glasses. It spread from 2018 with StyleGAN.

Core thesis

A face that nobody owns: the model averages thousands of faces into one believable head, and its faults are the signature.

Design principles

  • Centre one head at 1024px square with the eye line at 40% from the top and the eyes 30% of the width apart.
  • Set a blurred, swirled background of 2 to 3 warm hues at 25% to 40% blur strength.
  • Keep the shoulders at the lower 25% of the frame with a soft, plain garment.
  • Leave one small fault: a mismatched earring, a teeth blur, or a bright water-drop patch of 40px to 80px.
  • Align faces in a 4x4 grid of 256px tiles with a 8px gap to show many generated heads at once.
  • Use a plain caption in 14px sans below the tile; add the word generated and the seed number.
  • Keep the palette natural: skin tones, brown blur and one cool background tint.

Anti-patterns

  • Stylised illustration or anime faces
  • Strong dramatic lighting
  • Full-body or action shots
  • Dense overlays and ornament
  • Sharp, detailed backgrounds
  • Perfect symmetry with no fault

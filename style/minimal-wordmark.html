Wordmark-led minimal

logo · 1975–now · also: Logotype, Minimal wordmark, Type-only logo

The name alone, set or drawn in one weight of a sans, with a single detail that makes it own: a cut stroke, a hidden arrow, a joined pair of letters. No symbol and no frame.

Core thesis

The name is the mark: one carefully spaced word, with one detail of its own, is clearer and more durable than any symbol.

Design principles

  • Use the name in one line, in one weight of a sans or a custom monoline alphabet.
  • Make exactly one detail own the mark: a removed crossbar, a ligature, a cut stroke or a shape in the counter.
  • Space by eye: optical tracking of 0–5% for lower case, 5–20% for capitals.
  • Use one colour for the master; a second colour may mark one part of the word only.
  • Keep the height of the word at 15–25% of its width, so it fits a header and a narrow sign.
  • Define clear space as the height of one capital or one x-height on each side.

Anti-patterns

  • A symbol beside the word in the master mark
  • Two or more typefaces
  • Outline, shadow or bevel on the letters
  • A frame or a badge around the word
  • Distortion of letter widths to fit a box

Dual Camera Snapshot

internet · 2022–now · also: BeReal look, Front and back photo, Late photo

One post made of two photos from one moment: the back camera fills the frame and the front camera sits in a small inset. A time stamp and a late mark show that it was not planned.

Core thesis

Proof of a real moment: two cameras fire at once, there is no edit, and the late mark shows that the poster did not stage it.

Design principles

  • Take both photos in one 2-second window: the back camera fills the frame and the front camera fills an inset.
  • Place the inset at the top left, 28% of the frame width, with a 2px white border and a 12px radius.
  • Allow no filter, no crop and no edit: keep the raw colour and the phone noise.
  • Show a time stamp such as '14:32' and a late mark such as '2h late' in a mono face at 12px under the photo.
  • Set the page on pure black (#0B0B0B) so that the photo carries all the colour.
  • Limit the caption to 1 line of 40 characters or fewer, in white.
  • Show friends in a list sorted by time, with one tile for each person and a blur on tiles not yet unlocked.

Anti-patterns

  • Filters, presets and retouch
  • Staged poses with props
  • A single camera view
  • Long captions and tags
  • Bright, colourful app chrome
  • A fixed grid rhythm

Palette

  • Viewfinder Black #0B0B0B background
  • Panel Black #1A1A1A surface
  • Frame White #FFFFFF text
  • Grain Grey #BDBDBD muted
  • Late Red #FF3B30 accent
  • Timestamp Amber #FFD60A highlight
  • Neutral Grey #8E8E93 border

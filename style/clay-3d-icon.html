3D clay icon

icon · 2020–present · also: 3D icon, Soft 3D icon, Fluent 3D emoji style

Icons rendered in 3D with soft, matte, clay-like materials, large round corners and a soft shadow. They bring depth back after the flat years, but with toy-like simplicity and no real texture.

Core thesis

After a decade of flat glyphs, an icon can have volume again if the forms stay simple, round and soft like a toy.

Design principles

  • Model each object from simple volumes with large fillets: a radius of 15–25% of the object size.
  • Use a matte or satin material with soft subsurface light; no hard gloss.
  • Light the object with one large soft key light from the top left and a fill light at 30–50%.
  • Use a three-quarter view from 15–30° above.
  • Use 3 to 6 saturated pastel hues per icon, with gentle gradients from the light.
  • Ship each icon as a PNG at 256 px or more, with a transparent ground.

Anti-patterns

  • Hard, sharp edges
  • Photographic texture and dirt
  • Glossy chrome reflections
  • Flat front views
  • Thin line strokes

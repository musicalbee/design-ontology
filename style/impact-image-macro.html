Impact image macro

internet · 2006–now · also: Impact meme, Top-text bottom-text meme, Advice Animal

A square photo or a cartoon with white capital text in a thick black outline at the top and the bottom. The Impact typeface and the format spread on forums and 4chan from 2006.

Core thesis

Make the caption the picture: one setup line on top, one punch line below, in a font that stays legible at any size.

Design principles

  • Set text in Impact or an open look-alike (Anton, Oswald 700) in white capitals.
  • Add a black outline of 3px to 4px per 40px of text, drawn outside the glyph.
  • Place the setup line at the top and the punch line at the bottom, each 5% to 8% from the edge.
  • Size the text so that each line spans 80% to 95% of the width; break to a second line at 24 characters.
  • Crop the picture to a square or to 4:3 and let it fill the whole canvas.
  • Use a picture with an obvious face or pose; no logo, no border, no extra graphics.
  • Keep the captions short: 3 to 8 words in each line.

Anti-patterns

  • Lower-case text
  • Thin or unoutlined caption
  • Caption in a coloured box
  • Soft, tasteful photo grading
  • Long paragraphs of text
  • A decorative frame around the picture

Palette

  • Caption White #FFFFFF text
  • Outline Black #000000 text outline
  • Photo Sky #6FA8DC photo tone
  • Photo Skin #E0AC69 photo tone
  • Photo Grass #6AA84F photo tone

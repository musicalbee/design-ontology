Muscle and Pony Car

fashion · 1964–1974 · also: American muscle car, Pony car, Detroit performance coupe

The young, fast American coupe. A long bonnet, a short rear deck and a fastback or notch roof. It has a bold stripe, a bonnet scoop, vinyl bucket seats and a woodgrain dash.

Core thesis

A fast car is for the young and must cost little. It has a long bonnet over a big engine, a short tail and a few bold marks, on a small base.

Design principles

  • Make the bonnet long and the rear deck short, with the cabin set well back.
  • Keep the body compact, 4.6 to 4.9 m long, and the roof about 1.3 m high.
  • Fit a grille with a galloping horse, a bull or a bar, and set the headlamps wide apart.
  • Add bold marks: a racing stripe, a bonnet scoop, a rear spoiler or side stripes.
  • Show the engine size on the wing, in chrome numerals or a badge.
  • Trim the cabin with vinyl bucket seats, a bench or a console, and a woodgrain-effect dashboard.
  • Offer many options so that each buyer builds a car to his own taste.

Anti-patterns

  • Tail fins and heavy chrome
  • A tall, upright cabin
  • A fine, hand-built body
  • Dull body colours with no stripe
  • A body that is soft and round

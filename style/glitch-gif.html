Glitch GIF

internet · 2010–now · also: Datamosh loop, RGB-split GIF, Corrupted loop

A looped clip with torn bands, split colour channels and repeated frames, as if the signal failed. Artists on Tumblr and in net art circles made it common after 2010.

Core thesis

A fault in the signal is an image: show the error, do not hide it.

Design principles

  • Shift the red channel by 3px to the left and the blue channel by 3px to the right on each glitch frame.
  • Cut 3 to 8 horizontal bands of 4px to 40px height and move each sideways by 10px to 60px.
  • Run the glitch in bursts of 2 to 6 frames inside a loop of 20 to 40 frames at 12 fps.
  • Keep 60% of the frames clean, so that the error reads as an error.
  • Limit the palette to 64 colours and keep the dither visible.
  • Set text as a mono line at 14px with 2 duplicate offsets in red and cyan at 40% opacity.
  • Place a scanline layer of 2px stripes at 8% opacity over the frame.

Anti-patterns

  • A fully corrupted loop with no clean frame
  • Smooth gradients with no channel split
  • Glitch applied to body text
  • Soft pastel palettes
  • Slow dissolves in place of hard cuts
  • Photographic noise with no band structure

Palette

  • Dead Signal #0A0A12 background
  • Panel Night #171726 surface
  • Signal White #F2F2FA text
  • Channel Red #FF2D55 primary
  • Channel Cyan #00F0FF accent
  • Static Grey #A8A8C4 muted text
  • Error Green #39FF88 highlight

VSCO Preset Look

internet · 2013–now · also: VSCO filter, Matte film preset, VSCO girl photo

A quiet photo grade that copies film stock: matte blacks, soft contrast, muted greens and warm skin. A preset code such as A6 or HB2 sets it, and the app chrome stays pale and spare.

Core thesis

A preset, not a filter: the edit imitates a real film stock and must look as if no edit happened.

Design principles

  • Lift the black point to 8% to 12% grey and cut contrast by 10% to 20%.
  • Pull saturation to 80% and shift greens toward teal (hue +10°) and skin toward warm beige.
  • Add a grain of 4% to 8% and keep it at 100% view only.
  • Name each preset with a short code of 2 to 3 characters, such as A6, and show it in 11px capitals.
  • Show the preset strip as flat tiles of 72px with a 1px outline, and show the strength slider from 0 to 12.
  • Keep the app chrome in off-white (#F2EFEA) and charcoal (#1F1E1C), with no colour except the photo.
  • Use a wide margin of 24px to 40px around each photo and leave no border on it.

Anti-patterns

  • Strong vignettes
  • Thick white frames
  • Saturated, neon grades
  • Decorative type on the photo
  • Heavy grain at 15% or more
  • Many named filters with bold previews
  • Glossy or 3D chrome

Palette

  • Matte Bone #F2EFEA background
  • Paper White #FFFFFF surface
  • Film Ink #1F1E1C text
  • Film Taupe #8A7B66 primary
  • Faded Teal #6E8B8A accent
  • Warm Highlight #E9D7BE highlight
  • Lifted Shadow #3A3835 shadow

Mir iskusstva book graphics

print · 1899–1930 · also: Bilibin style, World of Art book graphics, Графика Билибина, Мир искусства

A firm, even black pen outline, in the manner of a woodcut, is filled with flat watercolour tints. The key line and the tints print in separate runs. Folk ornament frames each page.

Core thesis

A single firm line and flat tints, set inside an ornamental frame, make a fairy-tale page that reads as a unit with its border and letters.

Design principles

  • Draw the outline with a pen in one even weight of about 0.4–0.6 mm at print size; do not swell or taper it.
  • Fill each area with one flat tint, with no shading; use 3–6 tints from a set of vermilion, Prussian blue, ochre and green.
  • Treat wood, fur, cloth and water as repeated patterns of short strokes inside the contour.
  • Frame each picture with a border of folk ornament, and draw the title letters by hand to match.
  • Draw the picture, the border and the text block as one page.
  • Print the black line as the key and the tints as separate runs; keep the register within 0.5 mm.
  • Use a smooth cream paper that shows the flat colour without a texture.

Anti-patterns

  • Shaded or modelled forms and a gradient
  • A sketchy, loose line of changing weight
  • A picture with no border and no hand lettering
  • Photographic realism
  • A bright white or coated glossy sheet

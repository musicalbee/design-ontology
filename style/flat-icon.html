Flat icon

icon · 2010–present · also: Flat colour icon, Long-shadow icon, Flat design icon

Icons in flat, solid colour shapes with no gradient, no texture and no light model. A simple object sits on a coloured ground, often a circle or a rounded square. Flat icons replaced skeuomorphic icons in the early 2010s.

Core thesis

An icon is a sign, not a picture, and flat shapes in clear colour carry the meaning faster than material and light.

Design principles

  • Build each icon from basic shapes: circles, rectangles and rounded rectangles.
  • Fill each shape with one flat colour; no gradient and no texture.
  • Use 2 to 5 colours in one icon, from a palette of 8 to 12 hues for the set.
  • Use a front or a pure side view; no perspective.
  • Place the object on a coloured circle or rounded square that fills 100% of the grid, with the object at 50–65%.
  • If a shadow is used, make it a flat long shadow at 45° in a 10–20% darker tone of the ground.

Anti-patterns

  • Gradients and glossy highlights
  • Soft drop shadows
  • Texture of leather, wood or metal
  • Perspective views
  • Outlines around every shape

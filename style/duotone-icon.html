Duotone icon

icon · 2019–present · also: Two-tone icon, Bicolour icon

Icons in two tones of one colour: a main shape at full strength and a secondary layer at 20–40% opacity. The second tone gives hierarchy and a little depth, but the icon stays a flat glyph.

Core thesis

One colour in two strengths gives an icon a figure and a ground, so the meaning reads first and the detail second.

Design principles

  • Split each icon into a primary layer and a secondary layer.
  • Set the primary layer at 100% opacity and the secondary layer at 20–40% opacity of the same colour.
  • Make the secondary layer the larger, simpler area: a body, a ground or a fill under the outline.
  • Use the 24 px grid with a 2 px stroke or a solid primary shape; keep the weight of the base set.
  • Keep the two layers aligned; the secondary layer never offsets from the primary.
  • Use one hue for both layers; a second hue only as a deliberate brand choice.

Anti-patterns

  • Two unrelated hues in one icon
  • Secondary layer at more than 50% opacity
  • Gradients between the tones
  • Drop shadows
  • Secondary layer offset from the outline

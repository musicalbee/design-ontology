Apple Aqua

internet · 2000–2006 · also: Aqua, Mac OS X Aqua, Gel UI

Translucent blue gel buttons, pinstripe backgrounds, glossy droplets and brushed-metal windows. It came with Mac OS X in 2000 and made the screen look like water and candy.

Core thesis

The screen is a liquid, tactile surface: every control is a glossy drop that invites a click.

Design principles

  • Make buttons pill-shaped (radius 999px) with a vertical gradient of 2 to 3 stops and a white gloss highlight on the top 45%.
  • Use a pinstripe background of 2px horizontal lines at 4% opacity on #EDEDED.
  • Use Aqua blue (#2A6FB8 to #A8D4FF) as the accent and keep other colours below 20% of the surface.
  • Give each panel an inner highlight (1px white at 80%) and an outer 1px border of #8E8E8E.
  • Set a soft drop shadow of 0 2px 6px at 30% black under windows and buttons.
  • Draw icons as glossy, 3D, high-detail objects at 48px or larger with a reflection.
  • Set text in a humanist sans-serif at 13px to 16px; titles are centred in the title bar.

Anti-patterns

  • Flat fills with no gradient or gloss
  • Sharp rectangular buttons
  • Monochrome icons without volume
  • Dark themes with matt black surfaces
  • Neon colours
  • Hard offset shadows
  • Text on gel with contrast below 4.5:1

Palette

  • Pinstripe #EDEDED background
  • Aqua #2A6FB8 primary
  • Aqua Light #A8D4FF highlight
  • Graphite #2B2F36 text
  • Metal #C9CCD1 surface
  • Droplet Green #6CC24A status

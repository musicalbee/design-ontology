Voxel block world

game · 2009–now · also: Blocky voxel, Block-building look, Cube world

The world is a grid of 1 m cubes, each with a 16×16 pixel texture on its faces. Every house, room and hill is built block by block, with nearest-neighbour pixels and simple face light.

Core thesis

If the world is a grid of equal blocks, anyone can build in it, and the grid itself becomes the style.

Design principles

  • Build every surface from cubes of 1 m on a 3D grid.
  • Texture each face with 16×16 px pixel art, scaled with no filter.
  • Light each face by its direction: top brightest, sides mid, bottom darkest.
  • Keep characters blocky: a body of rectangular boxes.
  • Use clear, saturated material colours: grass green, dirt brown, stone grey.
  • Let builds read as stacked blocks; avoid hiding the grid.

Anti-patterns

  • Smooth curves or bevelled edges
  • High-resolution or filtered textures
  • Objects off the block grid
  • Realistic characters
  • Photographic materials

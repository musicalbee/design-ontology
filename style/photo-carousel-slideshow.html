Photo Carousel Slideshow

internet · 2022–now · also: Photo mode, Swipe carousel, Slide post

A set of 4 to 10 still images that the viewer swipes, with one line of text on each slide and a hook on the first slide. Instagram added carousels in 2017, and TikTok added photo mode in 2022.

Core thesis

Slow the feed down: one idea on each still slide, one swipe at a time.

Design principles

  • Use a 4:5 frame of 1080 x 1350 px or a 9:16 frame of 1080 x 1920 px for every slide.
  • Make slide 1 the hook: a 72 px headline of 6 words or fewer.
  • Keep 1 idea and 1 line of 40 px text on each slide, with 64 px margins.
  • Use 5 to 10 slides and show the count as dots of 8 px at the bottom.
  • Repeat the same grid, colour and type on all slides so the set reads as one.
  • Cue the swipe with an arrow or a cut-off element at the right edge on slide 1.
  • End on a save or share prompt at 44 px with a 100% contrast button.

Anti-patterns

  • A different layout on each slide
  • Paragraph text on a photo
  • No hook on slide 1
  • Low-resolution photos
  • More than 10 slides

Palette

  • Paper #F6F2EA background
  • Ink #1A1A1A text
  • Signal Coral #E4572E primary
  • Sage #7A9E7E accent
  • Stone #6B6B66 muted text
  • Card #FFFFFF surface

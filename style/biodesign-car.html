Biodesign Car

fashion · 1990–2002 · also: Bio-design, Jellybean car, Organic car

The soft, organic car of the 1990s. A rounded one-box body with an oval face, body-colour bumpers and large clear lamps. The cabin is soft cloth and curved plastic.

Core thesis

A car for everyday life has the forms of a plant or an animal. It is round, smooth and friendly, with no hard edge and no sign of a machine.

Design principles

  • Round each corner with a radius of at least 15 cm, and merge the bumpers into the body.
  • Paint the bumpers and the door mirrors in the body colour.
  • Shape the face as an oval or a smile, with large, clear, curved lamps.
  • Raise the roof into a bubble to give a tall, open cabin.
  • Cut the number of body panels, and keep the shut lines thin and even.
  • Shape the dashboard in soft curves, in two-tone plastic, with round vents and a single curved binnacle.
  • Trim the seats with a bright, soft knit or velour in a playful pattern.

Anti-patterns

  • Sharp creases and flat panels
  • Black plastic bumpers and trim
  • A long, low wedge with pop-up lamps
  • Retro chrome and fins
  • A hard, square, angular dashboard

Ravi Varma oleograph

print · c.1894–1960 · also: Oleograph, Calendar art, Bazaar god print, Ravi Varma Press print

A god picture printed by stone lithography in many colours, for the home shrine and the shop wall. The oil-like gloss, the saturated colour and the smooth blend copy the paintings of Raja Ravi Varma.

Core thesis

A lithograph in many colours can copy the smooth oil painting of a god and put it in every home for a few annas.

Design principles

  • Copy a painted original: smooth modelling, a soft light and a figure in the centre.
  • Print 6–12 stones or plates, one for each colour, in transparent inks that blend.
  • Use vermilion, ultramarine, gold yellow and a warm flesh tone as the main inks.
  • Draw a halo, a crown and jewels with a gold-yellow ink and a thin dark line.
  • Put a plain title in Devanagari or English in a strip at the foot of the sheet.
  • Finish the sheet with an oil varnish for a deep gloss.
  • Keep the register within 0.5 mm so that the face and the eyes stay sharp.

Anti-patterns

  • A flat matt colour with no blend
  • A halftone dot that shows at reading distance
  • Rough or crude outline of the woodcut kind
  • A pale pastel palette without a strong red and blue
  • A sketch line with no painted modelling

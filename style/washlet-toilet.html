Washlet toilet

ceramics · 1980 to now · also: Electronic bidet toilet, Warm-water toilet seat, Japanese smart toilet

A vitreous china WC with a heated seat, a warm-water spray nozzle and a control panel. The ceramic is plain and smooth, and the technology lives in the seat and the cistern.

Core thesis

A toilet is a clean machine for the body, so the ceramic stays smooth and the seat adds water, heat and control.

Design principles

  • Cast a smooth bowl with no rim shelf, so that dirt cannot stay under the edge.
  • Glaze the bowl with a hard, dense glaze that has a surface roughness below 0.1 micrometre.
  • Fit a seat that is heated to about 36 °C, with a lid that closes slowly.
  • Hide the nozzle in a cover and wash it with water before and after each use.
  • Place the control panel on the side or on a wall remote, with 4 to 8 simple icons.
  • Use a tornado flush of 4 to 5 litres, so that the bowl cleans with little water.
  • Keep the form compact, 650 to 700 mm long, with one body and one colour.

Anti-patterns

  • A bowl with a hidden rim shelf that holds dirt
  • A seat with a loud pattern or a coloured plastic
  • A thick, rough glaze that marks
  • A bowl with a cord that hangs on the floor
  • An ornate lid with a moulded relief

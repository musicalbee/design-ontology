Garalde old-style serif

typeface · 1495–1750 · also: Old-style serif, Garalde, Aldine, Renaissance antiqua

The roman of the Renaissance book: moderate contrast, a diagonal stress from the broad-nib pen, bracketed serifs and a small x-height. It is the default text face of the long tradition and reads well for hours.

Core thesis

A text letter keeps the rhythm of the hand that wrote it, so the eye reads a page without effort.

Design principles

  • Keep the stroke contrast between 2:1 and 3.5:1, measured on the lower-case o.
  • Tilt the stress of the o, e and c 15–30° to the left of the vertical, as a broad-nib pen does.
  • Give the serifs a curved bracket and a slight cup; the head serifs of b, d, h, k and l slope down to the left.
  • Hold the x-height at 0.58–0.70 of the cap height, so the ascenders rise clearly above the x-height.
  • Set the capitals at the classical proportions: a wide O, C, D and N, and a narrow E, F, L and S.
  • Use old-style figures (with ascenders and descenders) in running text.

Anti-patterns

  • A vertical stress with hairlines under 0.3 of the stem
  • Unbracketed, square slab serifs
  • A large x-height that crowds the ascenders
  • Tight, negative tracking in text sizes
  • Lining figures in the middle of a paragraph
  • Fake bold or fake italic made by the software

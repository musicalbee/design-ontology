Logomania

fashion · 1985–2005 · also: Designer logo fashion, Monogram bags and belts

Accessories that carry a brand mark on the whole surface. Examples are a monogram repeat on coated canvas, large metal letters on a belt and a logo on a scarf. The mark is the design, and it shows status.

Core thesis

The mark of the maker is the ornament, and the wearer shows a place in the world by it.

Design principles

  • Repeat the mark across the whole surface in rows, 2 to 4 cm tall.
  • Print or weave the mark in a tone that contrasts with the ground.
  • Use a coated canvas that shows the print and wears well.
  • Set a large metal letter or a double mark as a buckle or a clasp, 4 to 8 cm wide.
  • Carry the mark from the bag to the belt, the wallet and the scarf, so that the set matches.
  • Keep the leather trim in a plain tan or black to frame the print.
  • Place the mark so that it reads at a distance of 5 m.
  • Make a copy, a remix or a custom piece in the same logic, in the street.

Anti-patterns

  • A plain unmarked leather bag
  • A small, hidden logo on the inside
  • A quiet monochrome palette
  • Natural linen and hand-sewn edges
  • Random, unrelated prints

Liminal spaces

internet · 2019–now · also: Liminal space, The Backrooms look, Empty places

Empty rooms of transit and routine: office corridors, pools, car parks and malls under flat light, with no people. A 2019 image of yellow rooms started the look, and the Backrooms spread it.

Core thesis

An ordinary place with no people and no purpose feels strange: the viewer meets the unease of a threshold.

Design principles

  • Set the ground in a damp yellow (#C9B96B) or a dull carpet green with a slight grey cast.
  • Shoot or render empty rooms from eye height at 160cm, with a single vanishing point at the centre.
  • Light the room with flat fluorescent light and no strong shadow; the white point sits at 4000K to 4500K.
  • Add a low-grade capture look: JPEG blocks, a 4:3 crop, a 480p to 720p size, a timestamp in mono at 14px.
  • Repeat structure: tiled ceilings of 60cm cells, doors and corridors in rows of 6 or more.
  • Keep text rare and plain: a room code, a date or a sign in a default sans at 14px to 18px.
  • Leave 50% or more of the frame as empty floor and wall.

Anti-patterns

  • People and faces in the frame
  • Warm, cosy lighting
  • Bright saturated colour
  • Decorative type and ornament
  • Dramatic perspective and camera moves
  • Clean, high-resolution gloss

Palette

  • Wallpaper Yellow #C9B96B background
  • Ceiling White #EDEBDD surface
  • Carpet Olive #6F6A3C primary
  • Pool Teal #6FA8A3 accent
  • Corridor Grey #2A2A28 text
  • Damp Brown #8A7A4A border
  • Mid Grey #4B4A42 muted text

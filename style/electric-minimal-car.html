Electric Minimal Car

fashion · 2012–now · also: Electric car design, Minimal EV, Software-defined car

The quiet electric car. A smooth, closed body with no grille, flush handles and a glass roof. The cabin is bare, in recycled cloth, with one large screen in place of dials.

Core thesis

An electric car has no engine to show. It is a smooth, quiet shell with a bare cabin, and a screen holds all of the controls.

Design principles

  • Close the grille, and mark the face by a thin lamp line or a smooth panel.
  • Make the surface smooth, with a drag coefficient below 0.28, and fit flush door handles.
  • Raise a low, wide stance, with large wheels of 19 to 21 inches pushed to the corners.
  • Use a glass roof that extends over the cabin, so that the cabin is full of light.
  • Keep the cabin bare, with one large screen and no physical dials, and a few clean surfaces.
  • Trim the seats in recycled knit, felt or non-leather material, in two or three calm colours.
  • Use thin lamps in a single line, front and rear, and a plain badge.

Anti-patterns

  • A big chrome grille
  • Fake wood and gloss black trim
  • A dashboard of many physical switches
  • Fake engine sounds and vents
  • Retro details and fins

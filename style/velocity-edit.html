Velocity Edit

internet · 2020–now · also: Velocity edit, Speed-ramp edit, CapCut velocity

A short vertical montage with speed ramps: the clip slows to slow motion, then jumps to fast play on the beat, with glow, zoom shake and a colour grade. Editors make it with the velocity tool of CapCut.

Core thesis

Let time bend to the music: slow on the strong beat, fast on the rest.

Design principles

  • Ramp the speed between 20% and 400%; slow to 20% to 30% on the accent beat and jump to 300% on the off-beat.
  • Cut every 0.3 s to 1.2 s on the beat of a 120 BPM to 140 BPM track.
  • Add a zoom of 100% to 115% in 6 frames at each cut.
  • Add a glow with a 20 px blur at 30% opacity on highlights.
  • Use a high-contrast grade: crush blacks to 5% and lift highlights to 95%.
  • Overlay a 2 px shake of 4 frames on each strong beat.
  • Put one short word in 56 px type on the final hit, no more.

Anti-patterns

  • Constant speed through the clip
  • Cuts that miss the beat
  • Flat grey colour grade
  • Long caption text
  • Hard black bars at the sides

Palette

  • Void #08090C background
  • Hit Orange #FF6A1F primary
  • Cold Blue #2BB3FF accent
  • Highlight #FFF3E0 text
  • Steel #9AA3B2 muted text
  • Deep Panel #14161C surface

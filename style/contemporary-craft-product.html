Contemporary craft product

industrial · 2000–present · also: Quiet luxury objects, Japandi objects, Slow craft objects

The handmade object of the quiet home: stoneware with a speckled glaze, solid oak with an oiled finish, washed linen and unlacquered brass, made in small batches and left with the marks of the hand.

Core thesis

A good object is made by a person from one real material, and it gains value with use: its marks, its tone and its slow wear are the ornament.

Design principles

  • Use a solid material, not a veneer: oak 25 to 50 mm thick, stoneware with a wall of 5 to 8 mm.
  • Finish with oil, soap, wax or a matte glaze; keep the gloss below 15 GU.
  • Leave one mark of the maker on each object: a throwing ring, a plane mark or a hammer facet.
  • Make each form from a simple shape with slightly uneven edges and a soft radius of 3 to 10 mm.
  • Keep to a palette of 4 or 5 warm neutrals: oat, sand, clay, bone and a soft black.
  • Use brass without lacquer, so that it darkens with the hand and the years.
  • Make in batches of 1 to 100 pieces and sign the base or the back.

Anti-patterns

  • High-gloss plastic and chrome
  • Perfect machine symmetry
  • Saturated colour and loud pattern
  • Applied ornament and gilding
  • Synthetic veneer that copies wood
  • Logos and visible fixings on main faces

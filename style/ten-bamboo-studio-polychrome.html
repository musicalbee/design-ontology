Ten Bamboo Studio polychrome block print

print · c.1619–1644 · also: Shizhuzhai, 十竹斋, Doubanh jigsaw printing, 饾版, Gonghua embossing, 拱花

Hu Zhengyan's workshop cut many small blocks for one picture (douban) and printed them in turn on one sheet. Dry blocks pressed the paper (gonghua) and raised the outline of a petal or a cloud.

Core thesis

A page of a painting manual can carry the soft colour of a scroll when the printer splits the picture into small blocks that fit like a jigsaw.

Design principles

  • Split each picture by colour into small blocks, up to dozens for one page, and print them one by one on the same sheet.
  • Grade the ink on one block (a wet-brush effect) so one petal or leaf shades from pale to deep.
  • Print the key outline in light ink, with a thin line, and let the colour carry the form.
  • Press a dry block into the sheet (gonghua) to raise the outline of a petal, a cloud or a wave, with no ink on it.
  • Choose a quiet subject of the literati: a plum, bamboo, a rock, a bird or an orchid.
  • Use pale, clear colours: rose, moss green, water blue and ink grey.
  • Add a short poem in calligraphy and a red seal.

Anti-patterns

  • A heavy black outline in the manner of a folk print
  • Poster colours that are loud and opaque
  • Halftone dots or a photograph
  • A large flat area of one colour with no gradation
  • Three-dimensional shading

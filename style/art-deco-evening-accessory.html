Art Deco evening accessory

fashion · 1925–1939 · also: Deco minaudière and evening bag, 1920s–30s vanity case

The small, hard, bright accessories of evening dress between the wars. They include enamelled and engraved cases, the minaudière, beaded satin pouches and long kid gloves, in geometric forms of black, silver and a jewel colour.

Core thesis

An evening accessory is a jewel that holds a powder compact, and its geometry shows modern taste.

Design principles

  • Cut the form in a clean geometry: a rectangle, a circle or a stepped edge.
  • Keep the case small, 12 to 20 cm wide, so that the hand holds it as a jewel.
  • Set a bold contrast: black enamel with silver, or jade green with gold.
  • Fit the inside with a mirror, a powder compact and a lipstick holder.
  • Repeat angular motifs in rows: a sunburst, a chevron or a stepped line.
  • Finish the clasp with a row of small stones or a bar of enamel.
  • Use a chain or a cord of 20 to 40 cm so that the case is carried in the hand or on the wrist.
  • Pair the case with long kid gloves and a satin dress.

Anti-patterns

  • A soft slouchy bag with no frame
  • Floral Art Nouveau curves
  • Bright plastic with moulded seams
  • A large daytime tote
  • Fringe and loose beads that hang unevenly

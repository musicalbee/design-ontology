Clean girl

internet · 2022–now · also: Clean girl aesthetic, Glazed skin look

A look of dewy skin, a sleek bun, gold hoops, neutral clothes, white tiles and tidy shelves. It grew on TikTok and Instagram in 2021 and 2022 and sells wellness, skin and routine.

Core thesis

Show effortless order: a polished, neutral surface suggests a calm life, and the routine is the product.

Design principles

  • Set the ground in warm white (#FAF7F2) with beige (#E9DFD1) and a soft taupe (#9C8B7A) as support.
  • Use one gold accent (#C8A25B) for hoops, rules and small marks, under 5% of the surface.
  • Pair a clean geometric sans with one fine display serif or italic at 40px to 96px.
  • Photograph skin, hands and objects in soft window light with a 3% bloom and a visible sheen on highlights.
  • Frame products on white tile, marble or linen, in rows of 3 with 24px gaps; keep a 4:5 crop.
  • Show routines as numbered steps: 01 Cleanse, 02 Hydrate, 03 Glow, each in a pill of 999px radius.
  • Round all corners at 24px or more and use shadows at 6% opacity only.

Anti-patterns

  • Heavy makeup and loud colour
  • Dark, moody grading
  • Busy collage and stickers
  • Rough, grungy textures
  • Hard shadows and outlines
  • Cluttered shelves

Palette

  • Warm White #FAF7F2 background
  • Cream Tile #FFFFFF surface
  • Taupe Ink #3A332C text
  • Soft Taupe #9C8B7A border
  • Gold #C8A25B accent
  • Beige #E9DFD1 primary
  • Stone #6B6055 muted text

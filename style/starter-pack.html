Starter pack

internet · 2014–2019 · also: Starter pack meme, Identity collage

A white grid collage that lists the objects, clothes and habits of one type of person around a cut-out figure. It grew from Tumblr and Twitter in 2014 and became a general template for identity jokes.

Core thesis

Describe a type of person as a kit of things you can buy, wear and say.

Design principles

  • Divide a white canvas of 600px x 750px into a 3 x 3 grid of product cut-outs with 12px gutters.
  • Place a figure or a name label in the centre cell and put 5 to 8 object photos around it.
  • Cut each object out with a clean edge on a white ground, with no shadow.
  • Add a bold title at the top: "<Type> starter pack" in 40px black type.
  • Add a 14px caption under each object, in sentence case, with a brand or a habit.
  • Keep the object sizes within 20% of each other; the figure may be 1.5 times larger.
  • Use plain white for the ground and keep all photos in their own colours.

Anti-patterns

  • Dark backgrounds
  • Soft overlapping layouts with no grid
  • Handwritten decorations
  • Real private persons as the figure
  • Abstract objects with no real product
  • Long paragraphs

Palette

  • Canvas White #FFFFFF background
  • Title Black #111111 text
  • Caption Grey #555555 secondary text
  • Product Pink #F4B6C2 sample object tone
  • Product Blue #4C8BF5 sample object tone

Streetwear

fashion · 1980–present · also: Street style, Skate and surf wear, Hype wear, Urban casual

The casual dress that grew from surf, skate, hip-hop and sport clubs from 1980. It has the graphic T-shirt, the hoodie, the baggy jean, the nylon trouser and the cap, sold in small drops.

Core thesis

Dress comes from the street and the club: the wearer joins a group by a plain, comfortable garment that carries a mark.

Design principles

  • Cut the T-shirt and the hoodie boxy and 5 to 10 cm wider than the body.
  • Print a mark, a name or a graphic on the chest in one to four flat colours by screen print.
  • Choose heavy cotton jersey of 200 to 300 g/m² and brushed-back loopback terry for the hoodie.
  • Cut the jean or the trouser wide and straight, and let the hem break on the shoe.
  • Add one sport or work element: a nylon panel, a zip pocket or a drawcord.
  • Release the piece in a small run, so that few people own it.
  • Pair the garment with a cap and a sneaker.

Anti-patterns

  • Sharp tailoring and a pressed crease
  • Formal silk and satin
  • Delicate lace and frills
  • Tight, fitted sheath cuts
  • Fine, fragile cloth that cannot be washed
  • Quiet, unmarked luxury

Vector display arcade

game · 1979–1985 · also: Vector graphics arcade, Glowing line arcade

The beam of the tube draws lines, not pixels. Bright monochrome or colour outlines glow on pure black, with no fill, no texture and a world built from wire shapes.

Core thesis

A world can be drawn with light alone: a few sharp glowing lines on black are enough for the eye to build space.

Design principles

  • Draw every object as an outline of straight line segments; use no filled area.
  • Keep the ground pure black; it is the space between the lines.
  • Make the lines bright and let them bloom by 1–3 px into the dark.
  • Use 1 to 4 colours of full saturation, one for each class of object.
  • Show depth with perspective wireframes and a vanishing point, not with shading.
  • Keep each shape under about 30 segments so it reads at a glance.

Anti-patterns

  • Filled or textured surfaces
  • A grey or coloured background
  • Soft gradients or painted shading
  • Dense detail that the beam cannot draw in one frame
  • Pixel steps on a diagonal line

Word Caption Clip

internet · 2020–now · also: Karaoke captions, Hormozi captions, Animated subtitles

A talking head in a vertical frame, with the speech shown as huge captions, one to three words at a time. Each word pops in at the beat, and the current word takes a bright colour.

Core thesis

Make every word visible at the moment it is spoken: the caption is the main picture, and the viewer reads and hears the same beat.

Design principles

  • Set captions at 72px to 110px on a 1080x1920 frame, with 1 to 3 words on screen at a time.
  • Place the caption block at 55% to 65% of the frame height, centred, inside a 64px safe margin.
  • Colour the spoken word in yellow (#FFE600) or green (#3DFF7A) and keep the other words white.
  • Add a 6px black outline or a 4px hard shadow to each word.
  • Pop each word in at 90% to 110% scale over 6 to 8 frames at 30 fps.
  • Time each word to the audio within 2 frames.
  • Use a bold, heavy sans in capitals and one emoji or icon per sentence at most.

Anti-patterns

  • Full sentences of 12 words on screen
  • Thin, serif or script type
  • Captions in the lower 15% of the frame, under the app buttons
  • Low-contrast captions with no outline
  • Subtitles that lag the voice by more than 4 frames
  • Many colours in one caption line

Palette

  • Frame Black #0A0A0A background
  • Footage Grey #1C1C1E surface
  • Word White #FFFFFF text
  • Active Yellow #FFE600 primary
  • Active Green #3DFF7A accent
  • Outline Black #000000 border

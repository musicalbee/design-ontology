Stealth luxury accessory

fashion · 2006–now · also: Quiet luxury accessory, Unmarked fine leather goods

Plain, unmarked accessories of the finest leather and cashmere: a tote with no logo, a slim wallet, a raw-edged scarf. Only those who know the cloth, the cut and the stitch can read the price.

Core thesis

True luxury has no mark: the quality of the material and the work is the only sign.

Design principles

  • Remove every logo, plate and print from the outside.
  • Choose the best hide or the finest cashmere that the budget allows.
  • Cut a simple shape with 1 or 2 seams.
  • Keep the palette to 4 to 6 neutral tones: camel, oatmeal, charcoal, navy and black.
  • Stitch by hand at 8 to 10 stitches to the cm, in a thread that matches the leather.
  • Leave the edge raw, folded or painted by hand, and not covered by a trim.
  • Use hardware in plain brass or steel with a matte finish and no engraving.
  • Make the item to last 20 years, and offer repair.

Anti-patterns

  • A logo or a monogram on the outside
  • Bright colours and bold prints
  • Shiny gilt hardware
  • Beads, studs and fringe
  • A bag named after a season

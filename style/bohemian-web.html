Bohemian Web

internet · 2012–now · also: Boho, Boho chic web, Earthy folk web

Earth tones, hand-drawn florals, macramé and woven patterns, arched shapes, warm photography and relaxed script type. It shows a free-spirited, natural and craft-based taste.

Core thesis

The page is a lived-in, free-spirited space: natural, layered and warm.

Design principles

  • Build the palette from 4 to 6 earth tones (terracotta, sand, sage, mustard, plum) with saturation of 25% to 55%.
  • Use arches as the main shape: image masks with a top radius of 999px and a bottom radius of 0 to 24px.
  • Layer 2 to 4 pattern fills (floral, macramé, ikat, dots) at 8% to 20% opacity behind sections.
  • Pair a warm serif display at 48px to 88px with a script accent at 28px to 40px and a humanist sans at 16px.
  • Set photography warm (colour temperature +10% to +20%) with grain of 3% to 6% and natural light.
  • Add organic dividers: a wavy line of 2px to 3px or a scalloped edge of 16px to 24px.
  • Keep corners soft: radius of 16px to 32px on cards and 999px on tags.

Anti-patterns

  • Cold blue and grey palettes
  • Sharp corners and heavy black borders
  • Neon and glow
  • Strict symmetric grids
  • Cold stock photography
  • Tech and HUD details
  • Pure white backgrounds

Palette

  • Sand #F5EBDD background
  • Clay #B84A33 primary
  • Sage #8A9A7B accent
  • Mustard #E1A730 highlight
  • Bark #3D2B24 text
  • Dusk #6F5B52 secondary text

Industrial loft

interior · 1970–present · also: Industrial interior, Loft style, Warehouse conversion

The converted factory floor. Exposed brick, concrete, steel columns and ducts stay in view; large steel windows light one open plan, and salvaged furniture of wood, leather and steel stands in zones on a bare floor.

Core thesis

The building tells of its past as a factory; leave the structure and the services in view and live among them.

Design principles

  • Show the structure and the services: brick, concrete, columns, beams, ducts and conduit.
  • Keep one open plan; mark zones with rugs and furniture, not walls.
  • Use large steel-framed windows and keep them bare.
  • Mix raw materials with warm ones: steel and concrete with old wood and worn leather.
  • Choose salvaged or factory pieces: metal stools, cabinets, pendant lamps.
  • Keep the palette in the colours of the materials: brick red, concrete grey, black steel and wood brown.

Anti-patterns

  • Plastered and painted-over brick and beams
  • Ornate or delicate furniture
  • Wall-to-wall carpet
  • Small rooms that divide the floor
  • Pastel colour schemes

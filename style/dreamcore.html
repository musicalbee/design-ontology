Dreamcore

internet · 2019–now · also: Dream core, Dream aesthetic

Soft, hazy images of childhood rooms, pastel skies, old toys and stairways, with a faint glow and blurry text. It looks like a half-remembered dream and grew on Tumblr, Reddit and video sites.

Core thesis

Show a memory as a dream does: soft, glowing and slightly wrong, with the details out of focus.

Design principles

  • Set the ground in pastel gradient from #FBE3F0 to #CFE3FF at 180°.
  • Apply a bloom of 12px to 24px on all light areas and lower the contrast by 15% to 25%.
  • Use one soft focal image (a room, a toy or a sky) at 50% to 70% of the frame.
  • Add one odd detail: a door in a field, a stair with no top or a repeated window.
  • Set text in a thin serif or a rounded sans at 14px to 28px, in 60% opacity white or lilac.
  • Add grain at 4% to 6% and a vignette of 15%.
  • Keep motion slow: loops of 8s to 20s with cross-fades of 1.2s.

Anti-patterns

  • Hard black voids and red warning text
  • Sharp, high-contrast photography
  • Dense data labels
  • Neon colours
  • Hard grid layouts
  • Bold, loud display type
  • Glossy 3D effects

Palette

  • Dream Haze #FBE3F0 background
  • Sky Wash #CFE3FF surface
  • Soft Lilac #B9A5E8 primary
  • Warm Glow #FFD9A0 accent
  • Faded Ink #4B3F72 text
  • Pale Mint #D4F1E4 second accent

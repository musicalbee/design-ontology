Rams Functionalism (UI)

graphic movement · 1955–now · also: Braun design, Less but better, Ten principles of good design

Quiet matt surfaces, a muted grey palette, one orange signal colour and controls that show what they do. It follows Dieter Rams and Braun and appears again in calculators, audio and phone interfaces.

Core thesis

Good design is as little design as possible: the product should be useful, understandable and honest, and then step back.

Design principles

  • Limit the palette to 3 neutrals (warm greys) and one signal colour (orange #E8590C) used on at most 5% of the surface.
  • Group controls in labelled zones; each control has a text label of 12px to 14px and a fixed place on a 4px grid.
  • Give each control one job and make its state visible: use a solid fill for on and an outline for off.
  • Use radius 2px to 4px on buttons and panels; radius never exceeds 8px.
  • Set all type in one neo-grotesque sans serif at 4 sizes: 12, 14, 18 and 32px.
  • Arrange panels in a modular layout of equal units of 80px to 120px; align each edge to a unit.
  • Colour only the thing that signals: a state, a warning or a primary action.

Anti-patterns

  • Decorative gradients, glows and glass effects
  • Two or more saturated accent colours
  • Icons without a text label on a primary action
  • Skeuomorphic knobs with fake materials
  • Rounded containers with a radius above 8px
  • Ornamental type and display faces
  • Animation that does not show a state change

Palette

  • Fog #E9E7E2 background
  • Graphite #2B2B2A text
  • Stone #D5D2CB surface
  • Smoke #6B6A66 secondary text
  • Signal Orange #E8590C accent
  • Off White #F6F5F1 raised surface

Lineage

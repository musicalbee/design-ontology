Mini-Diffusion Meme

internet · 2022–2023 · also: DALL-E mini meme, Craiyon grid, Smudge grid

A 3x3 grid of small, smudged images that answer a silly text prompt, with warped faces and soft shapes. It went viral in 2022 from a free, low-resolution image model.

Core thesis

The joke is the gap between a plain prompt and a soft, wrong picture; the smudge is the format.

Design principles

  • Arrange 9 images in a 3x3 grid of 256px tiles with a 4px gap and no gutter.
  • Let each tile show a blur of 1px to 2px and a visible JPEG block pattern at 8px blocks.
  • Warp one feature in each image: a face, a hand or a sign with false letters.
  • Put the prompt as the caption above the grid in 20px mono, in lower case, inside quotation marks.
  • Set a 1px dark frame around the grid and a plain cream ground (#F4EFE6).
  • Use a colour range of muddy brown, green and a pale sky; avoid pure white and pure black in tiles.
  • Show a small orange button, such as Run, below the grid at 40px height.

Anti-patterns

  • Sharp, high-resolution renders
  • Glossy cinematic light
  • Single image output
  • Clean, accurate faces
  • Dark moody grading
  • Dense layout ornament

Palette

  • Paper Cream #F4EFE6 background
  • Tile Paper #E8E0D0 surface
  • Prompt Black #1E1E1E text
  • Run Orange #E5731A primary
  • Murky Green #5E7A52 accent
  • Smudge Brown #8B6B4E image reference
  • Sky Blur #9DB7D5 image reference

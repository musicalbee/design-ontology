Tweet screenshot meme

internet · 2015–now · also: Screenshot post meme, Post card joke

A cropped screenshot of a short social post in its own interface: avatar, name, text and a row of counters. The joke lives in the words, and the crop and the interface give it proof and tone.

Core thesis

Use the plain look of a social post as a frame, so that the text reads as a real voice.

Design principles

  • Crop the screenshot to the post card at a width of 550px to 600px with 16px of side padding.
  • Show an avatar of 48px (circle), a bold name and a grey handle above the text.
  • Set the post text in a system sans-serif at 15px to 17px with a line height of 1.35.
  • Keep a row of 4 counters (reply, repost, like, share) in 13px grey type under the text.
  • Use a white card on light mode or a #15202B card on dim mode; do not mix them.
  • Add a thin 1px border or leave the edge raw; crop with a few pixels of the next post visible.
  • Keep the screenshot slightly compressed, as the original; do not sharpen it.

Anti-patterns

  • Fancy fonts in the post text
  • Added decoration around the card
  • A cropped avatar with no name
  • Perfect, uncompressed rendering of a fake card
  • Bright custom backgrounds
  • Long threads in one image

Palette

  • Card White #FFFFFF background
  • Post Text #0F1419 text
  • Meta Grey #536471 secondary text
  • Link Blue #1D9BF0 accent
  • Dim Card #15202B dark background
  • Dim Text #E7E9EA dark text

Cinematic FPV

film · 2019–today · also: FPV drone, Drone dive, Cinewhoop and freestyle FPV

One continuous flight through a place: a small drone with an ultra-wide camera dives, banks and threads gaps at 15 to 35 m/s. The camera moves like a bird, and the cut hides inside a wipe.

Core thesis

A camera that can go anywhere should not cut: one unbroken flight turns space itself into the story.

Design principles

  • Fly one continuous shot of 20 to 60 s and hide each cut in a wipe: pass behind a pillar, a tree or a wall at 0 to 2 frames of black.
  • Shoot at 4K 60 fps with a 180° shutter (1/120 s) and conform to 24 or 30 fps for a slow-motion drift of 40% to 50% speed in the slow parts.
  • Use an ultra-wide lens of 150° to 170° field of view (about 14mm equivalent) and hold a subject at 3 to 10 m from the lens for scale.
  • Fly at 15 to 35 m/s on a dive and at 3 to 8 m/s for a reveal, and keep the camera uptilt at 25° to 45°.
  • Fit an ND filter of ND8 to ND16 in daylight so that the shutter stays at 180°.
  • Shoot in a flat log profile and grade with one LUT at a gain of 60% to 80%, saturation +10%.
  • Match each speed change to the music on a beat of 90 to 130 bpm and mix at −14 LUFS.

Anti-patterns

  • A cut every 2 s on a rapid-fire montage
  • A slow, high-altitude orbit at 5 m/s
  • A shaky, unstabilised shot with a rolling-shutter wobble
  • A shutter faster than 1/500 s that makes the flight look stuttered
  • A flat, eye-level pan with no change of altitude
  • A flight that ignores the subject and only shows the sky
  • A clip with the on-screen display left in

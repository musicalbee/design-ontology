HD-2D diorama

game · 2018–now · also: HD-2D, Pixel diorama, Tilt-shift pixel 3D

Pixel-art sprites stand in a 3D world built like a miniature model, seen from a high camera with strong depth of field, bloom and modern light. The world looks like a toy diorama lit at golden hour.

Core thesis

Old pixel sprites inside modern light and depth of field read as a lovingly built miniature.

Design principles

  • Keep characters as 2D pixel sprites of 32–64 px.
  • Build the world in 3D with pixel-art textures on simple geometry.
  • Use a high, slightly tilted camera and strong depth of field: sharp middle, blurred top and bottom.
  • Light with modern dynamic light: lamps, bloom and soft shadows.
  • Add particles: dust, embers, light shafts.
  • Grade toward warm golden hours or deep blue nights.

Anti-patterns

  • A flat 2D view with no depth
  • High-poly characters
  • Sharp focus across the whole frame
  • Flat even light
  • Photo textures

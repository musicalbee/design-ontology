Hyperpop Visual

internet · 2019–now · also: Hyperpop aesthetic, Hyperpop

Shiny chrome, candy colours, cute 3D shapes, Web 2.0 gloss and crowded collage, with pixel type and stickers. It links the PC Music look to a 2019 playlist and to short-video culture.

Core thesis

Make pop too sweet and too bright: take cute and chrome so far that they look artificial and strange.

Design principles

  • Set a pastel gradient ground from #FFC8F0 to #BDF0FF at 160°.
  • Place one chrome or glossy 3D object at 40% to 60% of the frame, with a hard white highlight.
  • Layer 4 to 8 stickers, stars and Web 2.0 buttons at tilts of -20° to 20°.
  • Set display text in a bold, round or pixel face at 48px to 120px, with a 2px dark outline.
  • Use hot pink (#FF2FB3) and electric blue (#2F6BFF) on at least 20% of the surface.
  • Add a gloss band of 40% height on buttons and panels, in white at 40% opacity.
  • Cut video edits every 6 to 15 frames with zooms and flash frames.

Anti-patterns

  • Muted, desaturated palettes
  • Minimal, quiet layouts
  • Realistic natural materials
  • Serious, formal serif type
  • Slow, long takes
  • Hand-drawn rough texture
  • Dark, moody lighting

Palette

  • Cotton Pink #FFC8F0 background
  • Hot Pink #FF2FB3 primary
  • Electric Blue #2F6BFF accent
  • Ice Blue #BDF0FF surface
  • Chrome Silver #D8DCE8 second accent
  • Spark Yellow #FFF04D highlight
  • Ink Navy #1A1033 text

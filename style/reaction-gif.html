Reaction GIF

internet · 2009–now · also: Reaction clip, Captioned GIF loop, Giphy loop

A short looped clip from a film, a show or a sport, sent in place of a sentence. The frame is small, grainy and often carries a one-line caption in heavy white capitals.

Core thesis

A looped second of a real face says a feeling faster than a sentence, so the clip is the reply.

Design principles

  • Loop a clip of 1 to 4 seconds at 10 to 15 fps with a seamless cut from the last frame to the first.
  • Export the frame at 480px wide or less in 256 colours, and let dithering and grain stay visible.
  • Set a caption in heavy white capitals with a 2px black outline, at 8% to 12% of the frame height.
  • Place the caption on the lower third of the frame, centred, with 4% margin from the edge.
  • Crop tight on one face or one gesture, so that the subject fills 50% to 70% of the frame.
  • Frame the clip in a 1px white outline on a dark card of #22222C when it shows inside a feed.
  • Show the search tag, for example #facepalm, in 12px capitals under the clip.

Anti-patterns

  • Smooth 60 fps video with no dither
  • Long clips with a visible cut point
  • Thin captions with no outline
  • Wide landscape frames with a small subject
  • Clean studio gradients behind the clip
  • Several captions in one frame

Palette

  • Feed Black #14141A background
  • Card Grey #22222C surface
  • Caption White #FFFFFF caption and text
  • Search Yellow #FFD60A primary
  • Link Blue #00C2FF accent
  • Dust Grey #B8B8C8 muted text

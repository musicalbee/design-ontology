VTuber Avatar

internet · 2016–now · also: Virtual YouTuber, VTuber, Live2D streamer

A drawn anime-style character takes the place of the host. A face tracker moves the eyes, mouth and head in real time. Pastel panels, sparkle and cute rounded frames hold the scene.

Core thesis

The host is a character: a tracked avatar gives a person a face that is safe, stable and designed, and the frame is dressed to match.

Design principles

  • Place the avatar at 40% to 55% of the frame height, centred or at one third of the width.
  • Track blinks, mouth shapes and head tilt at 30 fps or more, with a lag under 100ms.
  • Draw the avatar with flat colour, a 3px dark outline and 2 to 3 steps of cel shading.
  • Frame the scene with rounded panels of 24px radius and a 4px white edge.
  • Use a pastel gradient ground (for example #FFD6EC to #D6E4FF at 160°).
  • Add sparkle marks of 12px to 24px and soft stars at 3 to 6 points in the frame.
  • Show chat comments as speech bubbles of 16px radius with a 14px text size.

Anti-patterns

  • Photographic human faces
  • Dark, hard techno panels
  • Realistic 3D with no outline
  • Gritty textures and film grain
  • Dense data bars
  • Sharp, square corners

Palette

  • Cloud Lavender #F4EEFF background
  • Bubble White #FFFFFF surface
  • Plum Ink #2E1E4A text
  • Sakura Pink #FF8FC7 primary
  • Sky Blue #7CC4FF accent
  • Star Gold #FFD65C highlight

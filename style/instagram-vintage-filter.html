Instagram Vintage Filter

internet · 2010–now · also: Filter look, Valencia and X-Pro II, Square retro photo

A phone photo in a square 1:1 frame with a one-tap filter: warm fade, lifted blacks, a dark vignette and a thick white border. It made a phone snapshot look like an old print.

Core thesis

One tap turns a plain phone snapshot into a keepsake: the filter, the frame and the vignette supply the age that the photo does not have.

Design principles

  • Crop every photo to a 1:1 square and show it at full width, 640px on the first apps.
  • Fade the blacks to 10% to 15% grey and push the highlights toward amber (#E8B866) at 20% to 30% strength.
  • Add a vignette: a radial dark edge from 55% radius out to 100%, at 35% to 55% opacity.
  • Put a white border of 24px to 40px around the photo, with a thicker bottom edge of 64px for the caption.
  • Offer 15 to 18 named filters in a strip of 80px thumbnails, each with a one-word name in capitals.
  • Set the caption in a plain grotesque at 14px with the user name in bold, and keep the chrome flat.
  • Show a 1px hairline between the photo cards in the feed and a 44px tab bar of 5 icons.

Anti-patterns

  • Rectangular 16:9 or 3:4 crops
  • Cool, clean, neutral colour with no fade
  • A thin edge with no frame
  • Many filters stacked on one photo
  • Hard black shadows with no lift
  • Large decorative display type on the photo
  • A glossy 3D app chrome

Palette

  • Print Cream #FBF6EA surface
  • Frame Paper #EFE6D3 background
  • Sepia Ink #2B2118 text
  • Amber Wash #E0A04A highlight
  • Rust #C8782E primary
  • Faded Teal #5F8F94 accent
  • Vignette Brown #4A3524 shadow

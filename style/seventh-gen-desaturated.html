Seventh-generation desaturated bloom

game · 2005–2013 · also: Brown and grey era, Gritty HD, Bloom and brown

The first HD consoles push normal maps, bloom and HDR light, and art direction answers with grey, brown and teal: desaturated ruins, heavy contrast, glowing windows and wet, specular surfaces.

Core thesis

Realism is grit: a serious world is grey, worn and wet, and light blooms out of the dark.

Design principles

  • Desaturate the palette to browns, greys and a teal or a sepia grade.
  • Use normal maps on every surface for cracks, bolts and grime.
  • Let bright areas bloom: windows and sky glow past their edges.
  • Make surfaces wet and specular, with sharp highlights.
  • Use high contrast: crushed shadows and blown highlights.
  • Fill spaces with debris, cover and ruins.

Anti-patterns

  • Saturated, cheerful colour
  • Flat or drawn shading
  • Clean, empty surfaces
  • Soft, even light
  • Stylised proportions

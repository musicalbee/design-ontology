Deep-fried meme

internet · 2017–now · also: Deep fried, Fried meme, Crunchy meme

A meme picture pushed through many rounds of saving, sharpening and colour boost until it shows heavy JPEG blocks, burnt reds and yellows, lens flares and glowing emoji. It spread on Reddit and Tumblr in 2017.

Core thesis

Show the damage as the joke: a picture so copied and boosted that its own decay is the style.

Design principles

  • Recompress the image to JPEG quality 5 to 15, 3 to 10 times, with a resize of 50% and 200% between rounds.
  • Raise saturation by 150% to 300% and contrast by 40% to 80% until the reds burn.
  • Sharpen by 150% so that noise shows round every edge.
  • Add lens flare, glowing eyes or a laser flare in red (#FF1A1A) and yellow (#FFE000).
  • Add laugh-cry or 100 emoji at 10% to 20% of the canvas and a caption in outlined Impact-style capitals.
  • Add a 2px to 4px outer glow in orange on the text.
  • Keep the subject readable: the face or the object stays recognisable at 25% size.

Anti-patterns

  • Clean, sharp, modern grading
  • Subtle, even tones
  • Neat, aligned caption
  • Lossless export of a fresh photo
  • Soft pastel colours
  • Wide empty margins

Palette

  • Burnt Red #FF1A1A accent
  • Fry Yellow #FFE000 highlight
  • Hot Orange #FF7A00 glow
  • Crushed Black #0A0A0A background
  • Flash White #FFFFFF text
  • Cyan Noise #00E5FF noise

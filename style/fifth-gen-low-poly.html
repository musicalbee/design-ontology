Fifth-generation low poly

game · 1994–2001 · also: PS1 look, N64 look, 32/64-bit 3D

Real-time 3D at about 320×240: characters of a few hundred polygons, small textures of 64 or 128 px, vertex lighting and fog at the far plane. On one console the textures warp and the vertices jitter; on another they blur.

Core thesis

A world of few polygons can still be entered: a box with a picture on each face becomes a room the camera walks into.

Design principles

  • Build characters from 300 to 1,000 triangles and rooms from simple boxes.
  • Use textures of 64×64 to 128×128 px, with detail painted in.
  • Light with per-vertex colour; bake shadows into the textures.
  • End the draw distance in fog of one colour.
  • Keep the frame at 320×240 or similar, and accept stepped edges.
  • Use a free or a following third-person camera.

Anti-patterns

  • High polygon counts or smooth curves
  • Normal maps or per-pixel light
  • Large, sharp textures
  • Long clear views to the horizon
  • Physically based materials

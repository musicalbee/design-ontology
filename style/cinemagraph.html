Cinemagraph

internet · 2011–now · also: Living photograph, Still-motion loop, Photo with one moving part

A still photograph in which one small part moves in a seamless loop: hair in wind, steam from a cup, a blink. Two New York artists named it in 2011 during fashion week.

Core thesis

The image is still, and one small motion makes the viewer look longer.

Design principles

  • Freeze at least 90% of the frame; let one region of 5% to 15% of the area loop.
  • Loop the motion for 2 to 5 seconds with a seamless join, at 24 fps or more.
  • Mask the moving region with a soft edge of 4px to 12px, so that the join stays invisible.
  • Shoot on a tripod, so that the still part has no drift of more than 1px.
  • Grade the frame with muted colour, a contrast of 0.9 and a grain of 2% to 4%.
  • Set the frame at 4:5 or 16:9 with no caption inside it; put the title below it in a 14px serif line.
  • Show the image at full width with 24px to 64px of margin around it.

Anti-patterns

  • Several parts that move at once
  • A visible jump at the loop point
  • A moving camera that breaks the still part
  • Heavy filters and bright stickers on the frame
  • Fast, strobing motion
  • Captions drawn on the picture

Palette

  • Wall Grey #1E2121 surface

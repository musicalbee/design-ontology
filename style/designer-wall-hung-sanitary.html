Designer wall-hung sanitary ware

ceramics · 1994 to now · also: Designer bathroom ceramics, Wall-hung WC and basin, Architectural sanitary ware

Sanitary ware that a famous designer shapes as a clean object. A WC hangs on the wall, the cistern hides in the wall, and a basin sits on the counter as a thin, sharp bowl.

Core thesis

A bathroom fitting is a designed object, so the ceramic has a clear, simple shape that floats off the floor.

Design principles

  • Hang the WC on the wall at 400 mm seat height, and leave the floor free below it.
  • Hide the cistern in a frame of steel inside the wall, with a plate of 2 buttons.
  • Shape the bowl as a clear geometric form: an oval, a circle or a rounded square.
  • Make a counter-top basin with a wall of 6 to 10 mm and a straight, sharp edge.
  • Use one white glaze, or a satin glaze in a few quiet colours.
  • Keep the bowl rimless, so that the inside is easy to clean.
  • Match the shapes of the WC, the basin and the bidet in one family.

Anti-patterns

  • A floor-standing pan with a visible cistern
  • A moulded flower or a printed pattern on the bowl
  • A bowl with a hard, sharp edge on the inside
  • A mix of unrelated shapes in one set
  • A loud, bright coloured glaze

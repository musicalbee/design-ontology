Constructivism (web)

graphic movement · 1919–1935 · also: Russian Constructivism, Agitprop style, Rodchenko style

Diagonal compositions, heavy sans-serif type, photomontage and flat red, black and cream blocks. It came from the Soviet avant-garde and treats the page as a poster that makes a call to action.

Core thesis

The page is a machine for persuasion: every element is a lever, and the diagonal gives it energy.

Design principles

  • Set the main axis of a hero at an angle of 15° to 30°; rotate type blocks and image blocks together.
  • Use three colours only: red (#D62718), black (#111111) and cream (#EDE3CC); red covers 25% to 40% of the surface.
  • Build headings from heavy condensed or extended sans-serif capitals at 64px or larger, in 1 to 3 lines.
  • Cut photographs into hard shapes (circle, triangle, strip) and place them in monochrome black and red.
  • Use thick bars of 12px to 24px and circles as structure; each bar points at the next action.
  • Make each page carry one call to action in a red block, at 3 times the size of the body text or larger.
  • Overlap at least two elements in each composition by 8px to 40px.

Anti-patterns

  • Centred symmetric layouts
  • Soft gradients, shadows and glass effects
  • Pastel palettes or more than 3 colours
  • Serif or script display type
  • Rounded containers and pill buttons
  • Decorative illustration that makes no call to action
  • Horizontal-only compositions with no diagonal

Palette

  • Cream #EDE3CC background
  • Black #111111 text and bars
  • Agitprop Red #D62718 primary and accent
  • Grey #5C5648 secondary text
  • Sand #DDD0B0 alternate surface

Lineage

Echoes
Brutalism, Constructivist photomontage poster, Cubism, Futurism, Grotesque sans, ROSTA Window stencil poster, Soviet montage, Suprematism, Suprematist porcelain
Hybridises with
Bauhaus, De Stijl (web)
Rebelled against
Art Nouveau (web)

Label meme

internet · 2017–now · also: Labelled stock photo, Caption-the-image meme

A known photo or still where each person or object carries a small text label that gives it a role. The reader maps the roles and finds the joke. The form took off in 2017 with stock photos and cartoon stills.

Core thesis

Let a famous picture act as a diagram: the labels give the roles, and the pose gives the joke.

Design principles

  • Keep the source picture whole and uncropped; use a 4:3 or 3:2 frame.
  • Add 2 to 4 labels in white or black boxes of 4px padding with a 2px radius.
  • Set labels in a plain bold sans-serif at 24px to 32px, 1 to 4 words each.
  • Place each label over its subject, inside the subject area, at less than 20% of the width.
  • Use a white box with black text, or a black box with white text; use one style in all labels.
  • Add a thin line or arrow from label to subject only when the target is not clear.
  • Keep the picture unedited; the labels are the only addition.

Anti-patterns

  • Impact outline capitals
  • Labels that hide the faces
  • More than 5 labels
  • Heavy filters on the picture
  • Long sentences in labels
  • Labels in many colours

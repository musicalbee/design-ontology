Geometric sans

typeface · 1927–present · also: Geometric lineale, Bauhaus type, Futura style

A sans serif built from the circle, the square and the triangle: a round o, a single-storey a, pointed apexes and almost no contrast. It is the type of the modernist programme, clean and optimistic, with a small x-height in the classic cuts.

Core thesis

A letter is a construction of pure forms, free of the hand and of history.

Design principles

  • Draw the o, c, e and the bowls of b, d, p and q from a near-perfect circle.
  • Keep the stroke contrast at 1.0–1.3:1, measured on the o.
  • Use a single-storey a and g, and pointed apexes on A, M, N, V and W.
  • Hold the x-height at 0.58–0.70 of the cap height in the classic cuts, so the ascenders are long.
  • Give the capitals the widths of their figures: a circular O, a square-wide M, a narrow E and S.
  • Set headlines with tight tracking (−0.02 to −0.04 em) and short lines.

Anti-patterns

  • Calligraphic stress or angled pen terminals
  • A two-storey a or g
  • Narrow, oval o and c
  • Strong stroke contrast
  • Old-style figures with swash forms

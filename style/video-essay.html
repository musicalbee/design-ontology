Video Essay

internet · 2014–now · also: Essay video, Long-form commentary, Analysis video

A voice reads an argument over film clips, stills, quotes and maps. Clean type, a steady grid, slow zooms and a muted grade give the frame the calm of a printed essay.

Core thesis

Treat the frame as a page of an essay: one claim, one piece of evidence, and a quiet layout that lets the argument lead.

Design principles

  • Show one piece of evidence in each shot and hold it for 4s to 10s.
  • Set the clip in a frame at 80% to 90% of the width with a 24px black margin.
  • Put a quote or a title in a serif face at 36px to 48px, flush-left, for at least 4s.
  • Add a source line at 14px in the lower-left corner for each clip: title, year and maker.
  • Apply a slow zoom of 3% to 6% over the shot, as in the Ken Burns effect.
  • Use a muted grade with a charcoal ground (#14161A) and one warm accent (#E0A458).
  • Place numbered chapter cards of 2s between sections: a number in 96px and a title in 36px.

Anti-patterns

  • Loud outlined captions
  • Saturated primary colours
  • Face cut-outs with arrows
  • Cuts under 2s without a reason
  • Hard sound hits at each cut
  • Decorative frames that compete with the clip

Palette

  • Charcoal #14161A background
  • Slate #20242B surface
  • Paper #F1EEE7 text
  • Essay Amber #E0A458 primary
  • Archive Blue #6C8EAD accent
  • Ash #9AA0A8 muted

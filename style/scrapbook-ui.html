Scrapbook UI

internet · 2018–now · also: Collage web, Paper collage UI, Craft collage

Cut-out photos, tape, stickers, torn paper, handwritten notes and slight tilts on a paper ground. It makes a page feel like a handmade album and a personal object.

Core thesis

The page is a handmade album: warm, imperfect and made by a person.

Design principles

  • Tilt each card, photo and sticker by -4° to +4° and never place two neighbours at the same angle.
  • Put a strip of tape (80px by 24px, yellow or white at 70% opacity, rotated 5° to 15°) on the top corner of photos.
  • Add torn paper edges with an SVG mask of 6px to 12px amplitude on 2 sides of each paper block.
  • Use a paper ground (#F4ECD8) with a grain of 4% to 8% and 3 to 5 flat accent colours at 70% to 100% saturation.
  • Set notes in a handwriting face at 20px to 28px and body text in a plain sans at 16px.
  • Cast a soft shadow of 0 4px 8px at 18% under each paper item and rotate stickers with a 3px white outline.
  • Overlap items by 8px to 24px and layer at least 3 depth levels (paper, photo, sticker).

Anti-patterns

  • Perfect grids with equal gutters
  • Hard digital edges with no texture
  • Neon and glow effects
  • Monochrome minimal palettes
  • Stock photos with no crop or frame
  • Cold glass and blur
  • Fixed icon sets in a straight row

Palette

  • Paper #F4ECD8 background
  • Ink #2B2A33 text
  • Tomato #C8401C primary
  • Mustard #F3B700 accent
  • Teal #2A9D8F accent two
  • Kraft #C8A97E secondary surface

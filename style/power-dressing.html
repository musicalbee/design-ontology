Power dressing

fashion · 1980–1990 · also: Power suit, Dress for success, Shoulder-pad suit, 1980s office wear

The working woman's suit of the 1980s. It has a tailored jacket with wide padded shoulders, a skirt or trousers in wool and a silk blouse with a bow. The colour is strong and plain.

Core thesis

Dress is a uniform of authority. A woman at work wears the wide shoulder and the sharp line of the man's suit, and so claims the same standing.

Design principles

  • Pad the shoulder so that it measures 2 to 4 cm wider than the natural shoulder line.
  • Cut the jacket to the hip, with a nipped waist or a straight box line.
  • Set the skirt hem at the knee, and cut the trouser straight with a pleat.
  • Choose a fine worsted wool, gabardine or crepe in a plain colour.
  • Pair the jacket with a silk blouse, a bow or a shirt, and keep the neck covered.
  • Match the jacket and the skirt or trouser in one cloth.
  • Finish with a gold button, a belt, a plain pump and a structured bag.

Anti-patterns

  • Soft, loose hippie layers
  • Frills and ruffles
  • A short mini skirt
  • Casual denim and jersey
  • Bright floral prints
  • Sloppy, wrinkled cloth

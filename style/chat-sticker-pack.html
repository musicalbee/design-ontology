Chat Sticker Pack

internet · 2011–now · also: Messenger stickers, LINE stickers, Sticker set

A set of 8 to 40 drawn characters with a pose, a big face and one short word, sent in a chat as a picture. The LINE messenger made the form common in Asia from 2011.

Core thesis

A character with a big face says what a sentence cannot: the chat gets a mascot with a voice.

Design principles

  • Draw one character in a set of 8, 16, 24 or 40 poses on a square of 370px by 320px or less.
  • Outline each pose with a 6px to 8px white border, and add a 3px dark line around the figure.
  • Give the character a head of 50% of the body height, two dot eyes and a mouth of 1 to 3 strokes.
  • Add one short word of 1 to 3 words in rounded type, with a 4px white outline, in a corner of the sticker.
  • Fill shapes with flat colour from a palette of 5 to 7 hues and keep shading to one tone.
  • Tilt the figure by 2° to 4° and let hands and a speech tail break the square edge.
  • Show the pack as a 4 by 2 grid of 96px thumbnails on a pale green field.

Anti-patterns

  • Realistic faces with fine shading
  • Long sentences inside a sticker
  • Thin lines of 1px that vanish at 96px
  • Background boxes that hide the cut-out edge
  • Characters with no emotion in the face
  • Mixed illustration styles in one set

Palette

  • Chat Mint #F3FBEF background
  • Card White #FFFFFF surface
  • Line Ink #1E2A22 text and outline
  • Chat Green #06C755 primary
  • Pop Orange #FF8A3D accent
  • Moss #4F6155 muted text
  • Sky Pop #4DB6FF second accent

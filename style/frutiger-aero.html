Frutiger Aero

internet · 2004–2013 · also: Aero, Web 2.0 Gloss, Vista Aero, Skeuomorphic Nature Tech

Glossy glass buttons, aqua and grass-green gradients, water, sky and bubble imagery and humanist sans type. It grew from Y2K gloss into the Windows Vista, Mac OS X Aqua and Web 2.0 look of the late 2000s.

Core thesis

Technology belongs to nature and the human world, so a digital interface should look clean, glossy, alive and pleasant to touch.

Design principles

  • Build buttons and panels as glass: a vertical gradient, a white highlight on the upper 50% and a 1px light border.
  • Use a sky-to-grass or aqua-to-green gradient as the main background, with a lightness range of 45% to 95%.
  • Place nature imagery in the frame: water drops, bubbles, leaves, clouds, lens flares and blue sky at 20% to 60% of the hero area.
  • Set text in a humanist sans such as Frutiger or Segoe at 14px to 16px, in dark grey or navy, never in pure black.
  • Add a reflection under hero objects, at 25% opacity and 40% of the object height.
  • Round every container with a 6px to 12px radius, and use full pill shapes only for primary buttons.
  • Use soft shadows with 8px to 20px blur and a light glow of 4px to 8px on interactive elements.

Anti-patterns

  • Flat fills with no gradient or highlight
  • Hard offset shadows and thick black outlines
  • Dark, desaturated or monochrome palettes
  • Distressed texture and torn paper edges
  • Square 0px corners on all controls
  • Chrome-silver cyber motifs and pixel fonts
  • Abstract geometric shapes with no natural reference

Palette

  • Sky #7CC8F2 background top
  • Aqua #2BB7D8 primary
  • Grass #5DBB3F secondary and success
  • Glass White #F4FAFF panel surface
  • Deep Water #0A7BD5 links and focus
  • Slate #1F3A4D text
  • Sun #FFB300 single warm accent

Cel-shaded 3D

game · 2000–now · also: Toon shading, Cel shading, Anime 3D

3D models drawn to look like a cartoon or an animation cel: light falls in 2 or 3 flat steps, black outlines trace each shape, and colours stay bright and flat. A full 3D world looks hand drawn.

Core thesis

Real-time 3D does not need to imitate a photograph; it can imitate a drawing and keep its clarity at any angle.

Design principles

  • Light each surface in 2 or 3 flat value steps with a hard edge between them.
  • Draw an outline of 1–4 px around each silhouette and inner crease.
  • Use saturated flat base colours; add texture only as painted strokes.
  • Keep shapes simple and exaggerated: big heads, chunky buildings.
  • Use a bright, clear sky with stylised clouds.
  • Keep the outline weight the same at any distance from the camera.

Anti-patterns

  • Smooth gradient shading
  • Photographic textures
  • Thin or missing outlines
  • Grey, desaturated palettes
  • Realistic proportions

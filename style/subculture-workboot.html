Subculture workboot

fashion · 1960–present · also: Eight-eye boot, Air-cushioned boot, Skinhead and punk boot

The lace-up leather workboot turned youth badge. It has a smooth calf shaft, eight to fourteen eyelets, a yellow welt stitch and a thick air-cushioned sole. Skinheads, punks and others wear it.

Core thesis

A work boot on a young foot is a signal of class and group. It is heavy, plain, cheap and hard to damage. The owner wears it to show that he or she belongs.

Design principles

  • Cut the shaft from smooth calf of 2 mm, and lace it with 8 to 14 eyelets.
  • Stitch the welt with a yellow thread that shows against the black sole.
  • Mould the sole in an air-cushioned form 3 to 4 cm thick, with a grooved tread.
  • Add a pull loop at the heel, and a scripted tag on the loop.
  • Add a steel toe cap under the leather, or leave the toe plain.
  • Polish the leather to a hard shine, or scuff it on purpose.
  • Lace the boot in a set pattern; the lace colour marks the group.

Anti-patterns

  • A thin, dress sole
  • A soft, slouched upper
  • A fashionable heel
  • Decorative embroidery on the leather
  • A pale, delicate colour

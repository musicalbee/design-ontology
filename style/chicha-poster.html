Chicha poster

print · 1980s–present · also: Cartel chicha, Peruvian chicha poster, Fluorescent concert poster

A concert poster in fluorescent ink for chicha music. The maker draws big outlined letters and a tight border by hand, and prints in 2–5 loud flat colours on cheap paper.

Core thesis

A fluorescent poster with big hand-drawn letters can call a crowd to a street concert at a very low cost.

Design principles

  • Draw the headline in big letters with a thick outline and a drop shadow.
  • Use fluorescent pink, green, yellow and orange, with black for the outline.
  • Frame the sheet with a tight border of repeat shapes.
  • Add a photo cut-out of the band or a drawn face, in flat colour.
  • List the names, the place and the date in a dense block.
  • Print by screen in short runs on cheap paper.
  • Let the neon inks clash, and accept a gap or an overlap of 1–3 mm.

Anti-patterns

  • A muted or tonal palette
  • A neat, wide-margin layout
  • Halftone photographs
  • A thin, light typeface
  • Gloss varnish

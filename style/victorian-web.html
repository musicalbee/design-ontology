Victorian Web

graphic movement · 1880–now · also: Victorian, Victoriana, Gothic ornamental web

Dense ornament, engraved borders, high-contrast serif type, deep jewel colours, gold, dark wood and old paper. It recalls the 19th century and gives a page a proud, collected and gothic mood.

Core thesis

Ornament is value: every surface carries pattern, rule and flourish.

Design principles

  • Frame every page with a double border: 1px and 3px lines with a gap of 3px to 4px in a dark ink or gold.
  • Use ornamental corners and dividers (a flourish or a fleuron) of 24px to 64px at each section break.
  • Set headings in a high-contrast serif (Didone or old style) at 48px to 96px with upper case or small caps; body at 17px to 19px with a leading of 1.6.
  • Build the palette from deep jewel tones (burgundy, bottle green, navy) with gold (#B8860B) as accent on a parchment (#F1E5C8) or a dark ground.
  • Fill backgrounds with damask or wallpaper patterns at 6% to 12% opacity and a repeat of 80px to 160px.
  • Use drop caps of 3 to 4 lines on the first paragraph and a gold or red initial.
  • Add engraving-style illustrations with hatching lines of 0.5px to 1px; no flat vector.

Anti-patterns

  • Sans-serif display type
  • Flat empty backgrounds
  • Large empty space with a single focus
  • Neon and bright primary colours
  • Rounded friendly shapes
  • Thin light type
  • Plain borderless cards

Palette

  • Parchment #F1E5C8 background
  • Ebony #1E1612 text
  • Burgundy #6D1A2B primary
  • Bottle #1F4D3A accent
  • Gold #B8860B ornament
  • Sepia #6A5646 secondary text

Lineage

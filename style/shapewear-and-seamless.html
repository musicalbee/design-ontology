Shapewear and Seamless

fashion · 2000–present · also: Smoothing underwear, Control-top hosiery, Seamless knitwear

Smooth, bone-free underwear of stretch nylon and elastane: control-top tights, high-waist briefs and bodysuits. They are made seamless or with bonded edges, so that they hold the body and show no line under the dress.

Core thesis

Shaping can be soft: a knit with elastane holds the body without bone and leaves no line under the cloth.

Design principles

  • Knit the body from nylon and elastane, with 15 to 40 per cent elastane for firm hold.
  • Knit the garment in the round, or bond the edges, so that no seam shows.
  • Use zones: firm powernet at the stomach and the thigh, and a lighter knit elsewhere.
  • Raise the waist to the natural waist, and lengthen the leg to the mid-thigh.
  • Cut the edge with a laser or a bonded band, so that it lies flat on the skin.
  • Match the cloth to a wide range of skin tones, in at least 10 shades.
  • Leave out the bone, the busk and the metal hook.

Anti-patterns

  • Whalebone or steel boning
  • A visible seam or ridge under the dress
  • A pointed, wired cup
  • Rigid cotton coutil
  • A thick band that digs into the skin

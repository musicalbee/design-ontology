Sosaku-hanga creative print

print · 1904–1970 · also: Sosaku-hanga, 創作版画, Creative print, Self-carved, self-printed print

The artist draws, cuts and prints the block alone, and so rejects the three trades of the ukiyo-e shop. The result is a bold cut, an individual block, abstract shape and visible tool marks.

Core thesis

A print is art when one person makes the whole of it, from the drawing to the last rub of the baren.

Design principles

  • Cut the block yourself. Leave the gouge and knife marks in the print.
  • Use 1–8 inks, and choose them with the cut: a large black mass, a field of red, a ground of blue-grey.
  • Use the grain of the plank or the plywood as a texture, not a fault.
  • Print the ink unevenly, 0.5–1.5 mm out of register in places, and keep the shift.
  • Allow abstract shape, or a strong, simple figure, with no soft gradation.
  • Sign in pencil in the margin, and write the edition number (for example 12/30).
  • Print on washi or on thin machine paper with a baren or a small press.

Anti-patterns

  • A polished, even gradation in the manner of a commercial print
  • A carver and a printer who are not the artist
  • Halftone or a photographic tone
  • An airbrushed or vector-clean edge
  • Pretty detail with no cut mark

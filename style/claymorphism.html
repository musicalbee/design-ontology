Claymorphism

internet · 2021–2023 · also: Clay UI, 3D clay style, Soft 3D UI

Puffy, rounded 3D shapes in soft pastel colour with double inner shadows and a soft outer shadow, like modelling clay. It followed neumorphism and flat illustration and turns the interface into toys.

Core thesis

The interface is a toy: soft, inflated forms with colour make the screen friendly and touchable.

Design principles

  • Give containers a radius of 24px to 40px and buttons a radius of 999px.
  • Build depth with 3 shadows: an outer soft shadow (0 16px 32px at 20%), an inner light highlight (inset 8px 8px 16px white at 60%) and an inner dark one (inset -8px -8px 16px at 12%).
  • Use pastel fills with saturation of 40% to 70% and lightness of 70% to 88%; use 3 to 5 hues on one page.
  • Set headlines in a rounded, heavy display face at 40px or larger and body in a rounded sans at 16px.
  • Inflate 3D icons and illustrations to look like clay: round forms, no sharp edges, a 2px lighter rim.
  • Float shapes at 8px to 24px offsets and give the main object a gentle bob of 6px to 10px.
  • Keep text on pastel at contrast 4.5:1 or more by using a dark navy (#2B2D54) for body text.

Anti-patterns

  • Sharp corners and thin hairlines
  • Flat fills with no inner shadow
  • Dark, saturated or neon palettes
  • Hard black outlines
  • Thin or light type
  • Photographic realism
  • Shadows that are only black and hard

Palette

  • Cloud #F4F1FF background
  • Navy #2B2D54 text
  • Candy Pink #FF9CC2 primary
  • Sky #8FD3FF accent
  • Butter #FFE08A second accent
  • Mint #A8EBC8 third accent

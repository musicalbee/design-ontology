Art Nouveau

interior · 1890–1914 · also: Art Nouveau interior, Jugendstil interior, Modern Style, Stile Liberty

The room as one living organism. Whiplash curves of plant stems run from the furniture into the panelling, the door frames and the light fittings; stained glass, carved fruitwood and bronze make each room a total work of art by one designer.

Core thesis

Nature gives the only true line; one designer must draw every part of a room so that it grows as one plant.

Design principles

  • Draw every line as a plant stem: a slow curve that ends in a sudden whip.
  • Let the furniture, the panelling and the light fittings share one continuous line; no part stands alone.
  • Make the window the focal point and fill it with stained or opalescent glass.
  • Use soft, natural colours: moss, lily green, mauve, cream and amber.
  • Carve fruitwood and pearwood in soft, curved relief; cast lamps and handles in bronze as stems and buds.
  • Hide the joints between wall, ceiling and furniture under curved mouldings.

Anti-patterns

  • Straight rectilinear furniture with no curve
  • Revival ornament from past centuries
  • Loose furniture of different styles in one room
  • Pattern that repeats on a mechanical grid
  • Hard cool light

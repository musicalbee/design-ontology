Nordic functional outdoor

fashion · 1960–now · also: Scandinavian outdoor dress, Friluftsliv dress, Fjell and forest wear

The plain, tough dress of the Nordic hike and the long trek. A wax-proofed twill jacket, wool and fleece layers, and a design that serves the trail first.

Core thesis

Dress for a day out in all weather. Simple shapes, tough cloth that can be waxed and mended, and layers that serve the walker for a lifetime.

Design principles

  • Weave a dense poly-cotton twill of 300 to 400 g per square metre for the jacket and the trouser.
  • Rub in wax each season to keep the cloth proof against rain and wind.
  • Cut the jacket roomy, for 3 layers of wool and fleece beneath.
  • Add a large chest pocket and a hood that fits over a cap.
  • Reinforce the knee, the seat and the elbow with a second layer.
  • Choose a muted earth colour that sits in forest and fell.
  • Repair a worn piece in place with a patch, not with a new buy.

Anti-patterns

  • A glossy, thin shell for the city
  • Bright colour that clashes with the fell
  • A tight cut that blocks layers
  • Decoration that serves no use
  • A throwaway piece with no repair path

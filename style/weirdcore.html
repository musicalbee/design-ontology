Weirdcore

internet · 2020–now · also: Oddcore, Strangecore, Creepycore

Low-quality photos of empty places, red warning text, black voids, faceless shapes and old 3D or web fragments. The mood is a strange dream memory. It grew on Tumblr, Reddit and Twitter.

Core thesis

Make a familiar thing wrong in one small way, and let the poor image quality carry the unease.

Design principles

  • Use a low-resolution photo (320px to 640px wide) scaled up with no smoothing, with JPEG blocks visible.
  • Cast the photo with a 10% to 20% colour shift to yellow-green or magenta.
  • Place one black rectangle (the void) at 5% to 15% of the frame over a key area.
  • Set one line of red (#E01B1B) text of 14px to 24px such as a number or a short phrase.
  • Add one wrong detail: a face with no features, a second horizon or a door with no wall.
  • Add a date stamp or a plus sign of 12px in a corner.
  • Keep the frame off-centre by 8% to 20%.

Anti-patterns

  • Clean studio lighting
  • High-resolution sharp photography
  • Cheerful bright palettes
  • Symmetrical, polished layouts
  • Decorative flourishes
  • Smooth vector illustration
  • Friendly mascot characters

Palette

  • Void Black #0B0B0B background
  • Dull Fluorescent #C9C58A primary
  • Wrong Red #E01B1B accent
  • Carpet Teal #3B6B66 second accent
  • Old Wall #D9D4C0 text
  • Dust Grey #6F6E68 muted

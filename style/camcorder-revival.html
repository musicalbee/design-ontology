Camcorder revival

film · 2020–today · also: VHS look, Tape look, Home-video look, Y2K camcorder

A 4:3 picture with tape noise, a date stamp and a shaky hand. It looks like a home video from 1985 to 2005. Creators shoot on old Hi8 and MiniDV or add the look in post, to look near and unpolished.

Core thesis

A flawed, low-resolution tape image looks like a real memory, so the defect is the proof of the moment.

Design principles

  • Frame in 4:3 at 640 × 480 px (SD) and keep the 4:3 on a vertical feed with a blurred side fill.
  • Shoot at 29.97 fps (NTSC) or 25 fps (PAL) in 60i or 50i, with a fixed 1/60 s or 1/50 s shutter and an auto-exposure that pumps by 0.5 to 1 stop.
  • Add a date stamp or a REC and battery icon in the corner, for example 'NOV 18 2003', at 5% of frame height in orange or white.
  • Raise the black level to 8% to 10%, clip the highlights at 100% with a bloom of 5 to 10 px, and set saturation at +25%.
  • Add chroma bleed of 2 to 4 px to the right of red edges, 8% to 15% noise and a tracking glitch of 4 to 12 frames, no more than once a minute.
  • Hold takes of 8 to 30 s with a handheld, on a wide lens of 35mm to 40mm equivalent, and let the zoom hunt once in a take.
  • Record the sound on the camera microphone, mono, at about −18 LUFS integrated, with wind and 3 to 6 dB of auto-gain pumping.

Anti-patterns

  • A crisp 4K picture with a flat overlay
  • A smooth gimbal move
  • A 2.39:1 crop
  • A studio-clean voice-over mix
  • A teal and orange grade
  • A stack of effects on every shot
  • An even filter with no handheld shake

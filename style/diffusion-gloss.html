Diffusion Gloss

internet · 2022–now · also: AI hyperreal render, Midjourney look, Prompt-core gloss

A smooth, over-detailed image with a perfect rim light, a teal and amber grade and a soft bokeh ground. It became the default look of text-to-image models from 2022.

Core thesis

Everything is lit, sharp and finished: the model gives each surface the same expensive sheen, and the sheen is the style.

Design principles

  • Light the subject with a rim light of 2px to 6px width in amber (#F0A04B) against a teal haze (#1F8A99).
  • Smooth skin and surfaces to a pore size of under 1px, and add fine detail of 1px to 2px on hair, fabric and metal.
  • Place the subject on the upper-third line with a bokeh ground of 8 to 20 soft discs, 20px to 120px wide.
  • Raise contrast at the mid-tones and set a bloom of 12px to 24px around highlights.
  • Use a 4:5 or 16:9 frame at 1024px or more on the short side.
  • Add a short caption in 14px sans with the prompt words in lower case under the image.
  • Keep the colour grade at two hues, teal and amber, with one pink accent at 10%.

Anti-patterns

  • Visible grain and flat, dull light
  • Hand-drawn line art
  • Smudged low-resolution output
  • Messy, cluttered grounds with no focus
  • Neutral, even document light
  • Brutalist type

Palette

  • Midnight #0E1320 background
  • Deep Panel #182033 surface
  • Amber Rim #F0A04B primary
  • Teal Haze #1F8A99 accent
  • Bloom Pink #D96C8F second accent
  • Pearl Skin #EBC9B0 image reference
  • Mist #DDE6EE text

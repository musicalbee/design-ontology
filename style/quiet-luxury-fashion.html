Quiet luxury fashion

fashion · 2006–present · also: Stealth wealth, Old money dress, Logo-free luxury, Understated luxury

The logo-free, high-cost dress of the 2000s and after. It uses cashmere, loden and fine wool in neutral colours, with perfect cut and hand finish. The price is in the cloth and not in a mark.

Core thesis

Real luxury is quiet. It shows in the touch of the cloth and the fit of the cut, and only a person who knows will see it.

Design principles

  • Use no logo and no monogram on the outside of the garment.
  • Choose cloth of the highest grade: cashmere of 14 to 16 micron, fine wool, loden and silk.
  • Keep to neutral colours: camel, oatmeal, navy, grey, ivory and chocolate.
  • Cut a relaxed, long and straight line, with a soft shoulder and room in the body.
  • Finish by hand: linked knit seams, hand-set buttons and a fine hem.
  • Make each garment last 10 years or more, in a classic form.
  • Build the outfit from a few pieces that mix in one palette.

Anti-patterns

  • A large visible logo
  • Loud print and bright colour
  • Shiny synthetic cloth
  • Trend pieces that last a season
  • Heavy hardware and ornament
  • A tight, flashy cut

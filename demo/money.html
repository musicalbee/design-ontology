Money behind the styles

Each line is one flow of money or paid support. It runs from the backer on the left, through the person or the house that got it, to the styles that the money helped. A flow that went to a whole movement runs straight to the style. Rows are in the order of the first payment. Move the year to see who had paid by then. A dashed line is a contested claim. Select a name to see each flow, its amount where a source gives one, and the source.

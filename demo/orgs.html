Organisations and their styles

Each row is one studio, school, house, company, magazine or crew on the same time line as the styles. The line runs from the year of foundation to the year of closure. Before 1840 the scale is compressed. The pale band behind it is the period of each style that the org made, taught, published, exhibited, funded or sold. Dots are the years when a person joined. A dotted line means that no open source gives the year of foundation: it covers only the periods of its styles.

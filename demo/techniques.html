Techniques across domains

A technique starts in one domain and moves into others, often decades later. Each row is one technique. The ringed dot is the origin; each later dot is the year a domain took it up, with the lag in years above the hop. The letter and the colour name the domain. A dashed dot is an estimate. Before 1840 the scale is compressed, before 1400 it is compressed again, and before 700 BCE a third time.

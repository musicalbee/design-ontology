One composition, every painting style

The same schema: a horizon at 40% of the height, a diagonal path, one focal point on the third line, one dark mass in front and one light mass behind. Each style supplies only its palette, ordered by lightness.

Colours come from the palette of each record in data/painting/styles/. No image of a painting is copied here. The reference works are listed by museum and id: a mark of PD means the museum flags the image as public domain.

Shot length and frame, per film style

Each bar spans the average shot length written in the record, on a log scale from 1 s to 200 s. Each frame is the main aspect ratio of the style, at the same height.

Most figures are estimates and say so in the record; check Cinemetrics for a given title. Styles are in the order of their period.

Data: data/film/styles/*.json, fields editing_and_rhythm.average_shot_length and lens_and_format.aspect_ratio. No clips or stills are copied; each record lists its sources.

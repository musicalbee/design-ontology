The lineage graph

Every style on one time line, with a lane for each domain. Select a style to see all its edges, echoes included. Edges inside a domain show descent, rejection and mixing. Echoes join styles of different domains that share an idea. Echo reach follows the echoes for up to four hops, so you can see how far the idea of a style travels, and who carried it.

# Glam platform shoe

> A glam shoe adds height and noise: a thick platform and a bright upper make the wearer taller, louder and not quite real.

The raised shoe of glam rock and disco. It has a platform sole of 5 to 10 cm, a thick heel, and a loud upper in patent, glitter, suede or snake print.

**Period:** 1970–1979 · **Era:** Youthquake · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Platform boot, Glitter-rock platform, Seventies platform

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Glam platform shoe, you MUST:

1. Raise the sole 5 to 10 cm at the toe, with a heel 2 to 4 cm higher than the platform.
2. Cut the platform in layers of cork, wood or foam, and cover them in leather or cloth.
3. Use a rounded or a squared toe, and a wide, solid heel.
4. Cover the upper in a loud material: patent, glitter, mirror, snake print or metallic.
5. Add stars, stripes or a stacked sole in two or three colours.
6. Close the shoe with a thick ankle strap, a buckle, or a high lace shaft.
7. Match the platform edge to the upper in colour, or contrast it on purpose.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A heavy, tall shoe: a thick platform, a wide heel and a short vamp that sits on top.
- **Line:** A strong, stepped line: the platform edge, the heel and the upper in blocks of colour.
- **Fit:** Snug at the instep and the ankle, with a strap to hold the foot over the thick sole.
- **Proportion:** Platform 5 to 10 cm; heel 9 to 15 cm; the ratio of platform to heel about 1 to 2.
- **Rules:** Keep the platform thick and the heel wide. · Wear it with flared or wide trousers that show the sole. · Use two colours or more on the sole. · Add a strap at the ankle for a sandal or mule.

## Fabrics & materials

- **Primary:** patent-leather · suede · calfskin · satin
- **Secondary:** exotic-skin · kidskin · pvc-leathercloth · velvet · acrylic-glass · brocade
- **Avoid:** vegetable-tanned-leather · canvas · shell-cordovan · serge
- **Rule:** Use patent, suede and satin for the upper, and add snake skin, vinyl and clear acrylic for show. Build the sole from layers of wood, cork or foam.

## Construction

- **Methods:** built-up platform of layered cork or wood · covered platform in leather or vinyl · wide moulded heel · hand lasting over a platform last · ankle strap riveted to the quarter · contrast-colour sole edge

- **Details:** A stacked and striped platform, a glitter or mirror vamp, a star motif, a buckled ankle strap and a wide block heel.

### Platform sole (process)

A sole that is raised under the whole foot by layers of cork, wood or foam, and that is covered in leather or cloth. Origin: fashion, Florence, Italy, c. 1936.

- Build the sole in layers of cork, wood or foam, 5 to 10 cm thick.
- Cover the edge of the layers in leather, cloth or vinyl.
- Fit a wide heel that sits 2 to 4 cm higher than the platform.

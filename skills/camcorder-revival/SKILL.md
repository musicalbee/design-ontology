# Camcorder revival

> A flawed, low-resolution tape image looks like a real memory, so the defect is the proof of the moment.

A 4:3 picture with tape noise, a date stamp and a shaky hand. It looks like a home video from 1985 to 2005. Creators shoot on old Hi8 and MiniDV or add the look in post, to look near and unpolished.

**Period:** 2020–today · **Era:** Platform and creator video · **Domain:** Film & Video · **Also called:** VHS look, Tape look, Home-video look, Y2K camcorder

## When to load this skill

Load it when the user asks for: "camcorder", "vhs look", "home video", "tape look", "y2k camcorder", "90s home video", "lo-fi video", "date stamp".

Good for: music video and artist content, fashion and youth brand campaigns, found-footage horror, video prompts for a memory or a party.

Do not use it when:
- The brief needs a premium product shot or accurate colour.
- The viewer must read small text or detail on a phone.

## System directives

When planning, shooting, prompting, editing or grading moving images in Camcorder revival, you MUST:

1. Frame in 4:3 at 640 × 480 px (SD) and keep the 4:3 on a vertical feed with a blurred side fill.
2. Shoot at 29.97 fps (NTSC) or 25 fps (PAL) in 60i or 50i, with a fixed 1/60 s or 1/50 s shutter and an auto-exposure that pumps by 0.5 to 1 stop.
3. Add a date stamp or a REC and battery icon in the corner, for example 'NOV 18 2003', at 5% of frame height in orange or white.
4. Raise the black level to 8% to 10%, clip the highlights at 100% with a bloom of 5 to 10 px, and set saturation at +25%.
5. Add chroma bleed of 2 to 4 px to the right of red edges, 8% to 15% noise and a tracking glitch of 4 to 12 frames, no more than once a minute.
6. Hold takes of 8 to 30 s with a handheld, on a wide lens of 35mm to 40mm equivalent, and let the zoom hunt once in a take.
7. Record the sound on the camera microphone, mono, at about −18 LUFS integrated, with wind and 3 to 6 dB of auto-gain pumping.

## Composition

- **Framing:** A casual 4:3 frame with the subject centred and the head cut off at times; the horizon is slightly off level, by 2° to 5°.
- **Staging in depth:** A subject at 1 to 3 m in a lived-in room or a street; no staged layers.
- **Subject placement:** Centred or wherever the person stands; the operator follows late, by 0.2 to 0.5 s.

**Principles**

| Principle | Rule | Value |
|---|---|---|
| framing | Frame in 4:3 with a date stamp in the corner and a head that cuts the top of the frame. | head cuts the top 5% to 10%; 1 timestamp in a corner |
| focal-point | Centre the person and let the autofocus hunt, so that the focus drifts for 0.5 to 1 s. | focus hunt 0.5 to 1 s |

| rhythm | Hold shots for 6 to 20 s with no cutaway, and let the cut come late. | 6 to 20 s average shot |

## Camera & movement

- **Movement:** Handheld, with a sudden zoom, a pan to a laugh and a walk. The operator talks off camera.
- **Support:** Handheld by the strap or the grip
- **Camera height:** Chest or eye level, 1.2 to 1.7 m
- **Move speed:** Walking pace, 1.0 to 1.5 m/s; zoom hunt of 1 to 2 s

## Lens & format

- **Aspect ratio:** 4:3 (640 × 480 px)
- **Frame rate:** 29.97 fps (60i) or 25 fps (50i)
- **Shutter angle:** 180° (1/60 s at 29.97 fps; 1/50 s at 25 fps)
- **Focal length:** Wide end, about 40mm (35mm format); optical zoom 10× to 20×
- **Aperture:** Auto, f/1.8 to f/4
- **Resolution:** VHS about 240 lines; Hi8 about 400 lines; MiniDV 720 × 480 px
- **Focus:** Autofocus that hunts, with a soft, low-contrast picture.
- **Equipment:** Hi8, VHS-C or MiniDV camcorder · on-camera microphone · tape-look plug-in

## Light

- **Quality:** Whatever is in the room: ceiling light, a flash-like on-camera lamp, the daylight at a window.
- **Direction:** From above or the front; the face has red-eye or a shine, and the window blows out.
- **Key to fill ratio:** Uncontrolled, 2:1 to 6:1
- **Colour temperature:** Auto white balance that drifts between 3200K and 5600K
- **Source:** Room lights, windows and on-camera lamp

## Colour & grade

- **Treatment:** Warm, faded and slightly oversaturated, with lifted blacks, soft detail and a colour smear at edges.
- **Grade recipe:**
  - Scale to 640 × 480 px and back to the delivery size with a soft filter.
  - Lift the black point to 8% to 10% and clip the white point at 100% with a bloom of 5 to 10 px.
  - Raise saturation by +25%, add a slight green-cyan cast in the shadows of 5% and a warm cast in the highlights of 5%.
  - Add horizontal chroma bleed of 2 to 4 px and noise of 8% to 15%.
  - Add scanlines at 10% opacity and a tracking glitch of 4 to 12 frames at most once a minute.
  - Burn in the date stamp and a REC icon at 5% of frame height.
- **Texture:** Tape noise, amount 8% to 15%, with scanlines at 10% opacity

## Editing & rhythm

- **Average shot length:** 8 to 20 s
- **Shot length range:** 2 s to 60 s
- **Cutting logic:** Cut as if the operator stopped and started the tape: in-camera cuts, a hard start of the next scene, no cut on action.
- **Transitions:** Hard cut with a 2 to 4 frame glitch; a tape rewind or a record flash for a joke.
- **Runtime:** 30 s to 5 min; clips on a feed run 15 to 45 s

## Sound

- **Approach:** Camera microphone only: mono, near and noisy, with a voice off camera and room tone.
- **Dialogue:** Casual and overlapping; the operator speaks and laughs.
- **Music:** A song that plays in the room, or a radio or a cheap loop; no score.
- **Loudness:** −18 LUFS integrated, with 3 to 6 dB of auto-gain pumping

## Titles & graphics

- **Treatment:** On-screen camera display: REC dot, battery bar, PLAY arrow and a date stamp; blue screen at tape start.
- **Type:** Pixel or monospace display face, white or orange, 4% to 5% of frame height.

## Techniques

These techniques started in one domain and moved into this style. Apply the rules as they are written; the full record is in [`TECHNIQUES.md`](../TECHNIQUES.md).

### Glitch (process)

A fault in a signal, kept as an image: torn lines, split colour channels and repeated frames. Origin: film & video, Wuppertal, 1963.

- Shift the red and blue channels by 2 to 8px in opposite directions.
- Cut bands of 4 to 40px and move them sideways by 10 to 60px.
- Run the effect in bursts of 2 to 6 frames at 24 fps, and leave the image clean between bursts.
- Do not apply it to body text.

### Handheld camera (tool)

A camera that the operator carries on the body, with no tripod or dolly; the shake of the body shows in the frame. Origin: film & video, Paris, c. 1960.

- Use a light camera and a wide lens of 18mm to 35mm so that the shake stays small in the frame.
- Follow the action at the pace of a walking person and let the frame move 2% to 5% off the subject.
- Do not stabilise in post; accept a late refocus and a missed frame as part of the take.
- Shoot long takes of 30 seconds or more, so that the camera seems to live in the room.

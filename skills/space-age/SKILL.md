# Space Age Design

> The future is a place that people will visit, and a clean, rounded, machine-made form shows that the trip is safe and near.

White rounded forms, orbit rings, starbursts and wide extended type in cobalt, orange and silver. It started with Sputnik and peaked at Expo 67 Montreal and EXPO '70 Osaka, and it shows a confident future of monorails and rockets.

**Period:** 1957–1972 · **Era:** Modernist · **Domain:** Graphic & UI Design · **Also called:** Space Race Graphics, Expo Futurism, Retro-futurism (1960s)

## When to load this skill

Load it when the user asks for: "space age", "space race", "sputnik", "expo 67", "expo 70", "retro futurism", "1960s futurism", "orbit", "eurostile", "jetsons", "monorail", "world's fair".

Good for: science and technology events, museum and exhibition posters, aerospace and travel heritage, music and podcast covers, product launches with a playful future theme.

Do not use it when:
- The brand must feel warm, domestic and handmade; use mid-century modern.
- The brief asks for a dark, gritty or dystopian future.
- The product is a dense data tool that needs a neutral grid.

## System directives

When generating UI or graphic layouts in Space Age Design, you MUST:

1. Build forms from circles and ellipses: a 1 to 3 orbit rings, a sphere or a capsule fills 30 to 60% of the width of the layout.
2. Set headlines in an extended sans serif in upper case, with a width of 120% to 150% and tracking of +0.04em to +0.1em.
3. Use a palette of one deep blue, one signal orange and silver on a white ground, and keep the orange under 15% of the surface.
4. Fill one large field with op-art concentric rings or radial lines, with 8 to 16 repeats at an even step of 12px to 24px.
5. Add 1 starburst of 8 to 16 rays as a focal accent, and place it on an upper third line.
6. Tilt the main axis of the composition by 8 to 15 degrees so that the layout reads as a path of travel, upward and to the right.
7. Round every container with a radius of 24px or a full 999px pill, as in a spacecraft window or a capsule.
8. Pair each picture of a machine, such as a rocket, a monorail or a pavilion, with a small technical label of 11px to 12px in upper case.

## Guardrails

Each line below breaks the style. Check your output against the list before you return it.

- **NEVER:** Organic kidney and boomerang shapes with earth tones, which belong to the domestic atomic look.
- **NEVER:** Condensed or serif display type in place of an extended sans serif.
- **NEVER:** Neon glow, chrome bevels and scan lines from later retro-futurism.
- **NEVER:** Grey, dirty or worn surfaces that show a used and cramped future.
- **NEVER:** Sharp 0px corners on panels and buttons.
- **NEVER:** Dense body copy set in an extended face.
- **NEVER:** Astronaut and planet clip art used as a filler.

## Spatial & grid logic

- **Grid mechanics:** A 12-column grid with 24px gutters and an 8px baseline; the main shape and its orbit rings centre on a column line and may cross the grid by up to 96px.
- **Whitespace ratio:** 35–50% of the surface stays white; the spacing scale is 8, 16, 24, 48 and 96px.
- **Alignment rules:** Headlines sit on a diagonal axis of 8 to 15 degrees; body text is flush left and stays horizontal; labels align to a baseline row at the bottom edge.
- **Container geometry:** Cards use a 24px radius, buttons a full 999px pill, and image masks a circle or an ellipse with a 3:2 ratio; no square corners.

## Typography

- **Classifications:** extended sans serif · neo-grotesque sans serif
- **Tracking:** Display sizes take +0.04em to +0.1em; body stays at 0; small upper-case labels take +0.12em.
- **Leading:** Display 1.1; body 1.55; line-heights are multiples of the 8px baseline.
- **Hierarchy rules:**
  - Use weight 400 for display and subheads, because Michroma has one weight; use 400 for body, 600 for strong words and labels.
  - Set all display lines and labels in upper case; set body in sentence case.
  - Use four sizes, for example 12, 18, 32 and 64px, with a step of 1.6 to 2.
  - Keep display lines to 4 words or fewer; extended faces lose legibility in long lines.

## Colour & lighting

- **Palette structure:** A white ground, one deep blue for type and shapes, one signal orange for a single accent, and a silver grey for rings and rules.
- **Contrast rules:** Deep blue on white holds at least 12:1; orange carries large type or solid fields only; white type on deep blue holds at least 12:1.
- **Surface treatment:** Flat or near-flat fields; one soft linear gradient from white to Mist at 5% to 10% contrast may suggest a moulded plastic form.
- **Light and shadow:** A single soft light from the upper left: a gloss highlight of 20% white on the top-left of a sphere and a shadow of 0 8px 24px at 12% deep blue.

## Materiality & texture

- **Surface simulation:** Moulded white plastic and enamelled metal; surfaces read as smooth and seamless, with no seams, screws or wear.
- **Border rules:** Rings and rules in Orbit Silver or deep blue at 1px to 2px; shapes need no outline.
- **Blur and elevation:** One soft shadow of 0 8px 24px at 12% deep blue for raised cards; no hard offset shadows and no glow.

## Ornamentation & iconography

- **Iconography:** Solid pictograms with round terminals on a circular tile: rocket, orbit, satellite, monorail, pavilion; stroke of 2px where outlined.
- **Illustration:** Flat vector or screen-printed forms in 2 to 4 colours: a rocket, a monorail car or a pavilion dome set on a ring or a starburst; op-art concentric fields for the ground.
- **Decorative elements:** orbit rings · starbursts · concentric op-art circles · radial lines · small crosshair marks · dotted trajectories · technical labels

## Motion & interactivity

- **Born on screen:** no — the values here translate a print style for screen
- **Transition physics:** Translation for screen: shapes move on arcs and orbits, with a smooth launch and a soft landing; rings rotate slowly at 8 to 20 degrees a second.
- **Easing:** cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.8, 0.3, 1)
- **Duration (ms):** 360
- **Hover and active:** Hover lifts a card by 4px and rotates its ring by 15 degrees; active fills the pill with Rocket Orange and sets the label to deep blue.

- **Icons:** solid, stroke 2 on a 24px field, round caps, round joins, container: circle. Use: `<svg class="icon"><use href="pack/icons.svg#home"/></svg>`. An icon takes `currentColor`.
- **Icon family:** the sprite is cut from Phosphor (Fill) by Phosphor Icons, licence MIT. Reason: Solid forms with round terminals on a circle tile: the moulded plastic of a capsule window, and metaphors (rocket, planet, satellite) that the era knew. It is an open-source stand-in for a demo. For a product, use the icon library of the brand and choose the family by the rules below.
- **Display type:** weight 400, tracking 0.06em, leading 1.1, case: uppercase.
- **Weights:** display 400, subhead 400, body 400, bold in text 600, labels and buttons 600. The style uses 2 weights (400, 600) and no other. The display has the weight of the body, so size and face carry the hierarchy. The accent is case and tracking: an upper-case label is the accent, so the weights stay few.
- **Body type:** 16px, leading 1.55.
- **Page:** hero poster, left aligned, regular density, accent tilt 3°.
- **Ground:** rays. Space-age graphics show a launch: rays and orbits that leave one point on the horizon. Put the class `ground` on the first screen of the page; it takes `--ground`, a stack of gradients built from the colour tokens. The ground can run behind the copy, so put the class `ground-copy` on each text block of that screen: the block gets `--color-bg` and a soft edge. Keep the rest of the page on `--color-bg`.
- **Text colour:** `--color-text` is for the main reading field only. On each field, use its token: `--color-on-bg`, `--color-on-surface`, `--color-on-primary`, `--color-on-accent`.

### How to choose an icon pack

An icon pack is a typeface for pictures. Choose it with the same care as the text face. These rules hold for an open-source set and for a licensed library.

1. **The stroke matches the stem.** Set an icon next to the body text at text size. The line of the icon and the stem of a letter have the same weight.
2. **Terminals and corners follow the radius token.** Butt caps and mitre joins go with a radius of zero. Round caps and round joins go with a soft radius.
3. **The fill mode follows the technology of the era.** Print eras cut solid forms. Screen eras with depth fill and shade the form. Current product interfaces draw an outline.
4. **The metaphors are not younger than the style.** Each picture is an object that the era knew. An icon with a later metaphor breaks the period.
5. **One family, one grid.** All icons come from one family and one variant, drawn on one field. Do not mix families on one surface.
6. **The family covers the product.** List each concept that the product needs before you commit. A family that has no icon for one of them is the wrong family.
7. **The colour is a token.** An icon takes currentColor. A second tone comes from a token, not from the file.
8. **The licence permits the use.** Read the licence of the set. Give credit where the licence asks for it. A product with a brand uses its licensed library and applies the same rules to choose the family.

## Techniques

### Sunburst (motif)

Rays from a disc or a half disc: a sign of light and of the new, on a facade, a gate, a mirror or a clock. Origin: architecture, Paris, 1925.

- Draw 12 to 36 rays from one centre; alternate long and short rays, or two finishes.
- Keep the centre disc at 15% to 30% of the full diameter.
- In a room, use one sunburst as the focal point of one wall.

## Lineage fallbacks

When the user asks for a variation, do not improvise. Blend toward the neighbour named here, and load its skill.

- If the user asks for **warmer and more domestic**: move toward [`mid-century-modern`](../mid-century-modern/SKILL.md) (Mid-Century Modern). Swap the blue and silver for mustard, teal and walnut, replace rings with boomerang and kidney shapes, and set headlines in a geometric face; keep the white ground.
- If the user asks for **more trippy**: move toward [`psychedelic`](../psychedelic/SKILL.md) (Psychedelic). Saturate the concentric rings to magenta, orange and violet, let the lettering warp, and drop the white ground; keep the circular forms.
- If the user asks for **bolder and pop**: move toward [`pop-art-web`](../pop-art-web/SKILL.md) (Pop Art (web)). Add a 3px black outline to shapes, halftone dots at 8px and primary yellow; keep the extended headline and the tilted axis.
- If the user asks for **more rigorous**: move toward [`swiss-international`](../swiss-international/SKILL.md) (Swiss International Style). Set the radius to 0px, use one neo-grotesque typeface, and align everything to a 12-column grid; keep the single orange accent.

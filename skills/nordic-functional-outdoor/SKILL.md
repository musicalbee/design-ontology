# Nordic functional outdoor

> Dress for a day out in all weather. Simple shapes, tough cloth that can be waxed and mended, and layers that serve the walker for a lifetime.

The plain, tough dress of the Nordic hike and the long trek. A wax-proofed twill jacket, wool and fleece layers, and a design that serves the trail first.

**Period:** 1960–now · **Era:** Youthquake · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Scandinavian outdoor dress, Friluftsliv dress, Fjell and forest wear

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Nordic functional outdoor, you MUST:

1. Weave a dense poly-cotton twill of 300 to 400 g per square metre for the jacket and the trouser.
2. Rub in wax each season to keep the cloth proof against rain and wind.
3. Cut the jacket roomy, for 3 layers of wool and fleece beneath.
4. Add a large chest pocket and a hood that fits over a cap.
5. Reinforce the knee, the seat and the elbow with a second layer.
6. Choose a muted earth colour that sits in forest and fell.
7. Repair a worn piece in place with a patch, not with a new buy.

## Techniques

### Layering system (skill)

A set of thin layers, each with one task. A base next to the skin, a warm mid layer and a shell against wind and rain. The wearer adds or sheds a layer to fit the work. Origin: fashion, Himalaya, c. 1924.

- Put a base layer next to the skin that moves sweat away.
- Add a mid layer of wool or fleece to hold warm air.
- Close the outfit with a shell that stops wind and rain, and open it when the work warms the body.

### Wax proofing (process)

A dressing of oil or wax worked into a dense cloth so that it sheds water; the owner renews it each year. Origin: fashion, North Sea coasts, c. 1800.

- Warm the wax blend until it is liquid.
- Work the wax into the cloth by hand or by machine, and press it into the seams.
- Let the cloth cure, and renew the wax when water no longer beads on the face.

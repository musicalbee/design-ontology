# Costume Jewellery

> A jewel is an accessory to the outfit: it need not be real, because the effect counts for more than the value.

Jewels made to go with a dress, not to hold value. They are glass pearls, paste, gilt metal, Bakelite and chrome, worn in ropes and clusters by day and night, and chosen for the look.

**Period:** 1924–1960s · **Era:** Interwar modern · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Fashion jewellery, Bijouterie fantaisie, Faux jewels

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Costume Jewellery, you MUST:

1. Make the piece from cheap, bright materials: glass pearl, paste, gilt brass, celluloid, Bakelite and chrome.
2. Wear the piece in quantity: a rope of 120 to 200 cm of glass pearls, or six bracelets on one arm.
3. Copy a real jewel in size and form, then change one thing, such as a cross, a coin or a colour. The change makes it read as a joke on wealth.
4. Mix real and false pieces on the same person, and make no secret of which is which.
5. Change the jewel each season to match the new colour and cut of the dress.
6. Plate the base metal with gold, silver or chrome by the electric bath, and keep the plate thin and bright.
7. Cast, stamp or mould the parts in large runs from one die, and sell them at a low price.
8. Make a large piece: a brooch of 6 to 10 cm or a cuff of 4 to 6 cm width.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A large, bold, easy piece: a long rope of pearls, a wide cuff, a clip brooch or a pair of button earrings.
- **Line:** Round and rope-like, or a block of colour; shapes taken from coins, crosses, flowers and shells.
- **Fit:** Worn in layers, in ropes over a knit or a suit, pinned on a lapel, clipped on a collar or an ear.
- **Proportion:** A rope of pearls is 120 to 200 cm long. A cuff is 4 to 6 cm wide. A brooch is 6 to 10 cm across.
- **Rules:**
  - Hang two or three ropes of pearls in different lengths over a plain dress.
  - Match the colour of the jewel to the dress or to the season.
  - Clip a large brooch on the lapel or the hat.
  - Wear a wide cuff in gilt metal with a bright cabochon over the sleeve.

### Chrome plating (process)

A thin layer of chromium is put on steel by electrolysis. The layer gives a hard, bright surface that does not tarnish. Origin: fashion, New York, United States, c. 1924.

- Clean the steel part and plate it with copper and nickel.
- Put the part in a chromic acid bath and pass a current through it.
- Buff the part to a mirror finish.

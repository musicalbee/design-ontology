# Sneaker culture

> In this style the shoe is a signal: colour, release and story matter more than function, and an owner shows taste by what is rare.

The sneaker as a badge. It is a leather high-top in team colours with a bold brand mark, an air-cushioned sole and a limited release. Players, rappers and collectors wear it.

**Period:** 1985–present · **Era:** Power and subculture · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Signature basketball shoe, Hip-hop sneaker, Collector sneaker

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Sneaker culture, you MUST:

1. Cut the upper in leather panels of 3 or more colours, in a blocked pattern.
2. Show one large mark on the side, the tongue or the heel.
3. Raise the sole to 3 to 4 cm, with a visible cushion unit in the heel.
4. Use a high-top shaft to the ankle for a basketball shoe, or a low-top for daily wear.
5. Release a colourway in a limited run, and name each colourway.
6. Keep the white midsole clean, and lace the shoe on purpose, loose or tight.
7. Collect the pair in its box, and wear it with a matched jersey or a tracksuit.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A high, padded shoe: a wide, thick tongue, a high ankle collar and a chunky midsole.
- **Line:** A strong, blocked line from the toe cap to the ankle, with a large mark on the side.
- **Fit:** Snug and supported at the ankle, padded at the tongue and wide at the forefoot.
- **Proportion:** Sole 3 to 4 cm; shaft 12 to 15 cm above the sole; tongue height 3 cm above the lace.
- **Rules:** Block the colour in three or more panels. · Show the mark in a contrast colour. · Keep the midsole bright and clean. · Wear with trousers that show the shoe.

## Fabrics & materials

- **Primary:** calfskin · patent-leather · suede · canvas
- **Secondary:** nylon-mesh · eva-foam · vulcanised-rubber · exotic-skin · microfibre-suede
- **Avoid:** shell-cordovan · vegetable-tanned-leather · serge · satin
- **Rule:** Use smooth leather for the panels of a signature shoe, with patent, suede and mesh for the colourway. A foam and rubber sole carries an air unit.

## Construction

- **Methods:** cut-and-sew leather panels · cupsole stitched or bonded to the upper · air unit set in the heel · foam midsole moulded · perforated toe box · padded tongue and collar
- **Closures:** Flat laces through 7 to 8 eyelets, with a padded tongue and a wing at the ankle.
- **Details:** A large side mark, a toe cap, a perforated toe box, an air unit, a padded collar, a woven tongue label and a thick midsole.

## Surface & finish

- **Finishes:** patent-lacquered · matte · burnished · distressed · screen-printed · embroidered
- **Pattern:** Colour blocked in three colours; a printed or an embroidered mark; a patent or a mesh trim.
- **Gloss:** Leather at 20–50 GU; patent at 80–95 GU; suede at 2–5 GU.
- **Wear:** The style wants a clean pair, white sole and unmarked leather; a worn pair is for wearing, and a collected pair stays in the box.

## Making

- **Processes:** design of a colourway · cutting leather panels · moulding the sole and the air unit · stitching and bonding in a factory · limited release

### Air cushioning (technology)

A sealed bag of gas in the midsole of a shoe, which gives a spring under the heel without a heavy foam. Origin: fashion, Beaverton, Oregon, c. 1977.

- Seal a bag of gas in a film of polyurethane.
- Set the bag in the heel of the foam midsole.
- Show the bag in a window of the midsole.

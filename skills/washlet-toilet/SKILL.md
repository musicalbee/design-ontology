# Washlet toilet

> A toilet is a clean machine for the body, so the ceramic stays smooth and the seat adds water, heat and control.

A vitreous china WC with a heated seat, a warm-water spray nozzle and a control panel. The ceramic is plain and smooth, and the technology lives in the seat and the cistern.

**Period:** 1980 to now · **Era:** Plural and contemporary · **Domain:** Ceramics · **Also called:** Electronic bidet toilet, Warm-water toilet seat, Japanese smart toilet

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting fired clay: tableware, teaware, kitchen and storage ware, vases and display vessels, figures, tiles, architectural ceramics and sanitary and technical ware in Washlet toilet, you MUST:

1. Cast a smooth bowl with no rim shelf, so that dirt cannot stay under the edge.
2. Glaze the bowl with a hard, dense glaze that has a surface roughness below 0.1 micrometre.
3. Fit a seat that is heated to about 36 °C, with a lid that closes slowly.
4. Hide the nozzle in a cover and wash it with water before and after each use.
5. Place the control panel on the side or on a wall remote, with 4 to 8 simple icons.
6. Use a tornado flush of 4 to 5 litres, so that the bowl cleans with little water.
7. Keep the form compact, 650 to 700 mm long, with one body and one colour.

## Guardrails

Each line below breaks the style. Check your output against the list before you return it.

- **NEVER:** A bowl with a hidden rim shelf that holds dirt.
- **NEVER:** A seat with a loud pattern or a coloured plastic.
- **NEVER:** A thick, rough glaze that marks.
- **NEVER:** A bowl with a cord that hangs on the floor.
- **NEVER:** An ornate lid with a moulded relief.

## Form & profile

- **Shape:** A one-piece or two-piece WC with a smooth, long bowl, a slim cistern and an electronic seat on top.
- **Profile:** A rounded, elongated bowl with a continuous, smooth outer skin that sweeps into the base.
- **Foot and rim:** A closed, rimless or hidden rim, a broad base with a hidden trap and a seat hinge in the back.
- **Proportion:** Bowl 650 to 700 mm long and 360 to 400 mm wide; wall 8 to 12 mm; seat height 400 to 430 mm.
- **Rules:**
  - Keep every face of the bowl smooth, with a radius over 20 mm at the edges.
  - Hide the cords and the pipes in the base.
  - Choose white or ivory for the glaze, so that dirt shows and is cleaned.
  - Put the electronics in the seat, so that the ceramic stays pure.

## Clay bodies

- **Primary:** vitreous-china
- **Secondary:** plastic · stainless-steel · technical-porcelain
- **Avoid:** earthenware · terracotta · fireclay · bone-china
- **Rule:** Use a dense white vitreous body with almost no water absorption. The seat and the nozzle are plastic and stainless steel, not ceramic.

## Forming

- **Methods:** pressure casting in resin moulds · fettling of the trap by robot · spray application of glaze · hand finishing of the trap · assembly of seat and nozzle
- **Joins:** The bowl and the cistern are separate casts joined with seals and bolts; the electronic seat is fixed with two bolts and a plug.
- **Details:** A smooth foot with no visible bolt, a hidden trap, and a small, flat plate for the water inlet.

## Glaze & decoration

- **Glazes:** sanitary-glaze · coloured-gloss
- **Decorations:** undecorated
- **Pattern:** No pattern. White or ivory covers the whole piece, and the controls carry the only marks.
- **Gloss:** High gloss, about 90 to 100 GU, with a very smooth, dense surface.
- **Wear:** Little wear: the hard glaze keeps its gloss, and the seat and nozzle are the parts that age.

### Warm-water bidet seat (technology)

A toilet seat with a heater and a hidden nozzle that sprays warm water. A small pump and a water heater inside the seat replace a separate bidet. Origin: ceramics, Japan, 1980.

- Heat the seat to about 36 °C with an electric element.
- Store a small volume of water and heat it to about 38 °C when needed.
- Hide the nozzle in a cover and rinse it with clean water before and after each use.
- Place the controls on the side of the seat or on a wall remote.

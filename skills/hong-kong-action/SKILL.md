# Hong Kong action

> An action scene is a piece of choreography shot and cut for its rhythm, so the stunt is shown whole and the cut gives it speed and weight.

Action built in the cut and in the body: slow motion, wide anamorphic frames and a stunt shot in full view. Gunfights are choreographed like dance, and fights are made of clear, fast phrases.

**Period:** 1984–1997 · **Era:** Tape, cable and the blockbuster · **Domain:** Film & Video · **Also called:** Heroic bloodshed, Gun fu, Kung fu comedy action

Good for: action trailers and title sequences, music video with a fight scene, game cinematics, video prompts for a duel or a chase.

Do not use it when:

- The piece cannot show the whole body, or has no space for a fight.

## System directives

When planning, shooting, prompting, editing or grading moving images in Hong Kong action, you MUST:

1. Shoot the stunt in a wide or medium shot that shows the whole body, and keep the performer's head and feet in frame for 60% or more of the fight.
2. Cut at an average shot length of 1.5 to 3 s and build a fight from 4 to 8 short phrases of 2 to 5 s each.
3. Use slow motion at 48 to 96 fps for 1 to 3 s at the peak of a leap, a fall or a shot, then return to 24 fps.
4. Frame in 2.35:1 anamorphic with a 28mm to 50mm lens, and keep the camera at 0.5 to 1.5 m, moving at 1 to 3 m/s in a push, a whip or a spin.
5. Light with hard, coloured sources: a blue-green or sodium key at 4:1, with a smoke haze and neon in the back.
6. Do the dangerous stunt on set, with wire work or a real fall, and repeat the strike from two angles at 0.3 to 0.5 s apart.
7. Score with a synthesizer and a guitar theme, and add a punch, a whoosh and a shell casing to every hit.

## Guardrails

Each line below breaks the style. Check your output against the list before you return it.

- **NEVER:** A static, wide master shot of a fight with no cut.
- **NEVER:** A shaky, blurred close-up that hides the stunt.
- **NEVER:** A stunt double hidden by an edit.
- **NEVER:** A fight with no rhythm or slow-motion peak.
- **NEVER:** A realistic, quiet sound track with no foley.
- **NEVER:** A single, drab, desaturated grade.
- **NEVER:** A hero with no code of honour.

## Composition

- **Framing:** A wide 2.35:1 frame in which the whole body and the weapon are readable; the fighters are on the lines of the frame.
- **Staging in depth:** 3 planes: a foreground edge or a falling object, the fighters in the middle and a neon or a smoky back wall.
- **Subject placement:** Two fighters on opposite thirds; a hero in a long coat or a suit, in the centre third in the pause before a draw.

**Principles**

| Principle | Rule | Value |
|---|---|---|
| rhythm | Build the fight from short phrases of strike and reply, and make the slow-motion peak the longest beat. | 1.5 to 3 s average shot, 2 to 5 s per phrase |
| leading-lines | Aim a strike, a blade or a gun along a diagonal that crosses the 2.35:1 frame. | diagonals of 30° to 60° |
| rule-of-thirds | Put the two fighters on the opposite third lines and leave the centre for the clash. | fighters at 33% and 67% |
| layering-depth | Throw an object, a bottle or a shell casing through the foreground plane to give 3 layers. | 3 planes, foreground object at 1 to 2 m |
| contrast | Set a hard coloured key against smoke and dark to make a clear silhouette. | key to fill 4:1, haze 10% to 20% |

## Camera & movement

- **Movement:** Push in on the draw, whip between fighters, spin 360° around a duel and follow a fall with a drop. Moves are fast and finish on a still beat.
- **Support:** Handheld, dolly, jib and a car or a boat mount; a wire rig for the performer
- **Camera height:** 0.5 to 1.5 m; low for a leap, high for a fall
- **Move speed:** 1 to 3 m/s for a push or a spin; whip pan 180° per second

## Lens & format

- **Aspect ratio:** 2.35:1 (anamorphic)
- **Frame rate:** 24 fps; 48 to 96 fps for slow motion
- **Shutter angle:** 180° (1/48 s); 90° for a sharp, strobed strike
- **Focal length:** 28mm to 50mm (35mm format) for the body; 100mm for a gun close-up
- **Aperture:** f/2.8 to f/5.6
- **Resolution:** 35mm negative; scan at 2K or 4K
- **Focus:** A deep focus for a fight and a rack focus from a gun to a face over 0.5 to 1 s.
- **Equipment:** anamorphic lenses · a high-speed 35mm camera · wire and harness rig · squibs and breakaway props

## Light

- **Quality:** Hard and coloured, with haze to show the beams, and a back light to rim the hero.
- **Direction:** From the side and behind, with a neon or a sodium lamp in the frame; a top light for the interrogation.
- **Key to fill ratio:** 4:1 (2 stops)
- **Colour temperature:** Tungsten 3200K with blue gels at 7000K to 9000K and sodium at 2000K
- **Source:** Neon signs, sodium and tungsten with a haze machine

## Colour & grade

- **Treatment:** A saturated, slightly cool night look with blue-green shadows and amber neon; warm skin; clean, crisp blacks.
- **Grade recipe:**
  - Set the black point at 3% and keep deep, clean shadows.
  - Tint the shadows toward blue-green hue 190° at 20% to 30% strength.
  - Warm the highlights and skin to amber hue 35° at 10% strength.
  - Add a contrast curve of +25% and push saturation by +15% on neon and red.
  - Add grain at 10% and a halation of 4% to 6% around lamps.
- **Texture:** Medium grain at 10%, with halation and haze in the highlights

## Editing & rhythm

- **Average shot length:** 1.5 to 3 s in a fight (estimate; check Cinemetrics for each title)
- **Shot length range:** 0.3 s for a hit to 8 s for a long take of a gunfight
- **Cutting logic:** Cut on the impact and on the peak of a move. Show a strike from two angles at 0.3 to 0.5 s apart, and let a slow-motion beat stretch the climax.
- **Transitions:** Hard cut on impact, a speed ramp from 24 to 96 fps, a freeze frame of 1 s and a fast dissolve.
- **Runtime:** 90 to 120 min

## Sound

- **Approach:** A post-dubbed track with large foley: each punch, whoosh, shell and bullet is a clear, loud event.
- **Dialogue:** Dubbed, short and clipped, with a code-of-honour line before each fight.
- **Music:** A synthesizer or a guitar theme with a strong melody, and a strings cue for a death; the music cuts at the strike.
- **Loudness:** −20 LUFS integrated, with peaks at −2 dBFS for effects

## Titles & graphics

- **Treatment:** Bold titles that flash or slide on a freeze frame or a night skyline; credits roll over outtakes of the stunts.
- **Type:** A bold gold or red sans-serif or a brush script in Chinese and English, 2 to 4 s on screen.

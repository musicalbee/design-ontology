# Smartwatch

> A watch is the screen on the wrist. It shows the time, messages and health data. The user changes the face as often as a shirt.

A watch that is a small touch screen computer. It has a rounded case in aluminium or steel, a glass screen, a digital crown and a soft strap. The Apple Watch of 2015 set the type.

**Period:** 2015–now · **Era:** Contemporary · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Wrist computer, Connected watch, Fitness watch

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Smartwatch, you MUST:

1. Cover the face with a glass touch screen that shows the time as a hands dial or as digits.
2. Make the case 38 to 49 mm tall and 9 to 12 mm thick, with rounded corners and a back that holds the sensors.
3. Add a digital crown and one or two buttons that scroll and select.
4. Let the user change the watch face and the strap, so that one case serves many looks.
5. Fit a quick-change strap with a slot, a pin or a lug in the case.
6. Fit sensors on the back for heart rate and blood oxygen, and show the results on the screen.
7. Make the case in aluminium for the base model and in steel, titanium or ceramic for the upper models.
8. Charge the watch each day or two on a magnetic puck.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A rounded rectangle with a glass screen from edge to edge, a digital crown on the right and a soft strap.
- **Line:** A smooth edge with no bezel, a curved glass and a thin black border round the screen.
- **Fit:** The back of the case sits on the skin and the strap holds it close for the sensors.
- **Proportion:** Case thickness to height is about 1 to 4. The screen fills about 85 per cent of the front. The strap is 20 to 24 mm wide.
- **Rules:**
  - Make the screen the face, with no markers outside the glass.
  - Round the corners with a large radius and keep the case edge clean.
  - Put the crown and the button on one side only.
  - Make the strap fit in the case with no visible gap.

### Capacitive touchscreen (technology)

A glass screen with a grid of electrodes that senses the small current that a finger draws, so that a touch with no force selects an item. Origin: industrial design, Malvern, United Kingdom, 1965.

- Lay a grid of transparent electrodes under the glass.
- Read the change of charge at each point of the grid.
- Map each touch to a screen position within 1 mm.

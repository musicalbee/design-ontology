# Breguet classical watch

> A fine watch shows its worth by exact work and a quiet face, so each part is plain, thin and cut by hand.

The plain, exact gold watch that Abraham-Louis Breguet made in Paris from 1780. It has an engine-turned silver dial, blued steel hands with an open tip and a coin-edge case. Its movement set the rule for the dress watch.

**Period:** 1780–1830 · **Era:** Court and guild · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Breguet style, Classical dress watch, Guilloché dress watch

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Breguet classical watch, you MUST:

1. Turn the silver dial on a rose engine in a tight pattern of fine lines, checks or barley corns.
2. Print slim Arabic or Roman numerals and keep the minute track thin.
3. Blue the steel hands in a flame and cut each tip as an open ring, the Breguet hand.
4. Cut a fine coin edge on the case band, so that the thumb finds the edge.
5. Keep the case thin and round: the bow, the pendant and the crown carry no extra ornament.
6. Put the small seconds dial off centre, at 6 or 10 o'clock, and leave the middle of the dial clear.
7. Add a secret signature near the 12 that shows only in raking light.
8. Bend the end of the hairspring in an overcoil, so that the spring opens and closes in an even circle.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A thin round pocket watch, about 45 to 50 mm wide and 8 to 12 mm thick. It has a small round bow on a plain pendant.
- **Line:** A clean circle with a slim fluted band; no part rises far above the glass.
- **Fit:** The watch rides in the waistcoat pocket on a chain, and the thin case lies flat against the body.
- **Proportion:** Case thickness to width is about 1 to 5. The dial fills about 85 per cent of the case width. The minute hand reaches the minute track.
- **Rules:**
  - Keep the bezel narrow so that the dial fills the face.
  - Set the hour hand at the inner ring of numerals and the minute hand at the outer track.
  - Make the bow small and round, with no flat shoulder.
  - Leave the middle of the dial free of sub dials and printed words.

## Techniques

These techniques started in one domain and moved into this style. Apply the rules as they are written; the full record is in [`TECHNIQUES.md`](../TECHNIQUES.md).

### Engine turning (skill)

A lathe with a cutting tool cuts a regular pattern of fine lines in a flat metal face. The pattern catches the light and shows no tool marks. Origin: fashion, Europe, c. 1600.

- Fix the metal blank on the rose engine, and set the cam that gives the pattern.
- Cut each line at a depth of 0.05 to 0.1 mm, and keep the pitch the same.
- Polish the cut lines, or fill them with clear enamel.

### Tourbillon (technology)

A rotating cage that holds the balance and the escapement and turns once each minute, so that gravity acts on the balance in every position. Origin: fashion, Paris, France, 1801.

- Mount the balance wheel, the hairspring and the escapement in one light cage.
- Turn the cage once each minute with the fourth wheel.
- Keep the cage mass under 0.3 g, so that it does not drain the mainspring.

# Stealth luxury accessory

> True luxury has no mark: the quality of the material and the work is the only sign.

Plain, unmarked accessories of the finest leather and cashmere: a tote with no logo, a slim wallet, a raw-edged scarf. Only those who know the cloth, the cut and the stitch can read the price.

**Period:** 2006–now · **Era:** Contemporary · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Quiet luxury accessory, Unmarked fine leather goods

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Stealth luxury accessory, you MUST:

1. Remove every logo, plate and print from the outside.
2. Choose the best hide or the finest cashmere that the budget allows.
3. Cut a simple shape with 1 or 2 seams.
4. Keep the palette to 4 to 6 neutral tones: camel, oatmeal, charcoal, navy and black.
5. Stitch by hand at 8 to 10 stitches to the cm, in a thread that matches the leather.
6. Leave the edge raw, folded or painted by hand, and not covered by a trim.
7. Use hardware in plain brass or steel with a matte finish and no engraving.
8. Make the item to last 20 years, and offer repair.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A plain, soft or lightly structured form with one or two seams and no surface detail.
- **Line:** A clean edge and a calm, level top line.
- **Fit:** The piece is carried in the hand or on the shoulder and is worn with plain clothes; it does not draw the eye.
- **Proportion:** Tote 35–40 cm wide; slim wallet 10 cm by 8 cm; scarf 70 by 200 cm; belt 3 cm wide.
- **Rules:**
  - Show the material and the stitch and nothing else.
  - Cut the piece in few panels, so that the grain of the hide runs unbroken.
  - Fit one plain buckle or none at all.

## Construction

- **Methods:** hand-cut hide with few seams · hand saddle stitching · raw or hand-painted edges · unlined or lightly lined body · hand-finished cashmere hem · matte hardware cast in solid brass

- **Details:** No outer mark, a small stamp inside, a thread that matches the leather and a fine, flat seam.

## Surface & finish

- **Finishes:** matte · burnished · napped · distressed · woven-leather

- **Wear:** The style wants a patina: leather darkens and softens, cashmere takes a nap, and the maker repairs the piece.

### Saddle stitch (skill)

A hand stitch in which one thread with a needle at each end passes twice through each hole; if one stitch breaks, the seam holds. Origin: fashion, Paris, France, c. 1837.

- Mark the holes at 3 to 4 mm spacing with a stitching chisel.
- Pass the two needles through each hole from opposite sides, and pull each stitch tight.
- Finish the seam with a double stitch back, and burnish the edge.

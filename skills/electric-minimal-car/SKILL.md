# Electric Minimal Car

> An electric car has no engine to show. It is a smooth, quiet shell with a bare cabin, and a screen holds all of the controls.

The quiet electric car. A smooth, closed body with no grille, flush handles and a glass roof. The cabin is bare, in recycled cloth, with one large screen in place of dials.

**Period:** 2012–now · **Era:** Contemporary · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Electric car design, Minimal EV, Software-defined car

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Electric Minimal Car, you MUST:

1. Close the grille, and mark the face by a thin lamp line or a smooth panel.
2. Make the surface smooth, with a drag coefficient below 0.28, and fit flush door handles.
3. Raise a low, wide stance, with large wheels of 19 to 21 inches pushed to the corners.
4. Use a glass roof that extends over the cabin, so that the cabin is full of light.
5. Keep the cabin bare, with one large screen and no physical dials, and a few clean surfaces.
6. Trim the seats in recycled knit, felt or non-leather material, in two or three calm colours.
7. Use thin lamps in a single line, front and rear, and a plain badge.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A smooth, low-drag body with a short bonnet, a long wheelbase, a fast roof and a tail that is cut off or tapered.
- **Line:** One clean line from the nose over the roof to the tail, and a thin, level shoulder line.
- **Fit:** A spacious, flat-floor cabin with a high, light roof of glass; the seats are thin and the dash is low.
- **Proportion:** Length 4.4 to 5.1 m; wheelbase 2.8 to 3.1 m; wheels 19 to 21 inches; short overhangs; drag below 0.28.
- **Rules:**
  - Close the front as a smooth panel, with a thin lamp line.
  - Set flush handles into the body surface.
  - Place one large screen as the only display, and keep other controls out of the dash.
  - Finish the cabin in 2 or 3 calm, matte materials, with no bright trim.

## Techniques

These techniques started in one domain and moved into this style. Apply the rules as they are written; the full record is in [`TECHNIQUES.md`](../TECHNIQUES.md).

### Injection moulding (process)

Hot plastic is pushed into a steel mould under high pressure. The part cools, and the mould opens to release it. Origin: fashion, Newark, United States, 1872.

- Melt the plastic pellets in a heated barrel.
- Press the melt into the closed mould.
- Cool the part, open the mould and push the part out.

### Wind-tunnel testing (tool)

A model or a car stands in a tunnel with a strong air flow. The test shows how the body pushes the air and how much drag it makes. Origin: fashion, London, United Kingdom, 1871.

- Fix the model to the balance in the tunnel.
- Run the fan at a set speed and measure the drag and the lift.
- Change the body form and test again.

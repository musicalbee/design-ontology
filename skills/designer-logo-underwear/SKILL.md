# Designer Logo Underwear

> A basic garment gains value from its maker's name: the plain brief stays plain, and the label on the band carries the brand.

Plain cotton underwear with the name of a fashion designer on the waistband. In 1982 a fashion house put its name on a basic brief, and underwear became a branded, advertised object.

**Period:** 1982–1999 · **Era:** Power and subculture · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Branded waistband underwear, Name-band underwear, Designer basics

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Designer Logo Underwear, you MUST:

1. Keep the garment plain: cotton jersey, a clean cut and a flat seam.
2. Weave the maker's name in large capitals into the elastic waistband, 3 to 4 cm deep.
3. Pick one tone for the band, and set the name in a second tone with high contrast.
4. Cut the brief from jersey with 5 per cent elastane for a close fit.
5. Sell the garment in a plain pack with the name on it and a clean photograph.
6. Extend the same band to boxer shorts, bras and other basics.
7. Keep the colour range small: white, grey, black and navy.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A close, plain brief or short boxer, with a wide, visible waistband that carries the name.
- **Line:** A straight, level band at the hip, and a clean leg line.
- **Fit:** Close and smooth; the elastane gives a steady hold with no wrinkle.
- **Proportion:** Band 3 to 4 cm deep; rise 22 to 28 cm for the brief; boxer leg 10 to 15 cm.
- **Rules:** Weave the name in capitals along the full band. · Keep the band one tone and the letters a high-contrast tone. · Cut the garment in plain jersey with no print. · Bind the leg with a narrow band of the same jersey. · Put a small woven label at the back.

## Fabrics & materials

- **Primary:** jersey · elastic-webbing · jacquard
- **Secondary:** poplin · spandex-jersey · cambric · machine-lace
- **Avoid:** satin · taffeta · coutil · whalebone
- **Rule:** Use plain combed cotton jersey, and weave the name into a jacquard elastic band.

## Construction

- **Methods:** combed cotton jersey with a little elastane · jacquard elastic woven with the name · flat seam at the leg and the side · bound leg opening · woven label at the back · boxer in woven poplin with an elastic waist
- **Closures:** No closure: an elastic waistband; the boxer has a button fly.
- **Details:** The name along the band, a woven label at the back, a small flag at the side seam, and a plain pack.

## Surface & finish

### Branded waistband (motif)

A woven elastic band with the name of a designer in plain capitals; it turns a basic brief into a branded object that shows above the trouser. Origin: fashion, New York, 1982.

- Weave the name of the maker into an elastic band in jacquard.
- Sew the band to the top of the brief or the short.
- Let the name show above the waist of the trouser.

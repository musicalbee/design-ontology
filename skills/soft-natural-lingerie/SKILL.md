# Soft Natural Lingerie

> Underwear should follow the body and not shape it: a soft cup, a thin cloth and a skin tone that shows no line.

The soft underwear of the youth years: a sheer, unwired bra with no pad and no seam, stretch bodysuits and tights. They are cut to follow the natural body and to show no line under the dress.

**Period:** 1964–1979 · **Era:** Youthquake · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** No-bra look, Soft-cup bra, Natural-shape underwear

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Soft Natural Lingerie, you MUST:

1. Cut the cup from one piece of sheer stretch cloth, with no pad and no wire.
2. Match the cloth to the skin tone, so that the garment shows no line under thin dress.
3. Knit the body in nylon tricot or elastane, so that it stretches 50 to 100 per cent and recovers.
4. Keep the seams to a minimum: one at the side, one at the centre, and no bone.
5. Make the garment light: 40 to 120 g/m² for the cup and the band.
6. Fasten with a simple hook at the back or no fastening at all.
7. Name the garment for what it is: a soft bra, a bodysuit, a pair of tights.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** The natural body line: a soft bust, an open waist and a light, smooth hip.
- **Line:** A round, soft line with no point and no ridge; the garment follows the body.
- **Fit:** Light and close; the stretch cloth lies on the skin and moves with it.
- **Proportion:** Cup depth follows the bust; band 4 to 8 cm; bodysuit rise to the waist; leg to the upper thigh.
- **Rules:**
  - Cut the cup from one soft piece with no pad.
  - Use a thin elastic band at the lower edge of the cup.
  - Keep the neck and the back low, to suit a dress with a plunge or a bare back.
  - Cut the bodysuit in one piece with a snap at the crotch.
  - Choose a skin tone, or a pale colour, so that the line is hard to see.

## Construction

- **Methods:** single-piece soft cup · tricot knit with stretch in two ways · narrow elastic band at the lower edge · flat seams at the side · snap closure at the crotch of the bodysuit · tights knitted in one piece
- **Closures:** A single hook at the back, a snap at the crotch of the bodysuit, or no closure on a pull-on garment.
- **Details:** A narrow lace at the neck, a thin clear strap, a small label, and a smooth band with no bone.

## Surface & finish

- **Finishes:** matte · screen-printed · embroidered · synthetic-dyed
- **Pattern:** Plain skin tones and pastels; a small print or a stripe on the tights and the bodysuit.
- **Gloss:** Matte tricot at 4–10 GU; sheer nylon at 15–30 GU.
- **Wear:** The elastane loses its stretch after many washes, and the cloth pills at the seam.

### Raschel warp knitting (technology)

A flat warp-knitting machine that makes open nets and fine elastic meshes, and that could knit the new synthetic fibres at speed. Origin: fashion, Europe, 1859.

- Feed a beam of parallel yarns to a row of latch needles.
- Loop each yarn with its neighbour in a set pattern.
- Take up the mesh in a flat sheet and cut the pieces.

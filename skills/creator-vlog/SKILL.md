# Creator vlog

> A direct address from one person, tightened by the jump cut, builds more trust than a produced film, so the creator's pace is the style.

A person talks to the lens in a room or on the move. Every pause is cut out, and a title, a sound or a cut-away lands every few seconds. The creator is the set, the brand and the grade.

**Period:** 2005–today · **Era:** Platform and creator video · **Domain:** Film & Video · **Also called:** YouTube vlog, Jump-cut vlog, Talking-head vlog

## When to load this skill

Load it when the user asks for: "vlog", "youtube vlog", "jump cut", "talking head", "creator video", "day in the life", "selfie video", "vlog style".

Good for: creator and influencer channels, brand explainers with a person, product reviews and unboxing, video prompts for a day-in-the-life clip.

Do not use it when:
- The brief needs a cinematic mood with no direct address.
- The speaker has no personality to carry 8 to 15 min.

## System directives

When planning, shooting, prompting, editing or grading moving images in Creator vlog, you MUST:

1. Frame the face at arm's length, 0.4 to 0.7 m, with a 16mm to 24mm lens (35mm format), in 16:9 at 24 or 30 fps, 180° shutter.
2. Hook inside the first 5 to 10 s with the promise of the video, and show the payoff before 30 s.
3. Cut out each pause longer than 0.3 s; keep an average shot length of 3 to 5 s with a jump cut or a punch-in of 10% to 20%.
4. Add a cut-away or a b-roll shot of 1 to 3 s every 6 to 10 s, and a text or a sound effect on each change of topic.
5. Light the face with a soft key at 45° and 5600K, at a key-to-fill ratio of 2:1, with a window or a panel at 1 m.
6. Record the voice at 0.3 m with a lavalier or a shotgun, mix at −14 LUFS integrated, and sit the music bed 18 to 24 dB below the voice.
7. Grade for warm, clean skin: saturation +10%, a gentle S-curve, a white balance of 5300K to 5600K.

## Guardrails

Each line below breaks the style. Check your output against the list before you return it.

- **NEVER:** Long pauses and filler words left in.
- **NEVER:** A tripod shot with a dead static frame for 60 s.
- **NEVER:** A cinematic letterbox with a heavy teal grade.
- **NEVER:** A voice recorded on the camera microphone at 3 m.
- **NEVER:** A slow intro with a logo before the hook.
- **NEVER:** Music louder than the voice.
- **NEVER:** A script read from a teleprompter with no eye contact.

## Composition

- **Framing:** A close medium shot in 16:9, the face at 35% to 50% of the frame height, a wide-angle edge that bends the room.
- **Staging in depth:** The face at 0.5 m, the room at 1 to 3 m behind it; the background is a lived-in room, not a set.
- **Subject placement:** Eyes on the upper third line, centred or off centre by up to 15%; the lens is the second person in the room.

## Camera & movement

- **Movement:** Handheld at arm's length or on a gimbal grip; the camera walks with the creator. Cut-aways are static or on a slow slider.
- **Support:** Handheld, selfie grip, small gimbal
- **Camera height:** At the eye or slightly above, 1.5 to 1.8 m
- **Move speed:** Walking pace, 1.0 to 1.5 m/s; a punch-in of 10% to 20% in 0.2 s on the edit

## Lens & format

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (1920 × 1080 or 3840 × 2160 px)
- **Frame rate:** 24 or 30 fps
- **Shutter angle:** 180° (1/48 s at 24 fps; 1/60 s at 30 fps)
- **Focal length:** 16mm to 24mm (35mm format)
- **Aperture:** f/2.0 to f/2.8
- **Resolution:** 4K capture, 1080p or 4K delivery
- **Focus:** Face-tracking autofocus on the eye; the background falls off softly at f/2.0.
- **Equipment:** compact vlog camera or phone · clip-on lavalier microphone · small LED panel

## Light

- **Quality:** Soft: a window or an LED panel with a diffuser, and the room lamps as a warm practical.
- **Direction:** Key from 45° to the side and slightly above the eye line; a lamp in the background for depth.
- **Key to fill ratio:** 2:1 (1 stop)
- **Colour temperature:** 5600K key; 3200K practical in the background
- **Source:** Window, LED panel and room lamps

## Colour & grade

- **Treatment:** Clean, bright, warm skin. The grade is light and consistent from video to video, so the channel has one look.
- **Grade recipe:**
  - Set the black point at 3% and the white point at 97%; the image is bright and open.
  - Add a gentle S-curve of +10% contrast and keep skin at 60% to 75% luma.
  - Set white balance at 5300K to 5600K and nudge tint by +3 toward magenta for skin.
  - Raise saturation by +10% and lift the shadows by 4%.
  - Sharpen at 15%, add no grain and no vignette.
- **Texture:** Clean digital image, noise under 3%

## Editing & rhythm

- **Average shot length:** 3 to 5 s
- **Shot length range:** 0.5 s for a sound-effect cut to 12 s for a story beat
- **Cutting logic:** Remove each pause and breath; cut on the first word of the next phrase and punch in by 10% to 20% on a second angle.
- **Transitions:** Hard jump cut; a whoosh sound with a 4-frame zoom for a topic change; no dissolve.
- **Runtime:** 8 to 15 min

## Sound

- **Approach:** The voice leads at all times. Add a sound effect on each text or topic change and a music bed that ducks under speech.
- **Dialogue:** Direct address, 150 to 190 words per minute, in a casual register.
- **Music:** A looped instrumental bed at 18 to 24 dB below the voice; a stronger cue for b-roll.
- **Loudness:** −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP true peak

## Titles & graphics

- **Treatment:** A big, simple caption on key words, a lower third for 1.5 to 2 s, and a hand-drawn arrow or an emoji as a joke.
- **Type:** Bold sans-serif at 5% to 8% of frame height, white with a 2 px dark outline; one accent colour.

## Shot primer

- **Opening:** Open on the face, mid-sentence, with the hook line in the first 5 s and no logo.

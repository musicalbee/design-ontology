# Racing chronograph

> A racing chronograph is a stopwatch on the wrist. Its dial shows elapsed time and average speed in one glance, and the case stays slim enough to fit under a cuff.

The watch for the stopwatch era of motor racing. It has a steel case, a dark dial with two or three sub dials, two pushers and a tachymeter scale. The Speedmaster of 1957 and the Carrera of 1963 set the type.

**Period:** 1957–now · **Era:** War and the New Look · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Motorsport chronograph, Tachymeter chronograph, Driver's watch

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Racing chronograph, you MUST:

1. Fit two pushers at 2 and 4 o'clock, so that the driver starts, stops and resets the timer with one hand.
2. Print a tachymeter scale from 60 to 500 on the bezel or on the dial rim. It gives the speed over one measured mile or kilometre.
3. Arrange two or three sub dials in a balanced pattern, each 8 to 10 mm wide, for the running seconds, the minutes and the hours.
4. Use a matt dial in black, grey or white, with high contrast against the hands and the sub dials.
5. Cut the case 38 to 42 mm wide and 12 to 14 mm thick, so that the watch fits under a driving glove.
6. Add a stepped sapphire or domed acrylic crystal that keeps the glare low.
7. Choose a perforated leather strap, so that the wrist stays cool in a car.
8. Use a red or orange seconds hand and one signal colour on the dial.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A round steel case with two pushers, a crown, a bezel with a speed scale and two or three small circles on the dial.
- **Line:** A circle with lugs that curve down, two straight pushers and a thin bezel that carries the scale.
- **Fit:** The case sits flat on the wrist and goes under a cuff or a glove.
- **Proportion:** Case thickness to width about 1 to 3; the sub dials fill about 40 per cent of the dial; the pushers are 6 mm long.
- **Rules:**
  - Place the sub dials at 3, 6 and 9, or at 3 and 9 with a date at 6.
  - Make the sub dial hands thinner than the main hands.
  - Mark the minute track and the tachymeter scale at the outer edge in white or black.
  - Make the pushers and the crown line up with the axis of the case.

### Chronograph complication (technology)

A watch mechanism that starts, stops and resets a timing hand with pushers, apart from the hands that show the time of day. Origin: fashion, Paris, France, 1816.

- Fit a start and stop pusher and a reset pusher to the case.
- Drive a central seconds hand and sub dials for minutes and hours from a clutch.
- Print a tachymeter scale on the bezel to turn a time over a set distance into a speed.

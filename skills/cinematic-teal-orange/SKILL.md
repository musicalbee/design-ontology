# Cinematic teal and orange

> Two opposed hues, the warm skin and the cool environment, give a frame instant depth and a sense of cinema.

A staged, shallow-focus frame with warm orange skin against cool teal shadows, graded like a film still. It is a colour recipe from the digital intermediate, and it is now a default look of films, ads and image prompts.

**Period:** 2000–today · **Era:** Digital and the grade · **Domain:** Photography · **Also called:** Blockbuster grade, Orange and teal

## When to load this skill

Good for: film and trailer stills, automotive and tech campaigns, thumbnails and key art, image prompts for cinematic scenes.

Do not use it when:

- The look is already a cliché for the audience and a fresh grade is wanted.

## System directives

When making, briefing, prompting or grading photographs in Cinematic teal and orange, you MUST:

1. Light with a warm key at 3200 to 3500K and a cool fill or ambient at 5600 to 6500K, at a key-to-fill ratio of 4:1.
2. Shoot at 35 to 85mm (35mm format), f/1.8 to f/2.8, so the subject separates from the background by at least 2 stops of blur.
3. Grade the shadows to teal (hue 185° to 195°) and the skin and highlights to orange (hue 20° to 30°).
4. Reduce the other hues by 20 to 40% in saturation, so that the pair carries the image.
5. Frame at 2.39:1 or 16:9 with a subject on a third line and negative space with a leading line.
6. Add atmosphere: haze, backlight, practical lights and a vignette of −15%.

## Guardrails

Each line below breaks the style. Check your output against the list before you return it.

- **NEVER:** Even, neutral white balance on skin and shadow.
- **NEVER:** Saturated greens, magentas and yellows that compete with the pair.
- **NEVER:** Flat front light and a deep-focus background.
- **NEVER:** Casual snapshot framing with no staging.
- **NEVER:** Teal skin or orange sky from a heavy-handed hue shift.
- **NEVER:** A uniform tint over the whole frame, with no separation.

## Composition

- **Framing:** A wide 2.39:1 or 16:9 frame with a controlled foreground and background.
- **Viewpoint:** Eye level or slightly low, at 2 to 5m, with a lens of 35 to 85mm.
- **Geometry:** Leading lines, a rim light and layered depth: foreground blur, subject, lit background.
- **Subject placement:** On a third line with look space; the subject takes 20–40% of the frame.

**Principles**

| Principle | Rule |
|---|---|
| rule-of-thirds | Put the subject within 0.05 of 0.33 or 0.67 of the width, with look space on the facing side. |

| layering-depth | Use 3 planes: a blurred foreground, a sharp subject and a lit background. |

## Light

- **Quality:** Controlled: a soft or semi-hard key, practicals and a haze that shows beams.
- **Direction:** Three-quarter key from the side, rim light from behind, cool fill from the opposite side.
- **Key to fill ratio:** 4:1 (2 stops)
- **Colour temperature:** Key 3200–3500K (warm); fill and ambient 5600–6500K (cool)
- **Time of day:** Golden hour, blue hour or a lit night set

## Lens & camera

- **Format:** Full-frame or Super 35 sensor, 2.39:1 crop
- **Focal length:** 35mm to 85mm (35mm format)
- **Aperture:** f/1.8 to f/2.8
- **Shutter:** 1/50s at 24 fps (180° shutter); 1/125s for stills
- **Film speed:** ISO 400–800 (native)
- **Focus:** Focus pulled to the eyes; the background falls into blur with oval bokeh from an anamorphic lens if used.
- **Support:** Tripod, gimbal or dolly
- **Equipment:** cinema prime lens · anamorphic adapter · haze machine · LED key and fill

## Colour & grade

- **Treatment:** Complementary split: orange skin and highlights against teal shadows and background, with other hues pulled down.
- **Grade recipe:**
  - Set the black point at 4% and a soft shoulder at 92% in a log or flat base.
  - Add an S-curve of +25% contrast around the midpoint of 18% grey.
  - Push shadows toward teal: hue 190°, saturation +30%, strength 35%.
  - Push highlights and skin toward orange: hue 25°, saturation +20%, strength 30%.
  - Lower the saturation of greens and magentas by −30% and of yellows by −20%.
  - Add a vignette of −15%, a halation glow at 8% and a 2.39:1 crop.
- **Grain:** Fine film-emulation grain, amount 10%, size 20%

## Subject & staging

- **Subjects:** lead actor in portrait · city at night · desert road · car interior · rooftop · industrial set
- **Staging:** The scene is designed: costume, set colour and practical lights are chosen to land in the teal or orange family.
- **Direction:** Posed with intent: a look off camera, a lit profile or a slow walk; the pose reads as a still from a story.

## Post-processing

- **Workflow:**
  - Shoot flat or in log with a neutral white balance.
  - Build a primary grade: exposure, contrast and white point.
  - Add the split: teal in the shadows and orange in the highlights with a hue-versus-hue curve.
  - Use a skin-tone qualifier to keep faces at hue 20°–30°.
  - Add halation, grain and a vignette, then crop to 2.39:1.
- **Output:** A 2.39:1 still or a 16:9 frame in Rec. 709, with a look that holds when it is resized for a poster.

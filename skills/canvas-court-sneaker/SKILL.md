# Canvas court sneaker

> A court shoe is cheap cloth on a cured rubber sole, so it grips the floor and costs little; its plainness is the style.

The cotton canvas shoe on a vulcanised rubber sole. It is a white or navy high-top or low-top with a toe cap and a thin foxing strip. It was first made for basketball in 1917.

**Period:** 1917–present · **Era:** Belle Époque and the Great War · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Plimsoll, Canvas high-top, Gym shoe

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Canvas court sneaker, you MUST:

1. Cut the upper in cotton canvas of 400 to 600 gsm and stitch it to a rubber foxing strip.
2. Cure the sole with the upper in a vulcanising oven, so that the rubber bonds to the canvas.
3. Cap the toe in rubber, and set a round ankle patch on the inside of a high-top.
4. Use 8 pairs of eyelets with a flat cotton lace.
5. Print one small mark on the ankle patch; keep the rest plain.
6. Set the sole at 2 to 3 cm, flat, with a diamond or herringbone tread.
7. Leave the canvas free of coating, so that it can fade and crease.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A light, low-sided shoe with a flat sole, a rubber toe cap and a round toe.
- **Line:** A straight line along the foxing strip; the high-top rises to the ankle bone.
- **Fit:** Loose and flat, held by the lace; the canvas gives and takes the shape of the foot.
- **Proportion:** Sole 2 to 3 cm; high-top shaft 12 cm above the sole; toe cap one quarter of the shoe length.
- **Rules:** Keep the sole flat and white. · Run the foxing strip round the whole sole. · Use a toe cap and an ankle patch only. · Let the canvas crease and darken with wear.

### Rubber vulcanisation (material)

The heating of rubber with sulphur, so that it stays elastic in heat and cold. It made the rubber sole. Origin: fashion, Woburn, Massachusetts, 1839.

- Mix natural rubber with sulphur.
- Heat the mixture to about 140 degrees Celsius.
- Bond the cured sole to the canvas upper in the same oven.

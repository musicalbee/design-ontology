# Dive watch

> A diver's life depends on a clear dial and a sealed case. The watch trades slimness for a dial that is easy to read in the dark and under pressure.

The watch for the diver. It has a steel case sealed for 100 m, a black dial with large luminous markers and a rotating bezel that counts minutes. The strap fits over a wetsuit. Two Swiss models of 1953 set the type.

**Period:** 1953–now · **Era:** War and the New Look · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Diver's watch, Tool diver, Submersible watch

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Dive watch, you MUST:

1. Fit a screw-down crown, a screw-down back and a gasket that seal the case to 100 m or more.
2. Mark the rotating bezel in minutes from 0 to 60. Let it turn only against the clock, so that a knock shortens the time shown.
3. Paint the markers and hands with luminous paint in big, plain shapes on a matt black dial.
4. Make the case 38 to 42 mm wide and 12 to 15 mm thick, with a bezel that a gloved hand can turn.
5. Fit a domed acrylic crystal, so that the crystal does not crack under pressure.
6. Mark the 12 with a triangle or a double marker, so that the diver finds the top at once.
7. Print the depth rating on the dial.
8. Fit a strap that goes over a wetsuit sleeve, as a bracelet with an extension or a rubber strap of 200 to 250 mm.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A thick round steel case with a knurled rotating bezel, a screw-down crown with guards and a bracelet or strap.
- **Line:** A strong circle with a deep band, a bold bezel and short thick lugs.
- **Fit:** The watch sits high on the wrist and the strap lengthens to fit over a wetsuit.
- **Proportion:** Case thickness to width about 1 to 3; the dial fills about 70 per cent of the face; the bezel is about 5 mm wide.
- **Rules:**
  - Make the bezel grip with 60 notches, so that a glove can turn it.
  - Set a large round marker at each hour and a triangle at 12.
  - Make the hour hand wide and the minute hand long, both with luminous fill.
  - Guard the crown with a shoulder on the case so that a knock does not bend the stem.

## Techniques

### Luminous dial (technology)

A dial with hands and marks that carry paint that glows in the dark, so that the wearer reads the time without a lamp. Origin: fashion, United States, 1914.

- Paint the numerals and the hands with a luminous compound.
- Apply a layer of at least 0.2 mm, so that the glow lasts the night.
- Choose tritium or strontium aluminate paint, because radium paint is no longer safe.

### Screw-down crown (technology)

A winding crown that screws onto a tube on the case, so that the crown seals the case against water like a bolt in a gasket. Origin: fashion, Switzerland, 1926.

- Cut a thread on the tube of the case and on the crown.
- Fit a rubber gasket under the crown and press it as the crown screws down.
- Seal the case back with a second gasket and a screw-on ring.

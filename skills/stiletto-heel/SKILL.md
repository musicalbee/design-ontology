# Stiletto heel

> A thin steel-cored heel lifts the heel and lengthens the leg, so the shoe is about line, balance and the arch of the foot.

The court shoe on a thin heel of 7 to 12 cm, held by a steel shank. It has a long, narrow vamp, a pointed toe and a heel no wider than a pencil at the tip.

**Period:** 1953–present · **Era:** War and the New Look · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Spike heel, Pointed-toe court shoe, Stiletto court shoe

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Stiletto heel, you MUST:

1. Set the heel at 7 to 12 cm, and thin it to 1 cm or less at the tip.
2. Core the heel with a steel rod, so that it carries the weight of the body.
3. Fit a steel shank in the arch, so that the sole does not sag.
4. Cut a long, pointed vamp; show the toe cleft or a small toe cover.
5. Set the heel straight under the centre of the heel bone.
6. Cut the throat low so that the instep shows.
7. Cover the shoe in one material and one colour, with a matched heel.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A long, narrow shoe, with a pointed toe and a thin heel that carries the line of the leg.
- **Line:** A single curve from the toe through the arch to the heel; the foot is nearly vertical at the heel.
- **Fit:** Close and tight at the heel and the side, held by a deep heel cup and a firm shank.
- **Proportion:** Heel 7 to 12 cm; heel tip under 1 cm; the toe point about 6 cm beyond the ball of the foot.
- **Rules:** Keep the heel under the centre of the foot. · Cut the throat low and the vamp long. · Cover the heel in the same material as the shoe. · Finish a point with a thin, even edge.

## Fabrics & materials

- **Primary:** kidskin · patent-leather · satin · suede
- **Secondary:** calfskin · stainless-steel · exotic-skin · silk-twill · brocade
- **Avoid:** eva-foam · vulcanised-rubber · canvas · crepe-rubber
- **Rule:** Use fine kid or suede for day, patent or satin for evening, and a steel rod and shank to carry the heel. The rubber top piece is the only rubber part.

## Construction

- **Details:** A pointed toe, a thin steel-cored heel with a rubber top piece, a low throat, a covered heel and a padded insole.

### Steel-cored heel (technology)

A heel with a steel rod inside and a steel shank in the arch. The metal lets a thin heel carry the weight of the body. Origin: fashion, Florence and Paris, c. 1950.

- Cast a steel rod in the heel, 3 to 5 mm in diameter.
- Rivet a steel shank in the arch of the sole.
- Cover the heel and set a rubber top piece at the tip.

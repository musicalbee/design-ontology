# Shapewear and Seamless

> Shaping can be soft: a knit with elastane holds the body without bone and leaves no line under the cloth.

Smooth, bone-free underwear of stretch nylon and elastane: control-top tights, high-waist briefs and bodysuits. They are made seamless or with bonded edges, so that they hold the body and show no line under the dress.

**Period:** 2000–present · **Era:** Contemporary · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Smoothing underwear, Control-top hosiery, Seamless knitwear

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Shapewear and Seamless, you MUST:

1. Knit the body from nylon and elastane, with 15 to 40 per cent elastane for firm hold.
2. Knit the garment in the round, or bond the edges, so that no seam shows.
3. Use zones: firm powernet at the stomach and the thigh, and a lighter knit elsewhere.
4. Raise the waist to the natural waist, and lengthen the leg to the mid-thigh.
5. Cut the edge with a laser or a bonded band, so that it lies flat on the skin.
6. Match the cloth to a wide range of skin tones, in at least 10 shades.
7. Leave out the bone, the busk and the metal hook.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A smooth, continuous body line: a flat stomach, a rounded hip and a defined waist, with no edge in view.
- **Line:** One long line from the waist to the thigh; the edge lies flat.
- **Fit:** Close and firm in the zones that hold, light and open at the rest; the cloth stretches and recovers.
- **Proportion:** Waist at the natural waist; leg to mid-thigh or to the knee; band 3 to 5 cm; garment 120 to 260 g/m².
- **Rules:** Knit the garment in zones of different hold. · Bond or laser-cut each edge so that it lies flat. · Raise the waist to the natural waist for a smooth stomach. · Lengthen the leg so that the thigh has no roll at the edge. · Offer the colour in a range of skin tones.

## Construction

- **Methods:** seamless circular knit · bonded edge with no hem · laser-cut leg opening · zones of powernet at the stomach · gusset of cotton jersey · control-top panel in tights
- **Closures:** No closure on a pull-on garment; a hook-and-eye or a snap at the crotch on a bodysuit.
- **Details:** A bonded edge, a cotton gusset, a wide waistband, a small label at the back, and a zone of firm net.

## Surface & finish

- **Finishes:** matte · synthetic-dyed · screen-printed · heat-set-pleated

- **Wear:** The elastane relaxes after 50 or more washes, and the cloth pills at the edge.

### Raschel warp knitting (technology)

A flat warp-knitting machine that makes open nets and fine elastic meshes, and that could knit the new synthetic fibres at speed. Origin: fashion, Europe, 1859.

- Feed a beam of parallel yarns to a row of latch needles.
- Loop each yarn with its neighbour in a set pattern.
- Take up the mesh in a flat sheet and cut the pieces.

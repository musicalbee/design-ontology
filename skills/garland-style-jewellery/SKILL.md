# Garland Style Jewellery

> A jewel is as light and as white as lace: platinum is so strong that the metal can almost disappear and leave only the stones.

The white jewel of the Edwardian court. It is platinum lace of garlands, bows, laurel and ribbon, set with diamonds and pearls, as light as a thread. The design follows the Louis XVI forms.

**Period:** 1901–1914 · **Era:** Belle Époque and the Great War · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Edwardian jewellery, Guirlande style, Belle epoque platinum jewellery

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Garland Style Jewellery, you MUST:

1. Make the frame in platinum, so that thin claws and fine wire hold the stones with little metal.
2. Draw garlands, bows, laurel wreaths, ribbons and tassels from the Louis XVI ornament.
3. Pierce the metal into lace and edge it with a line of tiny beads, called milgrain.
4. Use white stones: diamonds and pearls, with a single black pearl or sapphire as a contrast.
5. Cut the stones in old cuts and set them in a pattern of 3 to 5 sizes within one piece.
6. Build a tiara or a dog collar from many small links so that it moves like fabric.
7. Make a long sautoir of pearls, 120 to 150 cm, with a tassel at the end.
8. Keep the back of each piece open and thin, so that it stays light on the dress.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A light, pierced jewel in white metal: a garland tiara, a dog collar of pearls, a bow brooch or a long pearl sautoir.
- **Line:** Curved and symmetrical, in bows, swags and wreaths, with fine edges.
- **Fit:** A dog collar sits tight at the neck, a tiara sits on the front of the hair, a bow brooch sits on the bodice.
- **Proportion:** A dog collar is 3 to 6 cm high and as thin as a ribbon. A tiara is 5 to 8 cm high. A sautoir is 120 to 150 cm long.
- **Rules:**
  - Fix a bow of diamonds at the centre of a pearl dog collar.
  - Set a tiara as a row of garlands, with a pearl drop in each loop.
  - Edge each platinum frame with milgrain beads.
  - Wear a long sautoir of pearls with a tassel of diamonds.

### Platinum mounting (process)

A frame of platinum, a metal strong enough to hold stones with thin lines, so that the metal nearly disappears. Origin: fashion, Paris, France, c. 1900.

- Draw the frame with thin, open lines.
- Saw and file the platinum and join the parts with a torch.
- Set each stone in fine claws and add a milgrained edge.

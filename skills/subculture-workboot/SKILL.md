# Subculture workboot

> A work boot on a young foot is a signal of class and group. It is heavy, plain, cheap and hard to damage. The owner wears it to show that he or she belongs.

The lace-up leather workboot turned youth badge. It has a smooth calf shaft, eight to fourteen eyelets, a yellow welt stitch and a thick air-cushioned sole. Skinheads, punks and others wear it.

**Period:** 1960–present · **Era:** Youthquake · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Eight-eye boot, Air-cushioned boot, Skinhead and punk boot

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Subculture workboot, you MUST:

1. Cut the shaft from smooth calf of 2 mm, and lace it with 8 to 14 eyelets.
2. Stitch the welt with a yellow thread that shows against the black sole.
3. Mould the sole in an air-cushioned form 3 to 4 cm thick, with a grooved tread.
4. Add a pull loop at the heel, and a scripted tag on the loop.
5. Add a steel toe cap under the leather, or leave the toe plain.
6. Polish the leather to a hard shine, or scuff it on purpose.
7. Lace the boot in a set pattern; the lace colour marks the group.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A thick boot: a rounded toe, a straight shaft to the ankle or the calf, and a heavy sole.
- **Line:** A strong, flat horizontal line at the sole, and a high, tight shaft.

- **Rules:** Keep the toe round and the sole thick. · Show the yellow welt stitch. · Lace the boot tight and evenly. · Wear the boot with the trouser turned up to show it.

## Techniques

These techniques started in one domain and moved into this style. Apply the rules as they are written; the full record is in [`TECHNIQUES.md`](../TECHNIQUES.md).

### Air-cushioned sole (technology)

A thick rubber sole with sealed air pockets in the heel and the forefoot, which soften the impact of each step. Origin: fashion, Seeshaupt, Germany, 1947.

- Mould the sole with sealed pockets of air.
- Join the sole to the welted upper with a heat seal and a stitch.
- Make the sole 3 to 4 cm thick.

### Goodyear welting (process)

A way to sew a strip of leather, the welt, to the upper and the insole rib, and then to stitch the sole to the welt. Origin: fashion, United States, c. 1869.

- Sew the welt to the upper and to the rib of the insole.
- Fill the gap under the insole with cork.
- Stitch the outsole to the welt, so that a cobbler can replace it.

# Cyberdelia

> The computer is a consciousness machine: use acid colour, fractal repetition and raw early software effects to show a mind that has gone into the network.

The psychedelic side of early cyberculture. It joins cyberpunk with acid colour: fractals, kaleidoscopes, posterised Photoshop collage, early 3D type and rave flyers. It takes its name from the club in the film Hackers (1995).

**Period:** 1988–1996 · **Era:** Postmodern & Early Digital · **Domain:** Internet · **Also called:** Cyber-psychedelia, Cyberdelic, Rave cyberpunk

## When to load this skill

Load it when the user asks for: "cyberdelia", "cyberdelic", "cyber psychedelic", "rave flyer", "fractal", "hackers 1995", "acid colours", "kaleidoscope", "early cyberspace".

Good for: club, festival and music event pages, game and demoscene tributes, art and zine projects, fashion and culture drops, book and album covers.

Do not use it when:
- The audience is conservative, medical or financial.
- The page needs fast reading of dense content.
- The product needs a quiet, neutral tone.

## System directives

When generating web pages, apps, memes, short video frames and other images of internet culture in Cyberdelia, you MUST:

1. Fill the ground with a fractal or kaleidoscope tile that repeats 6 or 8 times around a centre, at 40% to 60% opacity.
2. Set a palette of 4 acid hues (#FF2BD6, #19F0FF, #B6FF1A, #FF8A00) on a deep violet ground (#12002B).
3. Use gradients with hard bands: posterise to 5 to 7 steps, and never blend smoothly.
4. Set display type in extended, outlined or 3D-extruded letters at 72px or larger, with a 4px offset copy in a second hue.
5. Collage cut-out photographs with a 2px white keyline and a posterised colour wash at 60% opacity.
6. Rotate accent elements by 3° to 12° and overlap layers by 16px to 48px.
7. Keep body text on a solid dark block of 90% opacity at 16px with contrast of 7:1 or more.

## Spatial & grid logic

- **Grid mechanics:** A loose 12-column grid that layers break on purpose; blocks of text sit on dark panels over a full-bleed pattern.
- **Whitespace ratio:** 5% to 15% of the surface stays empty; the spacing scale is 8, 16, 32 and 64px.
- **Alignment rules:** Centred hero with a radial focus; text blocks rotate by 3° to 12°; elements overlap by 16px to 48px.
- **Container geometry:** Panels with a 0px radius and a 3px border in a neon hue; circles and starbursts for accents; a 4px offset shadow in a second hue.

## Typography

- **Classifications:** extended display · geometric sans
- **Tracking:** Display +0.02em; body 0em.
- **Leading:** Display 1.0; body 1.55.
- **Hierarchy rules:** Use weights 400 and 700 for text and 400 for display. · Use sizes 14, 18, 40 and 88px. · Set display in a wide face, and mix 2 sizes in one headline. · Put each key word in a second acid hue.

## Colour & lighting

- **Palette structure:** Four acid hues on a deep violet ground, with white as the one neutral.
- **Contrast rules:** Text on deep violet holds 12:1 or more; neon hues carry headlines, borders and shapes, not long text.
- **Surface treatment:** Posterised gradients in 5 to 7 hard bands; fractal and kaleidoscope tiles; a noise grain of 6%.
- **Light and shadow:** Hard offset shadows of 4px in a second hue; a glow of 8px to 16px on neon type; no soft cast shadow.

## Motion & interactivity

- **Born on screen:** yes
- **Transition physics:** Looping and hypnotic: kaleidoscopes rotate 360° in 30s; colour bands cycle at 6s; headlines jump in with a 4px glitch shift for 120ms.
- **Easing:** cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.3, 1)
- **Duration (ms):** 320
- **Hover and active:** Hover shifts the hue by 60° and the offset shadow by 4px; active inverts the panel colours.

- **Icons:** solid, stroke 3 on a 24px field, round caps, round joins, container: none. Use: `<svg class="icon"><use href="pack/icons.svg#home"/></svg>`. An icon takes `currentColor`.
- **Icon family:** the sprite is cut from Solar (Bold) by 480 Design, licence CC BY 4.0. Reason: Heavy solid forms with a thick edge match the loud shapes of a rave flyer. It is an open-source stand-in for a demo. For a product, use the icon library of the brand and choose the family by the rules below.
- **Display type:** weight 400, tracking 0.02em, leading 1, case: uppercase.
- **Weights:** display 400, subhead 400, body 400, bold in text 700, labels and buttons 700. The style uses 2 weights (400, 700) and no other. The display has the weight of the body, so size and face carry the hierarchy. The accent is colour: one coloured word or field is the accent, so the weights stay quiet.
- **Body type:** 16px, leading 1.55.
- **Page:** hero poster, center aligned, compact density, accent tilt -3°.
- **Ground:** swirl. Cyberdelia mixes rave flyer spirals with screen colour in one turning field. Put the class `ground` on the first screen of the page; it takes `--ground`, a stack of gradients built from the colour tokens. The ground can run behind the copy, so put the class `ground-copy` on each text block of that screen: the block gets `--color-bg` and a soft edge. Keep the rest of the page on `--color-bg`.
- **Text colour:** `--color-text` is for the main reading field only. On each field, use its token: `--color-on-bg`, `--color-on-surface`, `--color-on-primary`, `--color-on-accent`.

### How to choose an icon pack

An icon pack is a typeface for pictures. Choose it with the same care as the text face. These rules hold for an open-source set and for a licensed library.

1. **The stroke matches the stem.** Set an icon next to the body text at text size. The line of the icon and the stem of a letter have the same weight.
2. **Terminals and corners follow the radius token.** Butt caps and mitre joins go with a radius of zero. Round caps and round joins go with a soft radius.
3. **The fill mode follows the technology of the era.** Print eras cut solid forms. Screen eras with depth fill and shade the form. Current product interfaces draw an outline.
4. **The metaphors are not younger than the style.** Each picture is an object that the era knew. An icon with a later metaphor breaks the period.
5. **One family, one grid.** All icons come from one family and one variant, drawn on one field. Do not mix families on one surface.
6. **The family covers the product.** List each concept that the product needs before you commit. A family that has no icon for one of them is the wrong family.
7. **The colour is a token.** An icon takes currentColor. A second tone comes from a token, not from the file.
8. **The licence permits the use.** Read the licence of the set. Give credit where the licence asks for it. A product with a brand uses its licensed library and applies the same rules to choose the family.

## Techniques

These techniques started in one domain and moved into this style. Apply the rules as they are written; the full record is in [`TECHNIQUES.md`](../TECHNIQUES.md).

### Airbrush (tool)

A pen-sized spray gun that lays paint in soft, even tones; with masks, it makes hard edges and smooth gradients side by side. Origin: photography, United States, 1879.

- Build tone in 3 to 6 thin passes; each pass adds 20% to 40% of the final density.
- Cut a hard mask for each edge that must stay sharp; leave the light soft.
- Put one specular highlight of pure white on each chrome form.

### Photomontage (montage)

Fragments of photographs, cut and joined on one sheet, so that the joins make a statement that no single photograph makes. Origin: internet, Berlin, 1918.

- Join 3 to 12 photographic fragments on one ground and keep the cut edges hard and visible.
- Vary the scale of the fragments by 4:1 or more, so that the joins read as a claim and not as a scene.
- Set type as one more fragment, turned 0° to 30°, in the same stack of layers.

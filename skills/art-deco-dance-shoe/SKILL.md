# Art Deco dance shoe

> A dancing woman needs a shoe that stays on the foot, so the strap holds the instep and the Deco pattern shows in every step.

The strap shoe of the Jazz Age. It has a T-strap or a bar strap over the instep and a Louis or Cuban heel. It uses satin, kid or metallic leather with a cut-out or beaded pattern.

**Period:** 1920–1939 · **Era:** Interwar modern · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** T-strap dance shoe, Charleston shoe, Jazz Age evening shoe

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Art Deco dance shoe, you MUST:

1. Fasten the shoe with a T-strap or a bar strap over the instep; fix it with a button or a small buckle.
2. Set the heel at 5 to 7 cm, curved in a Louis or a Cuban shape.
3. Round the toe, and cut the vamp low so that the instep shows.
4. Cut a geometric pattern in the vamp: a sunburst, a chevron or a step.
5. Cover the shoe in satin, in kid, or in a metallic leather that catches the light.
6. Match the shoe to the dress in colour; dye the satin to a sample of the cloth.
7. Use a thin leather sole that slides on the floor.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A low, light shoe with a round toe, a strap across the instep and a curved heel.
- **Line:** A graphic line: the strap crosses the vamp at a right angle to the foot.
- **Fit:** Close at the heel and held at the instep by the strap, so that the foot can move freely.
- **Proportion:** Heel 5 to 7 cm; strap width 1 to 2 cm; vamp cut short, about one third of the foot.
- **Rules:**
  - Cross the instep with one strap or two straps, and fix it at the side.
  - Cut the pattern in a repeated geometric form.
  - Keep the sole thin and the heel curved.
  - Match the metal of the buckle to the dress.

## Fabrics & materials

- **Primary:** kidskin · satin · patent-leather · brocade
- **Secondary:** suede · exotic-skin · crepe-de-chine · velvet · vegetable-tanned-leather

## Construction

- **Closures:** A bar strap or a T-strap with a button or a small buckle on the outside.
- **Details:** A cut-out pattern, a rhinestone or a bead trim on the vamp, a metal buckle, a contrast heel and a kid lining.

### T-strap (motif)

A shoe strap in the shape of a T: one strap runs over the instep and one runs down the vamp to the toe. Origin: fashion, Paris and New York, c. 1920.

- Run a vertical strap from the vamp to the instep strap.
- Close the instep strap with a button or a buckle at the side.
- Cut the vamp low so that the T shows.

# Pilot and field watch

> A tool watch must be read at a glance, in poor light and with a glove on. Each part serves legibility and nothing else.

The legible tool watch for the cockpit and the field. It has a steel case, a matt black dial with large white luminous numerals, a large crown for gloves and a long strap. The Hour Angle watch of 1931 and the German observation watches of the 1930s set the type.

**Period:** 1931–now · **Era:** Interwar modern · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Flieger watch, Observation watch, Military field watch

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in Pilot and field watch, you MUST:

1. Print white Arabic numerals on a matt black dial, and mark the 12 with a triangle and two dots.
2. Cut the hands as bold batons or swords filled with luminous paint, and make the minute hand reach the track.
3. Make the crown 7 to 9 mm wide, as an onion or a fluted wheel, so that a gloved hand can wind it.
4. Make a field watch 34 to 38 mm wide, and a pilot's watch 40 to 55 mm wide that goes over a flight sleeve.
5. Fit a soft-iron cage round the movement, so that the magnetic fields of aircraft instruments cannot move the balance.
6. Keep the dial matt, so that the glare stays low in a cockpit.
7. Cut the strap 200 to 250 mm long, so that it goes over a flight jacket, and rivet it to the lugs.
8. Leave the dial free of ornament, logos and extra scales.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A round steel case, small for a field watch and large for a pilot's watch, with a big crown and a plain bezel.
- **Line:** A plain circle with short lugs, a thin bezel and a crown that stands clear of the case.
- **Fit:** The strap wraps the wrist or goes over a sleeve, and the dial faces the user without a tilt.
- **Proportion:** Case thickness to width is about 1 to 3.5. The dial fills about 85 per cent of the case. The numerals are 4 to 6 mm tall.
- **Rules:**
  - Make the dial the largest part of the face and keep the bezel narrow.
  - Set the 12 triangle at the top and keep the other numerals the same size.
  - Make the minute hand longer than the hour hand by at least 3 mm.
  - Leave the crown free of guards so that a glove can grip the crown.

### Luminous dial (technology)

A dial with hands and marks that carry paint that glows in the dark, so that the wearer reads the time without a lamp. Origin: fashion, United States, 1914.

- Paint the numerals and the hands with a luminous compound.
- Apply a layer of at least 0.2 mm, so that the glow lasts the night.
- Choose tritium or strontium aluminate paint, because radium paint is no longer safe.

# It bag

> A single bag can carry the whole fashion of a season, and its scarcity is part of its design.

The handbag that a house makes as the object of one season. It has a new shape in a bold material, a small run and a waiting list. A star carries it, and the street copies it within months.

**Period:** 1997–2010 · **Era:** Power and subculture · **Domain:** Fashion · **Also called:** Waiting-list handbag, Bag of the season

## System directives

When specifying, sourcing or prompting dress and what goes with it: garments, outdoor wear, underwear, shoes, accessories, jewellery, watches and the styling of cars in It bag, you MUST:

1. Give the bag one clear shape that a child can draw: a short strap bag, a saddle, a padlock tote.
2. Name the bag, so that people ask for it by name.
3. Cover it in a bold surface: beads, python skin, patent, studs or a woven weave.
4. Make a small first run, under 5,000 pieces, with a waiting list.
5. Give it to a star in the first month, so that the press shows it.
6. Carry it under the arm or on the shoulder, 20 to 30 cm wide.
7. Add a heavy gilt hardware piece as the second sign.
8. Offer it in a new material or colour each season.

## Silhouette & form

- **Shape:** A compact, easy shape with a short strap, a bold surface and heavy hardware.
- **Line:** A soft, short curve of the strap, and a clear shape on the hip.
- **Fit:** The bag is worn under the arm or on the shoulder, and is seen in profile on the street.
- **Proportion:** Bag 20–35 cm wide, 10–25 cm high; strap drop 12–20 cm; hardware 3–6 cm.
- **Rules:** Make one shape and repeat it in many surfaces. · Use a bold surface at the front and keep the back plain. · Fit a heavy gilt piece: a buckle, a padlock or a chain. · Keep the bag small enough to carry all day.

### Couture beading (skill)

Beads and sequins are sewn by hand on a cloth, one by one or in a chain, in rows that make a dense, glittering surface. Origin: fashion, Paris, France, c. 1924.

- Stretch the satin on a frame and draw the pattern on it.
- Sew the beads with a hooked needle, or a thread of 3 to 5 beads at a time.
- Check the rows against the drawing, so that the lines run even to the millimetre.
